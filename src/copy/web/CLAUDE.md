# copy/web

The Spanish text of what a web service does with a request: `web-reasons.ts` turns the reasons `web-site-chosen`, `web-default-site` and `web-answered` into sentences, and `describeReason` (`src/copy/reasons.ts`) hands them over; `site-problems.ts` has `SITE_PROBLEM_TEXT`, the sentence for each code `siteProblem` gives when the player places a site the engine does not take (`no-web-service`, `name-has-site`), which the card of a table of sites writes under the table (`problemOf` in `src/game/view/cards/table-card.ts`); `web-text.ts` has what the views of the web say: `WEB_BOX_TEXT` (the fields «URL», «Petición HTTP», «Respuesta HTTP» and «curl» of the inspection box, with the notes that a real server and a real browser send more headers, and the sentence of a `curl` that waited and gave up with code 28), `BROWSER_TEXT` (the tab of the browser: its title, the tray «Dirección», the button «Abrir», the name of what it shows, «Lo que ve el navegador», and the three plain-word errors) `SITES_TEXT` (the «Sitios» card) and `URL_STRIP_TEXT` (the strip of the url of a terminal: the group «Dirección», the names of the five parts, the separators `://` and `:`, and the words «sin puerto» and «sin query» for a part a url does not write; they come from the prototype of V28 that the student chose). The problems of a site tag no term and name no code; the url lines of the box tag `port`, `default-port`, `ip-address`, `url-path` and `query-string` only once the player knows them.

- A sentence tags a term only once the player knows it (`known`). Before `host-header`, the request is only «dirigida a» the name it carries; after, «La cabecera Host de la petición indica» that name. The sentence of an answer says the code of the response and nothing about why a response comes back, and it never says that a firewall or a rule lets the reply through.
- The choice of a site says whether the service has a site with that name or answers with its site by default. It never says the server knows the site by the IP or by the CNAME: the name is the one the request carries.
- The lines of a request and a response are the protocol's own text and appear in the box before `http-header` and `status-code` are taught (the exception declared in rule 3 of the chapter 3 spec); the sentences around them never call an HTTP message a packet, say nothing of cookies, redirections or HSTS, and the browser texts imitate no real browser. `web-text.test.ts` pins the words, the term tags and that no text of the web calls a message a packet.
- Not here: the logic that chooses a site or an answer (`src/engine/web/`), what a `curl` prints (`src/copy/tools/`).
