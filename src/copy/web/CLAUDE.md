# copy/web

The Spanish text of what a web service does with a request: `web-reasons.ts` turns the reasons `web-site-chosen`, `web-default-site`, `web-needs-login`, `web-forbidden` and `web-answered` into sentences, and `describeReason` (`src/copy/reasons.ts`) hands them over; `site-problems.ts` has `SITE_PROBLEM_TEXT`, the sentence for each code `siteProblem` gives when the player places a site the engine does not take (`no-web-service`, `name-has-site`), which the card of a table of sites writes under the table (`problemOf` in `src/game/view/cards/table-card.ts`); `web-text.ts` has what the views of the web say: `WEB_BOX_TEXT` (the fields «URL», «Petición HTTP», «Respuesta HTTP», «Código de estado», «Quién lo generó» and «curl» of the inspection box, with the notes that a real server and a real browser send more headers, and the sentence of a `curl` that waited and gave up with code 28), `BROWSER_TEXT` (the tab of the browser: its title, the tray «Dirección», the button «Abrir», the name of what it shows, «Lo que ve el navegador», the three plain-word errors, the tray «Usuario», the button «Iniciar sesión» and «Lo que recuerda»: its title, the sentence «Cada fila es una {{cookie}}.», the columns «sitio», «nombre» and «valor» and the text of an empty table; they come from the prototype of V29 that the student chose) `SITES_TEXT` (the «Sitios» card) and `URL_STRIP_TEXT` (the strip of the url of a terminal: the group «Dirección», the names of the five parts, the separators `://` and `:`, and the words «sin puerto» and «sin query» for a part a url does not write; they come from the prototype of V28 that the student chose). «Código de estado» (behind `status-code`) writes the code with the term that names it (`status-200`, `status-404`, `status-400`, `status-401`, `status-403`, through `statusCodeName`, which writes the number alone while that term is unknown) and its class in one line («Clase 2xx: la petición se cumplió.», «Clase 4xx: la petición, tal como llegó, no se puede cumplir.», the `4xx` tagged `client-error`); «Quién lo generó» says «La app de» and the name of the device whose app answered. The problems of a site tag no term and name no code; the url lines of the box tag `port`, `default-port`, `ip-address`, `url-path` and `query-string` only once the player knows them.

- A sentence tags a term only once the player knows it (`known`). Before `host-header`, the request is only «dirigida a» the name it carries; after, «La cabecera Host de la petición indica» that name. The sentence of an answer says the code of the response and nothing about why a response comes back, and it never says that a firewall or a rule lets the reply through. Before `status-code` it says «de código 404»; after, «con el código de estado» and the code behind its own term, when that is known too. It says nothing of who is at fault: a 4xx seems an error of the client and is not always one.
- The reason of a 401 speaks of who asks («no sabe quién pide /admin: la petición no trae una credencial válida, y sin ella no puede hacer la autenticación») and the one of a 403 of what is allowed («sabe quién pide /admin: es marta. Pero la autorización de /admin no deja entrar a marta: no tiene permiso»); each tags its term (`authentication`, `authorization`) once the player knows it, and neither says the password is wrong or that the code is temporary. They come from the steps `web-needs-login` and `web-forbidden` that the app stage writes between the site chosen and `web-answered`.
- The choice of a site says whether the service has a site with that name or answers with its site by default. It never says the server knows the site by the IP or by the CNAME: the name is the one the request carries.
- The lines of a request and a response are the protocol's own text and appear in the box before `http-header` and `status-code` are taught (the exception declared in rule 3 of the chapter 3 spec); the sentences around them never call an HTTP message a packet, and the browser texts imitate no real browser; the terms of what a browser remembers are tagged only where the table says them (`cookie`), and redirections and HSTS are for later blocks. `web-text.test.ts` pins the words, the term tags and that no text of the web calls a message a packet.
- Not here: the logic that chooses a site or an answer (`src/engine/web/`), what a `curl` prints (`src/copy/tools/`).
