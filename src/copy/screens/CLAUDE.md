# copy/screens

The Spanish texts of the screens, as named constants and formatters, one file per screen group: the roadmap, notice and entity catalog screens and the interface texts (`ui.ts`, which also holds `MESSAGE_TEXT`, the word each kind of `Message` wears on its tab and says to a screen reader) and the incident screen (`incident-ui.ts`: the map, the inspection box, Lucía, the task card, the terminal, the network settings and services cards, the tabs of a piece, the tables with the words of the problem of a refused entry (`TABLE_TEXT.problems`, one for each code of `RECORD_PROBLEMS`, `ROUTE_PROBLEMS` and `CHIP_PROBLEMS`), the card that classifies, the clock of the top strip, the case card, the explanation, the result, the names of the buttons of the bar of systems (`SYSTEMS_TEXT`: «Terminal», «Navegador» and «Registro»), the X of a window (`WINDOW_TEXT`: «×», named «Cerrar la ventana»), the Almanaque (`ALMANAC_TEXT`: its button, its tabs «Términos» and «Lecciones» and the sentence of a Lecciones with no lesson) and the registry (`REGISTRY_TEXT`: «Registro (N)», the count of a group and «Ver fila en la tabla de rutas») and the screen).

- `ROLES` and `Role` (in `incident-ui.ts`) are the closed list of the roles a server of the internet can play, and `MAP_TEXT.roles(roles, known?)` and `MAP_TEXT.rolesLine(roles, known?)` say them in words: «resolutor de DNS», «servidor autoritativo», «servidor web», «proxy inverso» and «app, servidor de origen», each tagged with its term once the player knows it and plain before (with no `known`, every term is known). `proxy` and `app` tag `reverse-proxy` and `origin-server` (03-07 registers them), so before that they are plain. Two roles join as «a y b». The list lives here, and not in `src/game/view/roles/` where the roles are derived, because the words and the scene cannot import `@/game`; the closed list is why a role with no words, no mark and no tint does not compile.
- Not here: the term registry, tagged text, engine reason codes and packet labels (`src/copy/`), logic that decides outcomes (`src/engine/`), incident content (`src/levels/`).
- Text follows `.claude/rules/rigorous-plain-copy.md`.
- May import: `@/engine` and other files of `src/copy/` (`@/copy/<file>`).
