# scene/camera

The camera of the 2D map: one transformation of `div.map-stage__sheet`, the common parent of the three layers (the `svg`, the HTML layer and the `svg` of the flying labels), so they move and zoom as one and every label stays on its piece. The map is drawn once, at one unit to the pixel, and the camera moves the finished drawing; nothing here draws on the map. The design is in `docs/specs/2026-10-29-follow-the-packet-shell-layout-b-design.md` («El mapa en B», contract points 2 to 5 and 8).

- `camera-view.ts` is pure: no React, no DOM. A `View` is the zoom (`scale`) and where the top-left corner of the sheet lands (`x`, `y`, in screen pixels from the top-left corner of the gap). `fitView(gap, drawing, limits)` is the fit: the drawing at zoom 1 when it fits the gap, otherwise smaller, never under `limits.min`; centered sideways to a whole pixel when it is narrower than the gap and against the left edge when it is not, and always against the top edge; a gap with no height yet (a stage as tall as its drawing, before the drawing is there) does not limit the height. `zoomAt(view, point, scale, limits)` zooms around a point of the gap, which stays where it is, and keeps the scale between the limits. `viewOfPan` and `panOfView` go between a `View` and the numbers of the library (see below). The `drawing` of these functions is a `Box`: where the drawing stands in the sheet (`frameOf`) and its size.
- `use-camera.ts` is `useCamera({ stage, gap, drawing, limits, controls })`: it makes the library's `Panzoom` on the sheet once the map is drawn, fits the drawing at that moment and each time the stage or the gap changes size (the fit is not kept: a resize of the window fits again), and gives back the `ref` of the sheet. The gap is the one it is given or, with none, the box of the stage read from the DOM at that moment (a stage as tall as its drawing changes size in the same render as the drawing, and the size the hook has in state is one frame late). `controls` is an optional ref to `CameraControls` (`zoomTo(scale)`, which zooms around the center of the gap with no limits, for a test that needs an exact zoom).
- The limits are `ZOOM_LIMITS`, 0.7 to 2. The label font of the map is 12 px, so at 0.7 it is 8.4 px. Looked at on 2026-10-30 with captures of the map at 0.5, 0.6, 0.7 and 0.75: at 0.5 and 0.6 the letters of a name merge, from 0.7 on they read. 0.7 is also the highest minimum that still fits 04-01 whole in the box of 1280×800 (it needs 0.71), the case the spec names. The maximum is 2.
- `@panzoom/panzoom` (4.6.2, `.claude/rules/prefer-established-libraries.md`) writes `transform` and `transform-origin` on the sheet, and `overflow: hidden`, `user-select: none` and `touch-action` on the stage. Its model is `scale(s) translate(x px, y px)` about the center of the element, with `x` and `y` in unscaled pixels; `viewOfPan` and `panOfView` convert it to a `View`. Its own limits are opened wide, because the project's limits live in `zoomAt`. A change goes through `reset` with the three numbers (zoom and pan in one write): `zoom` and `pan` one after the other would write twice. The library writes in an animation frame, so a test waits for the transform. `noBind` is set: nothing here listens to the pointer yet. The library also pans in a timeout at its creation, which would undo a fit made before it: the fit is given as its start values.
- A native scroll of the stage (`focus()` on a label out of the box, a scroll into view) is taken by the camera: the stage's `scroll` event applies the scroll to the view and puts the stage back at 0, so the box is never left scrolled and «fit» undoes everything. It is the interim until the camera brings a piece into view by itself.
- Not here: what is drawn (`src/scene/entities/`, `overlay/`, `traffic/`), the layout of the map (`layout/`), the screen that decides the gap (`src/ui/incident/`), the Spanish text (`src/copy/`).
- Tests: `camera-view.test.ts` (the pure calculations) and `camera.browser.test.tsx` (the alignment of the three layers at zoom 0.5, 1 and 2, the width of the gap, the fit and a native scroll).
