# scene/map2d/marks

What stands on the 2D map once the layout is done, as pure functions: no React, no DOM, no clock, no randomness. The layout (`../layout/`) says where each mark is and gives the boxes of the labels and the icons; this folder decides which stamps are drawn at each mark, where the label of a packet stands, how two shapes on one point are told apart and which boxes overlap. The design is in the spec (`docs/specs/2026-10-12-follow-the-packet-2d-map-design.md`, «Los sellos», «Los paquetes y su etiqueta», «Colisiones»).

- `stamp-fan.ts`: `fanStamps(stamps, marks)`. `stamps` holds the stamps of each place by the name of its station (`StampSource`: an id, whether it is a problem, and its `send` number, which a live flow of the incident does not have); `marks` is `layout.marks`. It returns a `Fan` for every place that has stamps, in the order of the marks, and throws when a place with stamps has no mark. The «+N» of a fan (`MoreStamp`) has its `id` (`more:<station>`, the one `collisions` lists it by), its `count`, the `ids` of every stamp of the place and `hidesProblem`.
- `stampsByPlace(runs, drawn)`, also in `stamp-fan.ts`: the stamps of each place, by the name of its station, which `fanStamps` takes. A run (`StampedRun`: its id, its packet, its trace, whether `hiddenStop` stopped it, the four facts of its stamp and, for a packet the player sent, its `send`) stands at the last station `stationsFor` gives its path, flagged as a problem when `isProblemStamp(stampOf(run))`, with its `send` only if it has one. The runs stand in the order they are given: the stage gives the live flows of the incident first and then the packets the player sent (`Session.sent`, which is already the last send of each combination, in the order of the first send of each), so the fan opens from its low end with the flows and the oldest sends first. A run that starts at a piece the map does not draw (`startsOnMap`) is left out, as it is not drawn. The stage and the sweep of Task M8 use this one function, so the sentinel of J16 is measured on what the map draws.
- `packet-tags.ts`: `placeTags` (where the label of each packet stands on the still map, leaving out the label of a stamp that went into a «+N»), `separateGlyphs` (the shapes of two packets on one point), `legTextAt` (the text of the leg a packet stands in, in plain text: as the packet asks, or, coming back, with the ports swapped) and `changeDistance` (how far along its path the router that translates its ports stands).
- `still-packets.ts`: `stillPackets(runs, layout, fans)`, what the still map draws of its packets (`../traffic/` draws it): the path of each packet, its shapes and its label, put together from the pieces above. Its pieces are exported for the flight of the packets (`../traffic/flight.tsx`, which draws the same shapes and the same paths): `wayOf` (a run's look, stations and path), `seedsOf` (the shapes of a way: role, variant and intruder) and `translationAt` (how far along the path of a run the router that translates its ports stands: the one place that reads `changesAt` against the layout).
- `collisions.ts`: `collisions(layout, fans, tags)`, the pairs of boxes that overlap.
- `reading-order.ts`: `readingOrder(layout, fans)`, the order of the buttons of the map.
- `boxes.ts`: `crowds` and `holds`, the two box tests of the other three, with a noise of 1e-6 px so a float that lands a hair over an edge is not a collision.

## The fan

- A fan opens across the link (along the `axis` of its mark), centered on the mark, with its casillas 25 px apart (`FAN_PITCH`) and a stamp 36 px wide (`STAMP`). When the stamps are no more than the casillas of the mark (at most 3), every stamp is drawn and the fan has as many casillas as stamps, so a lone stamp stands on the mark and is never moved, hidden or counted. The stamps keep the order they were given in, from the low end of the axis (`y` going down, `x` going right) to the high one.
- When they are more, the fan uses all the casillas of the mark: `casillas - 1` stamps and, in the last one, the «+N» that counts the rest, `N = stamps - (casillas - 1)`, never less than 2. A mark of one casilla draws a lone «+N» that counts every stamp. The list of the «+N» (`more.ids`) holds every stamp of the place, the drawn ones too, in the order they were given; `hidden` holds the ones that went into it.
- Who goes into the «+N»: the stamps with no problem before the ones with a problem, and among equals the oldest first. The age is the `send` number, the last send of that combination (the game keeps one stamp per combination, with the number of its last send); a live flow has no `send` and is older than everything the player sent, and among live flows the first one given is the oldest. So a problem stamp stays in sight while any other can go in, and with more problem stamps than casillas the oldest problem stamps go in.
- `hidesProblem` is true when at least one of the stamps that went into the «+N» is a problem stamp; a problem stamp that the fan draws, and is only listed in `ids`, does not count. It is the answer to question J16 (ruling of the student, 2026-10-19): a «+N» that hides a problem shows it with a small dot in the danger colour of the game on its lower right edge, its ring stays ink, and a «+N» that hides only stamps that arrived has no dot. This folder only carries the flag. Task M12 draws the dot (`../entities/stamp.tsx`, with a paper outline so its vermilion stands on paper and not on the ink of the ring), names the button with the words of `MAP_TEXT.moreStampsWithProblem` and exports the specimens of the catalog.
- A place never loses a stamp in silence: a station with stamps and no mark throws.

## The labels of packets

- A label (`PacketTag`) has the path of its packet, the size of its box and, optionally, the id of its stamp and `staysInDevice`. It stands on the middle of its path. If a box is in the way it slides along the path (no farther than it takes: the nearest place where it clears, forward on a tie), and if no place of the path is clear it moves up or down from the path, to the nearest height at which an edge of its box touches an edge of a box in the way, and tries the whole path again at that height. It reaches 66 px up (`TAG_REACH_UP`) and 44 px down (`TAG_REACH_DOWN`), the old three steps of 22 px and two, measured in pixels and to the pixel; the nearest height wins, and up wins when both are as near. Up is toward smaller `y`. The heights tried are the ones where the box meets a box in the way at a vertex of the path or at the place where it meets the side of one, so the answer is the least height that exists and no step in pixels is chosen (Task M8 decided it, ruling A'; it replaces the 22 px steps of the spec and of Task M6). It was checked against a search of every 0.5 px of the path and of the height on 40,000 random paths, straight and slanted, and on every label of the sweep: it never misses a place and never takes more height than the least.
- The obstacles are the boxes the caller lists (the drawn stamps and the «+N», the labels of the pieces, the icons, the names of the groups, whatever else it counts) and the labels already placed, in the order given. The stage grows the box of each stamp and of each «+N» by half a pixel (`STAMP_CLEARANCE` of `still-packets.ts`, `grown` of `boxes.ts`) before it hands it over: a stamp is tilted 8 degrees, so the box the page measures for it is 36.14 px wide, not the 36 of the model, and a label that only touched the model box overlapped the one in the page by 0.07 px (found by the parity run of Task M17 in 02-09 at 1440×900; the sweep checks it with the tilted box). A label that nothing touches does not move. A label that no height clears stays in the middle of its path and says it is not `clear`; `collisions` then lists it.
- The label of a packet whose stamp went into a «+N» is not drawn on the still map (O-8): `placeTags` leaves it out when it is given the `fans` (its third argument) and its text is the line of the list. A label that is left out does not push the others.
- The label of a packet that never leaves its device (`staysInDevice`: its path ends at its own doorstep, `doorstep:<device>`, 62 px from the door, as in a ping to `127.0.0.1` or to its own IP) is left out in the same way when no height clears it (O-10, decision of the student, 2026-10-19): it is not drawn on the still map and its text is shown when the player inspects its stamp, as for a stamp in a «+N». The caller sets the flag from the stations of the packet (the last one starts with `doorstep:`), never from the length of the path or from the label having failed. Every other label must find a clear place: one that does not is a defect, and the sweep of Task M8 fails on it. In the sweep, 46 of the 532 labels are left out for this reason, all of them in 02-03 with every combination sent (14 maps).
- `separateGlyphs`: a shape that falls on the point of one placed before it (1e-6 px) moves 12 px (`GLYPH_STEP`) further along its own path, and again until it stands alone; near the end of the path it moves back instead.

## The packets of the still map

`stillPackets(runs, layout, fans)` takes the runs the stage draws (`StillRun`: a `StampedRun` plus `replied` and the `ports` the game gives a TCP or UDP packet of an office) in the order they stand, the layout and the fans, and gives one `StillPacket` for each run that starts at a piece the map draws (`startsOnMap`): its `path` (`route(graphOf(layout), stationsFor(...))`), how its run ends (`end`, from `flowLook`), its `glyphs` and, for a packet with ports, its `label`.

- The shapes (`StillGlyph`: role, variant, whether it is an intruder's, point and box of `GLYPH_BOX`, 40 px): the shape of the protocol halfway along the path (`going`); a packet that was answered or that a router or a host warned wears near its start (`answer`) the ring of its protocol, or the warning that came back, at `REPLY_MARK_DISTANCE` (24 px) from the start or a quarter of the way along a path shorter than four of them (`min(24, length / 4)`); the warning that the sender's own device generates has no packet to come back and no shape halfway: it stands at the end of the path (`appears`). An intruder keeps its second border on the shape and the ring, and a warning is never an intruder's. A shape that falls on the point of another moves 12 px along its path (`separateGlyphs`), the ring and the warning included.
- The label (`StillLabel`): the text, in plain text, of the leg where the packet stands halfway along its path (`legTextAt(legs, changeDistance(path, point of the router that translates), length / 2)`: the second leg when the router lies before the middle and the first when it lies after: in the sweep the packets with two legs are those of 02-08, and only at 360 px, in the snake, is the router before the middle, so 6 of its 42 labels say the public port and the rest the private one), the box the layout computes for that text (`labelWidth`, 20 px tall), `staysInDevice` (the last station of the path starts with `doorstep:`, never the length of the path or the packet having failed) and its `place` (what `placeTags` gives). A packet with no ports has no label and neither has the one that never left its device and got a warning from it (no shape stands halfway). The place is missing, and the text stays, when the stamp of the packet went into a «+N» (O-8) or when the packet never leaves its device and no place is clear (O-10).
- The obstacles of a label are the labels of the pieces, the icons, the names of the groups, the drawn stamps and the «+N», every shape of every packet, its own included, and the four strips outside the drawing (`outsideOf`): the label starts on the middle of its path, where its shape stands, so it moves along the path (or up or down) until it stands clear of the shape and inside the drawing. Without the strips, 78 of the 416 labels of the sweep stood past an edge of the drawing at 360, 390 and 768 px, where the link of the snake runs along the edge (02-01 by 23.5 px, 02-08 by 14.5 on the right and 41.5 on the left, 02-09 by 52.5), and the svg cuts what lies outside it. A label that sits on the shape would hide the one thing the player reads the protocol from. The sweep of Task M8 runs on this function, so what it measures is what the stage draws: with the shapes counted as obstacles and the real text of each label, the same 416 labels find a place, 70 go into a «+N» and 46 stay in their device, as before.
- The router that translates stands on the path of every packet of the registered incidents that has two legs (only 02-08 has any; the sweep fails if a new one does not). When the map does not draw that router `changeDistance` is undefined and the packet wears its first leg all along. No registered incident draws a translating router off the map.

## Collisions

- `collisions` lists, in the order label of a piece, icon, name of a group, stamp and label of a packet, every pair that overlaps. Boxes that only touch do not. Two stamps of one fan overlap by design and are not listed while both lie inside the box of their fan (an overlap of the same stamps that strays from the fan is). A «+N» is a stamp.
- The name of a group is checked when the layout it is given has `captions` (the boxes `captionBoxes(layout.groups)` gives, in `../layout/captions.ts`; `layoutMap` does not hold them, so a caller adds them: `collisions({ ...layout, captions: captionBoxes(layout.groups) }, fans, tags)`). A layout without them lists nothing about names. Pass the same boxes to `placeTags` among its obstacles so a label of a packet keeps clear of a name.

## The reading order

`readingOrder(layout, fans)` is the order the Tab key walks the buttons of the map, the order a person reads it (spec «Orden de lectura»). The pieces go from the top of the map to the bottom and, along one line, from the left to the right, by the center of the icon of each (the box of a name, which hangs to one side or the other, does not move a piece). After each piece come the stamps that stand beside it: the fans whose box is nearest to the icon of that piece (a tie goes to the piece that is first in the order), the fans from the top to the bottom, and each fan in its own order, from the low end of its axis to the high one with its «+N» last. A «+N» is an entry of kind `more` named by its id (`more:<station>`, the one `collisions` lists it by); a map with no piece lists its stamps by fan. `MapLabels` (`../overlay/map-labels.tsx`) walks this order and draws the label of each piece and, through what the stage hands it (`../overlay/map-stamps.tsx`), each stamp and «+N», so the Tab key goes from a piece to the stamps beside it and on to the next piece.

The flows that every incident runs by itself, placed as stamps on the 450 maps of the start states, collide nowhere and none needs a «+N». The sweep of Task M8 (`src/ui/incident/map-sweep.test.ts`) puts there the stamps of the real sends too: the 25 registered incidents in 180 states (the start, the solved world, each rule that lets an intruder in on a filter incident, each case of a diagnose, each combination of chips sent alone, and every combination sent, in the start and the solved world) at the seven widths, 1260 maps, with the stamps of the flows and of the last send of each combination, the problem flag of `isProblemStamp`, the names of the groups and the labels of the packets with ports (532: 70 in a «+N», 416 placed and 46 left out as above). The stamps stand only at `door`, `doorstep`, `router`, `firewall` and some `mid` marks, all of three casillas (637, 245, 238, 161 and 21 places of those maps): no registered incident sends a packet that ends at a place M4 exempts or at a mark of one casilla, so those limits are not exercised by any of them, and a new incident that does is caught by the sweep.

Marks of one casilla (measured on 450 maps: the 25 incidents at the start and solved, in the row, the compact row and the snake at seven widths): `bus-end` holds one in 594 of 594, `mid:bus:*` in 158 of 594, `mid:<stop>-line-end` in 156 of 324, `line-end` in 2 of 450 and other `mid` marks in 2 of 378. A stamp that ends there belongs to a packet that died (nobody has the IP, or a router the map does not draw dropped it), so it is a problem stamp, and any two of them make a lone «+2» that hides problem stamps. No send of a registered incident ends at such a mark; what hides a problem stamp in a «+N» today is plain arithmetic: 02-03 with every combination sent leaves four problem stamps at `firewall:marta-laptop`, which holds three casillas, so the two oldest go into the «+N» (question J16, answered by the ruling above: the «+N» wears the dot, `hidesProblem`). No exception was added; the sentinel of the sweep is that no «+N» hides a problem without showing it.
