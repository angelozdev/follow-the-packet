# scene/map2d/cards

Where the card that opens beside a piece of the 2D map goes, as pure functions: no React, no DOM, no clock. Everything is in pixels from the left and top edges of the map, and everything that is measured comes from the boxes of the layout (`../layout/`), not from a camera. The card itself (HTML) is `src/ui/incident/map-cards/` and the anchor that holds it in the layer is `../overlay/card-anchor.tsx`.

- `placement.ts`: `cardPlacement(piece, others, layout)` says how a card anchors (`CardPlacement`: the point it hangs from, its gap, the places it must keep clear of, and the pieces it may cover when it docks), and the functions that already worked in pixels in `src/scene/layout.ts` pick what is left: `terminalSide` (the side, or the dock), `cardSpan`, `dockOffset`, `cardTop`, `sideHeight`, `dockHeight` and `dockRoom`. The constants pass unchanged: `CARD_WIDTH` 300, `LABEL_REACH` 64, `TITLE_BAR` 56, the gaps of 70 (a terminal) and 110 (a firewall) and the margins. `src/scene/layout.ts` is the 3D one and does not change; the functions here are the same rules in another form (jscpd forbids a copy while both exist).
- The anchor is the middle of the icon of the piece, as the 3D anchor was the point where the piece stands. The gap is measured from it: 70 px is the half of the icon (24), the stamp that stands before it (22 from the edge and 18 of half stamp) and 6 px to spare, so a terminal opens just past the stamp of the door of its piece; 110 px is the wide gap of a firewall, which keeps the bar and its two fans.
- A label is not a point. `LABEL_REACH` reads each `x` it is given as 64 px on both sides, which covered the widest label of the 3D map (a name with its lock), and a label of the 2D map is as wide as its characters: 170 px for 18 of them with the lock, 245 for 32. `reachPoints` gives, for the span of a box, the `x` that `terminalSide` and `dockRoom` need so that 64 px on each side of them cover all of it: one in the middle for a box up to 128 px, and for a wider one several, from 64 px inside its left edge to 64 px inside its right one and never more than 128 px apart. `terminalSide` and `dockRoom` keep their signatures.
- The terminal avoids the labels of every piece, its own included (a name hangs to the left of its icon in the row, above it in the snake, to the right of a server); a firewall avoids the labels of every other piece (its own excluded, which its wide gap clears) and the screen alone decides its side, the one with more room, so when that side is not clear of the labels the card docks and never takes the other side (`screenPicksSide`, Task M17: the card of a firewall hid the labels of the laptops at 1280 and 1440 px in 01-12, 01-13 and 02-05). Stamps are not among what a side avoids, as in the 3D map: in the 2D map the links run from the icons toward the bus, so a card beside a piece covers the fans of stamps that stand on the links under it. The dock covers none: when no side is clear the card docks in the bottom left corner, 6 px from both edges, and `dockRoom` caps its height with the lowest row of any label, icon or fan of stamps it spans (`CardPlacement.pieces` lists them all, from the labels, the icons and the `marks` of the layout).
- Where the drawing stands in the map is the caller's: the row modes keep their natural width and the drawing is centered in its box, under the title bar. The caller adds that shift to `anchor`, to `avoid` and to `pieces` before it calls `terminalSide`, `dockRoom` and `dockOffset`; the tests do the same. The map the dock hangs from is the layer of the drawing, and in a snake taller than its box the bottom left corner is where the lanes and the servers hang, so a docked card finds its 200 px of room (`MIN_DOCK_ROOM`) only if the layer leaves 222 px under the lowest piece (200 px, the margin of 6 and the 16 of `GROUND_REACH`, `ROOM_UNDER_THE_DRAWING`): `MapStage` leaves them under the drawing while a card is open.
- `slot.ts`: `cardSlot(placement, shift, layer, windowHeight)` says where a card sits in the layer (`CardSlot`): the anchor with the shift of the drawing added, the side or the dock (`terminalSide` against the width of the layer, which is what the card has to fit in, and not the width of the drawing that the row modes center inside it), the gap, how far the bottom left corner of the layer is from the anchor (`dockOffset`), how high a card beside it may rise (`cardTop`) and how tall it may be: what the layer gives it (`sideHeight` beside the piece, `dockRoom` or `dockHeight` docked) and never more than the window gives (`sideHeight` of the window height), so its foot can be in view, and never less than a card that can be used. `CardLayer` is the layer: the width of the sheet and its height, which with a card open includes `ROOM_UNDER_THE_DRAWING` (222 px, exported by `placement.ts`) under the drawing.
- Not here: the card (`src/ui/incident/map-cards/`), the anchor that draws it (`../overlay/card-anchor.tsx`), the boxes of the layout (`../layout/`).
- May import: `@/scene/map2d/layout/<file>` and `@/scene/map2d/marks/<file>`. Its tests also read `@/engine`, `@/copy` and `@/testing`.
