# scene/map2d

The 2D (SVG) map of the game, built beside the 3D one until the 3D is deleted. It lives here, and not in `src/scene/`, because that folder has 11 of its 12 files and the flag of Task M9 takes the last one; when `three` goes (Task M24 of `docs/plans/2026-10-12-follow-the-packet-2d-map.md`) its sub-folders move up into `src/scene/` with the same names. The design is in `docs/specs/2026-10-12-follow-the-packet-2d-map-design.md`.

- Sub-folders, each with its own guide: `layout/` (pure functions that decide where everything goes, in pixels), `marks/` (pure functions that decide what stands on the layout: the fan of stamps with its «+N», the labels of packets, the pairs of boxes that overlap and the reading order of the buttons), `cards/` (pure functions that decide where the card beside a piece opens), `entities/` (what is drawn in SVG: the boxes of the groups, the links and the dots of the marks) and `overlay/` (what stands on the HTML layer: the names of the groups). The plan adds `traffic/` and `catalog/` here, one task at a time.
- `map-stage.tsx` is `MapStage`, the map in its box. It measures its sheet with `useElementSize`, draws nothing until the sheet has a width above 0 (so `layoutMap` is never called with 0 or `NaN`, and `NoRoomError`, which only a width that no registered world reaches can raise, is not caught), then lays the map out at that width and draws it at one unit to the pixel: an `svg` (`aria-hidden`, `viewBox` `0 0 <width> <height>` of the layout, `.map-stage__svg`) and the HTML layer (`.html-layer`, the layer that already existed, `overflow: clip`) over the whole sheet. `frame.ts` (`frameOf`) says where the drawing stands in the sheet: centered to a whole pixel when the sheet is wider than the drawing, against the left edge when it is not (below 360 px the drawing keeps 360 px and the stage scrolls sideways), and `TITLE_BAR` (56 px) from the top, under the bar that floats over the map. Whoever puts something on the layer adds that frame to the pixels of the layout (the names of the groups do, and the cards of Task M15 must).
- The box of the stage is the sheet. Above 900 px `.incident--map2d .map-stage` fills `.incident__map` and scrolls down when the drawing is taller than the window (the title bar belongs to `.incident__map`, so it stays); the sheet is at least as tall as the box, so the layer covers the whole box. At 900 px and under the row of the map is `auto` (`.incident--map2d`, set by `incident-screen.tsx` when `MAP_RENDERER` is `2d`) and the stage is as tall as its drawing, `TITLE_BAR` plus `size.height`, so the page scrolls.
- For Task M15: the stage ends at the drawing, with no room under it. A docked card needs 222 px under the lowest piece (`ROOM_UNDER_THE_DRAWING`: 200 px of card, the margin of 6 and the 16 of `GROUND_REACH`); on a phone, where the stage is as tall as the drawing, M15 must add that room under the sheet while a card is open (or always), and the layer has to keep covering it. Above 900 px the stage scrolls, so the room can be added the same way. M9 does not add it, so that nothing is drawn that is not a group, a link or a mark.
- `game-scene.tsx` is `GameScene2D`, what `src/scene/index.ts` chooses when `MAP_RENDERER` (`../renderer.ts`, from the build flag `VITE_MAP_RENDERER`, `3d` unless it is `2d`) says `2d`. It takes the props of the 3D scene and, besides, `known` (the terms the player knows, which `incident-screen.tsx` passes from the session; none known when absent), because the name of a group shows only what the player has met and `GameSceneProps` carries no terms. From the props it draws only `world` and `pieces` so far; the others are for the tasks that draw what they hold.
- The 3D files (`game-scene.tsx`, `map-canvas.tsx`, `road.tsx`, `ink.tsx`, `layout.ts` and the folders `entities/`, `traffic/`, `overlay/` and `catalog/` of `src/scene/`) do not change while this folder exists, except the pure files a task of the plan names.
- Draws what the game computed, like the rest of `src/scene/`: it never decides an outcome.
- Not here: panels and screens (`src/ui/`), the Spanish text (`src/copy/`), anything that moves a packet (`src/engine/`).
- May import: `@/copy`, `@/levels`, `@/design`, only types from `@/engine`, and other files of `src/scene/` (`@/scene/<file>`, `@/scene/map2d/<folder>`).
