# scene/map2d/traffic

The packets of the 2D map, in SVG, in the units of the layout (one unit is one pixel): the trace of each packet and the label it wears. It takes what the pure step of `../marks/still-packets.ts` decided (paths, shapes, labels) and draws it; it decides nothing. Hidden from screen readers by the `aria-hidden` of the svg that holds it (`../map-stage.tsx`): what a screen reader hears of a packet is its stamp and the box of its stamp, which say the same words as its label.

- `still-trace.tsx`: `StillTraffic`, the layer of the still map (`g.map-traffic`), drawn by `MapStage` only when `still` is true, over the pieces and under the HTML layer. It draws a `StillTrace` for each packet (`g.map-trace`, with `data-packet` and `data-end`): the line of its path (`path.map-trace__line`, ink; `--blocked` in the danger colour when a firewall the player knows stopped it; `--lost` dashed when it died on the way) and its shapes (`PacketGlyph`, `g.map-packet` with `data-role` `going`, `answer` or `appears`, and `data-shape`, plus `map-packet--intruder` for an intruder). The shape is `CatalogIcon` of the packet entity of the catalog (`@/scene/entities`, whose `PACKET_ICON_PATH` and warning triangle it reuses by composition: the shape of the protocol, filled to ask and a ring to answer, a second border of the same shape for an intruder, a triangle with `!` for a warning), drawn in a box of 40 px (`GLYPH_BOX`). The stylesheet gives the color (`.map-packet` ink, `--intruder` danger, through `currentColor`) and the width of the stroke (`vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke`, as the pieces), and takes the paper fill the pieces give the catalog icon off a packet (`.map-packet .map-piece__icon .entity-icon { fill: none }`), because the second border of an intruder is a path with no fill of its own and would paint paper over its shape. Over all the traces, in a layer of their own, `g.map-packet-tags` holds the labels (`PacketTag`) of the packets that have a place for one.
- `packet-tag.tsx`: `PacketTag`, the label of a packet with ports: `g.packet-tag` (the class the 3D map's HTML label has, which the end-to-end helpers read) with a `rect` on its box and a `text` in the monospaced font of the map, 12 px, whose `textLength` is the width the layout computes for the text (`labelWidth` less the padding). `textLength` fixes the width of the text whichever font draws the arrow `→`, which the font served for the map does not have: measured in Chromium on 2026-10-19, `getComputedTextLength()` still answers the natural width of the font (84.6 px in `serif`, 92.7 in `sans-serif` for `TCP 51514 → 80`) and the drawn width (`getStartPositionOfChar` to `getEndPositionOfChar`, `getBBox`) is the `textLength` (101 px) in every font. The group holds the text of the label and nothing else (no `title`), so `.packet-tag` reads as the label.
- Tests: `still-trace.test.tsx` and `packet-tag.test.tsx` (jsdom: what is drawn, where, with which classes), `packet-tag.browser.test.tsx` (Chromium: the width in several fonts, the place of the rectangle and the text, the font and colors). The colors, the dashes and the paper fill of the shapes need the stylesheet, so they are checked in `../map-stage.browser.test.tsx`.
- Not here: where a path, a shape or a label stands, and which label is left out (`../marks/still-packets.ts`, `../marks/packet-tags.ts`), the stamps (`../overlay/`), the pieces (`../entities/`), anything that moves a packet (the flight of a packet is built here by a later task of the plan).
- May import: `@/copy`, only types from `@/engine`, the icon of the catalog through `../entities/device.tsx` (`CatalogIcon`) and other files of `src/scene/map2d/`.
