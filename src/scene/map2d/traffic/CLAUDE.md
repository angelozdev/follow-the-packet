# scene/map2d/traffic

The packets of the 2D map, in SVG, in the units of the layout (one unit is one pixel): the trace of each packet, its flight and the label it wears. It takes what the pure step of `../marks/still-packets.ts` decided (paths, shapes, labels) and the plan of `flight-plan.ts` (times) and draws it; it decides no outcome. Hidden from screen readers by the `aria-hidden` of the svg that holds it (`../map-stage.tsx`): what a screen reader hears of a packet is its stamp and the box of its stamp, which say the same words as its label.

- `still-trace.tsx`: `StillTraffic`, the layer of the still map (`g.map-traffic`), drawn by `Traffic` (`traffic.tsx`) only when `still` is true, over the pieces and under the HTML layer. It draws a `StillTrace` for each packet (`g.map-trace`, with `data-packet` and `data-end`): the line of its path (`path.map-trace__line`, ink; `--blocked` in the danger colour when a firewall the player knows stopped it; `--lost` dashed when it died on the way) and its shapes (`PacketGlyph`, `g.map-packet` with `data-role` `going`, `answer` or `appears`, and `data-shape`, plus `map-packet--intruder` for an intruder). The shape is `CatalogIcon` of the packet entity of the catalog (`@/scene/entities`, whose `PACKET_ICON_PATH` and warning triangle it reuses by composition: the shape of the protocol, filled to ask and a ring to answer, a second border of the same shape for an intruder, a triangle with `!` for a warning), drawn in a box of 40 px (`GLYPH_BOX`). The stylesheet gives the color (`.map-packet` ink, `--intruder` danger, through `currentColor`) and the width of the stroke (`vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke`, as the pieces), and takes the paper fill the pieces give the catalog icon off a packet (`.map-packet .map-piece__icon .entity-icon { fill: none }`), because the second border of an intruder is a path with no fill of its own and would paint paper over its shape. Over all the traces, in a layer of their own, `g.map-packet-tags` holds the labels (`PacketTag`) of the packets that have a place for one.
- `packet-tag.tsx`: `PacketTag`, the label of a packet with ports: `g.packet-tag` (the class the 3D map's HTML label has, which the end-to-end helpers read) with a `rect` on its box and a `text` in the monospaced font of the map, 12 px, whose `textLength` is the width the layout computes for the text (`labelWidth` less the padding). `textLength` fixes the width of the text whichever font draws the arrow `→`, which the font served for the map does not have: measured in Chromium on 2026-10-19, `getComputedTextLength()` still answers the natural width of the font (84.6 px in `serif`, 92.7 in `sans-serif` for `TCP 51514 → 80`) and the drawn width (`getStartPositionOfChar` to `getEndPositionOfChar`, `getBBox`) is the `textLength` (101 px) in every font. The group holds the text of the label and nothing else (no `title`), so `.packet-tag` reads as the label.
- `traffic.tsx`: `Traffic`, what `MapStage` draws in its `svg` for the packets: `StillTraffic` when `still` is true and, when it is not, the packets in flight (`g.map-flights`). `FlightLabels` is the other half of the flight: the labels of the packets with ports (`g.map-flight-tags`), for an `svg` of its own that `MapStage` draws after the HTML layer, so the labels fly over the stamps and the labels of the pieces, as the 3D labels do (the spec: «la etiqueta sigue al paquete aunque pase sobre un sello»), while the packets go under them. That `svg` is `aria-hidden`, as large as the drawing and at its place, and `.map-stage__flight-labels` gives it `pointer-events: none` (the `.packet-tag` rule of `primitives.css` gives the same to the labels), so every stamp, button and card under a label stays pressable and hoverable. Both halves have the same clock: two `svg` roots have two SMIL timelines, and measured in Chromium on 2026-10-19 the two that `MapStage` mounts together read the same `getCurrentTime()` (0 µs apart over five frames), so a label stays with its packet; a test that stops the clock stops both (`pauseAnimations()` and `setCurrentTime(t)` on each). Each live flow gets `flowCycles` copies and each send one `sendCycle`; a flow is keyed by its id and its copy and a send by its combination and its send number (`flightKeyOf`), so a new send number mounts a new group. The session keeps one run for each combination of chips, the last one, so a repeat would replace the packet still in flight: `useSendsInFlight` (`use-sends-in-flight.ts`) keeps each send beside the newer ones of its combination until its own trip ends (`plan.duration` after it was first seen, on one timer), as the 3D map keeps one packet for each send (`use-packet-runs.ts` there), and `MapStage` calls it once (`useSendsInTheAir` in `traffic.tsx`: the hook with the layout, and nothing when animations are off, which also makes it forget what it kept) and hands the one list to `Traffic` and to `FlightLabels` as `inTheAir`, so a label stays with its packet and the two can never expire apart (`Traffic` keeps `sent` for the still map, which draws one trace for each combination). It remembers the run a send left with, not its path, which is worked out again at the layout of each render; keeps the flights in the order they first stood, so none moves in the DOM (a moved SMIL element would begin again) when another joins or goes; drops at once the flights of a combination that leaves the sent list (the world changed), and leaves the last send of a combination in the list after its trip, finished and invisible, as before. A packet that starts at a piece the map does not draw is not drawn.
- `flight-plan.ts`: the pure times of a flight, no DOM. `flightPlan(path, end, wait, changesAt)` says, in seconds from the moment a packet leaves, how long it takes to reach the end of the path (`turn`: 120 px per second, `PACKET_SPEED`), how long it is in the air (`travel`: twice the path when it is answered or a router warns), how long it is on the map (`duration`: the travel and the half second of its ending, `PACKET_END_SECONDS`), when each shape shows (`glyphs`: the one going, the ring or warning coming back, the warning of the sender's own device, which does not travel and appears at the end of the path), the windows of the labels (`labels`: the first leg until the router that translates the ports and the last from there, and coming back the reply with the ports swapped, the last leg first) and the ending (`burst` when blocked, `shrink` when lost, `appears`). `distanceAt` and `routeOf` say where the packet is at a second; `motionOf` gives the `keyTimes` and `keyPoints` of its `animateMotion`; `flowCycles` gives a live flow its copies (a packet every `FLOW_PERIOD` 3.5 s, shifted `FLOW_OFFSET` 1.1 s for each flow before it, and `ceil(duration / 3.5)` copies, each with a period of that many periods and shifted by one, when its trip lasts longer than the period); `sendCycle` and `answerBackAt` give a send its cycle and the second where its answer is back at its sender, which is when a send that waits for it may leave; `keyframes` turns the values of an attribute at some seconds into `keyTimes` and `values` that start at 0 and end at 1; `labelRide` gives the points where a label rides, above the packet and inside the drawing, and the `keyTimes` and `keyPoints` that keep it with the packet. The numbers are its own (120, 3.5, 1.1, 0.5: the 3D has them in files behind `three`, which Task M24 deletes).
- `use-sends-in-flight.ts`: `useSendsInFlight(sent, secondsOf)` and `flightKeyOf`, described under `traffic.tsx`; it holds the only timer of the folder (`setTimeout` for the next trip to end, re-armed when the list changes) and no test file of its own (the folder is at its cap): `flight.test.tsx` and `flight.browser.test.tsx` test it through `Traffic` and `FlightLabels`, drawn by a `Stage` component that calls `useSendsInTheAir` once, as `MapStage` does, and `../map-stage.browser.test.tsx` through the real stage.
- `flight.tsx`: `FlightBody` (a packet: a group that one `animateMotion` carries along its route with `keyPoints`, `keyTimes`, `calcMode="linear"` and `repeatCount`, drawn around the point where the group stands, with each shape in a group of opacity 0 that a discrete `animate` shows during its span, `animateTransform` scaling the shape that shrinks and the ring that bursts) and `FlightTags` (the labels: another `animateMotion` over the centers of `labelRide`, with a label for each window, so the one that shows changes at the router). The shapes are `PacketGlyph` and the label `PacketTag`, the ones of the still map.
- SMIL, as measured in Chromium on 2026-10-19 (the Vitest browser tests do it again every run): React 19 passes `keyPoints`, `keyTimes`, `calcMode`, `repeatCount`, `begin`, `dur` and `path` to the DOM as they are (`flight.test.tsx`). A numeric `begin` is read on the timeline of the whole svg, not from the mount of its element: a group mounted when the timeline is at 10 s with `begin="0s"` has already ended. So a live flow keeps its numeric `begin` and `repeatCount="indefinite"` (its phase is that of the timeline, wherever it was mounted) and a send is `begin="indefinite"` with `beginElementAt(wait)` called on each of its animations when its group is mounted (a callback ref that returns a cleanup, which `endElement`s them: `main.tsx` is under StrictMode, which runs the callback, its cleanup and the callback again). The callback is a `useCallback` of the wait, and its identity must stay: when it changes React runs the old cleanup and the new callback and the send would begin again at every re-render of the stage (`flight.test.tsx` and `flight.browser.test.tsx` render a flying send again and see it keep its start). `getStartTime()` of an animation throws `InvalidStateError` once its interval has ended; read it before moving the clock. A browser test stops the clock with `svg.pauseAnimations()` and puts it at an instant with `svg.setCurrentTime(t)`, and `getComputedStyle` of a group shows the opacity a discrete `animate` gives it at that instant.
- Tests: `flight-plan.test.ts` (the times, pure), `flight.test.tsx` (jsdom: what is drawn, with which attributes; the SMIL probe; the sends of one combination with fake timers) and `flight.browser.test.tsx` (Chromium: the packet is where the plan says at an instant, a send starts from zero after the clock has moved on and under StrictMode, a send sent again leaves the first on its way and starts a second, the label changes at the router, the ending of each kind, the label inside the drawing; it mounts both `svg`s as `MapStage` does); the order of the label over a stamp and the stamp under the pointer are in `../map-stage.browser.test.tsx`; `still-trace.test.tsx` and `packet-tag.test.tsx` (jsdom: what is drawn, where, with which classes), `packet-tag.browser.test.tsx` (Chromium: the width in several fonts, the place of the rectangle and the text, the font and colors). The colors, the dashes and the paper fill of the shapes need the stylesheet, so they are checked in `../map-stage.browser.test.tsx`.
- Not here: where a path, a shape or a label stands, and which label is left out (`../marks/still-packets.ts`, `../marks/packet-tags.ts`), the stamps (`../overlay/`), the pieces (`../entities/`), anything that decides whether a packet moves or where it ends (`src/engine/`).
- May import: `@/copy`, only types from `@/engine`, the icon of the catalog through `../entities/device.tsx` (`CatalogIcon`) and other files of `src/scene/map2d/`.
