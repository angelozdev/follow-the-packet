# scene/map2d/overlay

What the 2D map puts on its HTML layer (`.html-layer`, the layer above the svg): the text and the controls the player reads and presses, at the pixels the layout gives. Everything here is plain DOM that a screen reader reads; the art under it is the svg (`../entities/`). Task M11 puts here the names of the groups and the labels of the pieces, Task M12 the stamps and Task M15 the card anchor.

- `group-captions.tsx`: `GroupCaptions`, the name of every group that has something to say, as a `span.caption` at the box `captionBoxes` (`../layout/captions.ts`) keeps for it, so the guarantee of the sweep of Task M8 holds for what is drawn. A name is the parts of `Group.name` whose term the player knows (`shownParts`), joined by a space: «Oficina» for an office (always), «VPC» for a VPC (once `vpc` is known), the name of a floor (always) or of a subnet (once `vpc` is known), and the block `192.168.1.0/24` once `cidr` is known. The words of the offices and the VPCs are `MAP_TEXT.groups` in `src/copy/`, and the layout puts them in `Group.name` (`../layout/arrange/groups.ts`), so the box of the name is as wide as the name can ever be. A group left with no part to show draws nothing. `BlockNames` writes the name of the block (`blockName`, which `incident-screen.tsx` takes from the card that classifies) on the tab of each outline of a block, as a `span.caption.map-tab-name` at the box `blockTabBoxes` keeps for it: the caller draws it only once `cidr` is known, as `MapStage` does.
- `map-labels.tsx`: `MapLabels`, the label of every piece the layout holds a box for. Each is the button the map has always had (`PieceLabel`, `../../overlay/piece-label.tsx`, which this folder reuses and never edits: its name, its lock, its alert, its trigger and its click), inside a `div.map-label` at the pixels of the box of the label with the frame added, in the order the Tab key must walk the map (`readingOrder`, whatever the order of the pieces it is given). The holder says how many lines the label has (`data-lines`, from `labelLines`): the label of a firewall is in two lines, its term and its resource, and the stylesheet breaks the line before the name tag; its width is the box of the layout and its height too, but the button inside has the width of its text, which the browser test of Task M21 compares with the model. Every label of the map is monospaced, 12 px on a line of 16 px, at the width the model counts (`.map-stage .map-button`: the name tag inside it has no border, padding or shade, so its width is its characters), and the font is the token `--font-mono` that `.map-stage` redefines without `ui-monospace` (`tokens.css` does not change). The stamps join the same order: `MapLabels` takes, besides, the `fans` of stamps (none by default) and `stamp`, what draws one stamp or «+N» of the reading order, and puts each right after the label of the piece that stands nearest to its fan (`readingOrder(layout, fans)`), so one walk of the Tab key goes from a piece to the stamps beside it and on to the next piece, and a `MapStamps` placed after `MapLabels` would have walked every label before the first stamp.
- `map-stamps.tsx`: `stampButtons`, which takes the fans, the runs they were made from (`DrawnRun`: a `StampedRun` with its piece id and its label), the frame and two triggers, and returns what `MapLabels` calls for each stamp and «+N» (the plan names a `MapStamps` that draws the whole list; this draws one entry, for the reason above). A stamp is the button `StampButton` (`../entities/stamp.tsx`), named by its packet and how it ended (`stampLabel` of the plain label and `stampOf(run)`), with the trigger of its packet (`inspectTrigger(run.pieceId)`); a «+N» is `StampMore`, with the trigger of its list (`inspectStampsTrigger(more.ids)`: the ids of every packet of the place, which the screen turns into the id of the list with `PIECE_ID.stamps`, because `scene` cannot import `@/game`). Each stands in a `div.map-stamp` at the center of its place in the fan with the frame added (`translate(-50%, -50%)`, so no size of the stamp is written twice). The one under the pointer or with the focus comes to the front (`.map-stamp:hover`, `.map-stamp:focus-within`: `z-index: 1`), and, otherwise, the paint order is the order of the DOM: each stamp covers the edge of the one before it and the «+N», which is last in its fan, is on top.
- `card-anchor.tsx`: `CardAnchor`, the card the screen opens beside its piece, as one `div.map-card` at the middle of the icon of its piece (the layout's `icons` box with the frame added) in the layer. It takes the piece, the others, the layout, the frame, the layer (the sheet's width and its height, with the room under the drawing) and the height of the window, asks `cardPlacement` (`../cards/placement.ts`) how the card anchors and `cardSlot` (`../cards/slot.ts`) where it sits, and writes the answer in the CSS variables the 3D card has always had (`--card-width`, `--card-gap`, `--card-x`, `--card-y`, `--card-top` and `--card-room`), which `.map-card--left`, `.map-card--right` and `.map-card--dock` read in `src/styles.css`. The slot is worked out in each render from the layout it is given, never kept in a state: when the container changes size and the layout changes mode, the card follows its piece to the new place, and it is one element whose class is the only thing that changes with the side, so what the card holds (the picks of a terminal, the tab of a card) never remounts. It draws what it is given: `MapStage` is the one that decides whether a card is open (a piece the layout holds no icon box for opens none). The holder is `.map-card` (`MAP_CARD` in `../../html-layer.ts`), which the inspection box watches; `z-index: 30` puts it over the labels, the stamps and the labels that fly and under the inspection box and the title bar. `use-reveal-card.ts`: `useRevealCard`, which asks the browser to bring the card into view (`scrollIntoView`, `block: 'nearest'`; the card's scroll margins are the title bar and the dock margin) when it mounts and when another piece opens it, before the browser draws, and again, with a `ResizeObserver` on the card and on the layer that holds it, each time either changes size (Task M17: a terminal that grows with its output, or a drawing that grows under a docked card because the traceroute adds routers, took the foot of the card out of reach in a stage that scrolls): a docked card hangs from the bottom of a layer that is taller than the window, and a card beside a piece low in a drawing taller than the window would otherwise open out of sight. A change of size does not record the scroll positions again, so closing the card still puts the stage and the page back where they were when it opened. It scrolls the stage and the page and never the layer, which clips and does not scroll, and when the card goes (or another piece opens) every scroller above it goes back to where it was before the card came into view, so closing a card leaves the map where the player had it.
- The layer keeps the classes the helpers of the browser tests (`src/testing/`), the end-to-end tests and the inspection box read: `.caption` (not `.caption--soft`, which `MAP_MARKS` leaves out), `.map-button`, `button.stamp` (the stamps and «+N») and `.map-card`. The names of the groups are the direct children of the layer and the stylesheet places `.html-layer > .caption`, not every `.caption` under the stage: the card, which lives in the layer, holds `.caption`s of its own (the names of its trays) that must stay in its flow.
- Not here: the svg (`../entities/`), where anything goes (`../layout/`), where a card sits (`../cards/`), which stamps stand at a place (`../marks/`), the card itself (`src/ui/incident/map-cards/`), the Spanish text (`src/copy/`).
- May import: `@/copy`, `@/scene/overlay/piece-label` and `@/scene/overlay/inspect-trigger` (the label button and its trigger, shared with the 3D map), and other files of `src/scene/map2d/` (`@/scene/map2d/<folder>/<file>`).
