# scene/map2d/overlay

What the 2D map puts on its HTML layer (`.html-layer`, the layer above the svg): the text and the controls the player reads and presses, at the pixels the layout gives. Everything here is plain DOM that a screen reader reads; the art under it is the svg (`../entities/`). Task M11 puts here the names of the groups and the labels of the pieces; Tasks M12 to M15 add the stamps and the card anchor.

- `group-captions.tsx`: `GroupCaptions`, the name of every group that has something to say, as a `span.caption` at the box `captionBoxes` (`../layout/captions.ts`) keeps for it, so the guarantee of the sweep of Task M8 holds for what is drawn. A name is the parts of `Group.name` whose term the player knows (`shownParts`), joined by a space: «Oficina» for an office (always), «VPC» for a VPC (once `vpc` is known), the name of a floor (always) or of a subnet (once `vpc` is known), and the block `192.168.1.0/24` once `cidr` is known. The words of the offices and the VPCs are `MAP_TEXT.groups` in `src/copy/`, and the layout puts them in `Group.name` (`../layout/arrange/groups.ts`), so the box of the name is as wide as the name can ever be. A group left with no part to show draws nothing. `BlockNames` writes the name of the block (`blockName`, which `incident-screen.tsx` takes from the card that classifies) on the tab of each outline of a block, as a `span.caption.map-tab-name` at the box `blockTabBoxes` keeps for it: the caller draws it only once `cidr` is known, as `MapStage` does.
- `map-labels.tsx`: `MapLabels`, the label of every piece the layout holds a box for. Each is the button the map has always had (`PieceLabel`, `../../overlay/piece-label.tsx`, which this folder reuses and never edits: its name, its lock, its alert, its trigger and its click), inside a `div.map-label` at the pixels of the box of the label with the frame added, in the order the Tab key must walk the map (`readingOrder`, whatever the order of the pieces it is given). The holder says how many lines the label has (`data-lines`, from `labelLines`): the label of a firewall is in two lines, its term and its resource, and the stylesheet breaks the line before the name tag; its width is the box of the layout and its height too, but the button inside has the width of its text, which the browser test of Task M21 compares with the model. Every label of the map is monospaced, 12 px on a line of 16 px, at the width the model counts (`.map-stage .map-button`: the name tag inside it has no border, padding or shade, so its width is its characters), and the font is the token `--font-mono` that `.map-stage` redefines without `ui-monospace` (`tokens.css` does not change). The stamps (Task M12) will join the same order.
- The layer keeps the classes the helpers of e2e and the inspection box read: `.caption` (not `.caption--soft`, which `MAP_MARKS` leaves out), and, as the tasks add them, `.map-button`, `button.stamp` and `.map-card`.
- Not here: the svg (`../entities/`), where anything goes (`../layout/`), the Spanish text (`src/copy/`).
- May import: `@/copy`, `@/scene/overlay/piece-label` and `@/scene/overlay/inspect-trigger` (the label button and its trigger, shared with the 3D map), and other files of `src/scene/map2d/` (`@/scene/map2d/<folder>/<file>`).
