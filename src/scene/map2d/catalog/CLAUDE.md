# scene/map2d/catalog

The canvas of the `/entidades` page in 2D (Task M16): every entity of the catalog in every variant and state, as the 2D map draws it, in a grid of 5 columns. `src/scene/index.ts` exports it as `CatalogScene`.

- `catalog-cells.tsx` lists the cells, one row per entity or variant, in a fixed order, so the rows keep their place: the networks (an office with a floor inside it, a VPC with a subnet inside it, and the outline of a block with its filled tab; drawn through `GroupBoxes`), the laptop, the printer and the EC2 instance in four states each (normal, locked, selected, alert) with the server of the internet, the host firewall and the Security Group in four states (locked, configurable, selected, blocking), the router, the Internet gateway and the globe, the three actors, the packets (the Echo Request and Echo Reply, then the TCP and UDP ones, each as an intruder's too, and the trace of a warning), the traces (one for each way a packet ends, with the stamp it leaves) and, last, the three «+N» of `MORE_SPECIMENS`. A cell holds its `caption`, its `art` (what goes in the svg, given whether animations are off) and its `html` (the label of a piece, the stamp of a trace or the «+N», which are buttons and stand on the stage like the layer of the map does). Every piece is the one the map draws (`PieceShape`, `GroupBoxes`, `PacketGlyph`, `StillTrace`, `StampButton`, `StampMore`, `PieceLabel`): nothing here redraws a piece, and the states are the ones the game gives them (`selected`, `intruded`, `blocking`, `locked`).
- `catalog-scene.tsx` is `CatalogScene2D`: a `div.catalog__canvas.catalog2d` with a `div.catalog2d__row` per row (a grid of `CATALOG_COLUMNS` columns of `STAGE.width` px, so a row of 3 starts at column 1) and a `figure` per cell. The figure holds the stage (`div.catalog2d__stage`, `aria-hidden` and `inert`: the drawing, the labels and the stamps are only to look at, so a screen reader and the keyboard skip them) and its `figcaption`, which names the figure (`aria-labelledby`) and is the specimen's name: a piece reads «name · state» (`laptop · con candado`), a network and a packet its name as `entityName` writes it (`contorno del bloque`, `TCP pide`, `Echo Request · intruso`), a trace the way the packet ended (`llegó`, `bloqueado`, `intruso`) and a «+N» its count (`CATALOG_TEXT.more`). Below its width the canvas scrolls inside its own box (`.catalog2d`, `overflow-x: auto`) and the page does not.
- Animations: the pieces that move do (`still` is false: the laptop under attack shakes and a selected bar pulses) and with them off they wear their still mark (the second border, the thicker bar). The traces are drawn still in both modes: the flight of a packet needs the layout and the plan of a map (`../traffic/flight.tsx`), and the catalog only has to show how each ending looks.
- The labels take the map's look (`.catalog2d .map-button`, the 12 px mono face with the `--font-mono` the stage redefines).
- Tests: `catalog-scene.test.tsx` (jsdom: every entity in every variant and state with its caption, the traces and their stamps, the «+N», hidden from screen readers with the caption as the name, the 5 columns, the pieces that move) and `catalog-scene.browser.test.tsx` (Chromium: nothing draws outside its stage, the page does not scroll sideways on a phone).
- Not here: the page around the canvas and the list of icons (`src/ui/catalog/`), the pieces themselves (`../entities/`, `../pieces.tsx`, `../traffic/`), the Spanish text (`src/copy/`).
- May import: `@/copy`, only types from `@/engine`, other files of `src/scene/` (`@/scene/overlay/piece-label`, `@/scene/overlay/inspect-trigger`, `@/scene/traffic/flow-look`) and other files of `src/scene/map2d/` (`@/scene/map2d/<folder>/<file>`).
