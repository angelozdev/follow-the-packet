# scene/map2d/layout/arrange

How the pieces of the 2D map are arranged, as pure functions: no React, no DOM, no clock, no randomness. `layoutRow(world, pieces, density)` is the map in a row, left to right; the snake and the choice of mode come in Task M5 and build on the same files. It never reads what the player sent (no stamps, no packets): the size of the map depends on the pieces only. The design is in the spec (`docs/specs/2026-10-12-follow-the-packet-2d-map-design.md`, «La fila», «Piezas, enlaces y estaciones», «Los sellos»).

- `geometry.ts`: the types of the layout (`RowPiece`, `Group`, `Link`, `Mark`, `RowLayout`), `NORMAL` and `COMPACT` (the only two numbers that change with the density: the trunk, 44 or 32 px, and the lane, 66 or 52 px), and every measure with its name. `RowPiece` is the structural type of what the game gives the scene (`MapPiece` is assignable to it): kind, anchor, label, lock, state, `inOffice` and `insideBlock`.
- `pieces.ts`: `classify` says what the pieces stand for in the world (devices that hang from a bus, servers, clients, the routers along the main line from the office outward, the gateways of another office, the lone edge, the internet); `drawnOf` builds the `Drawn` that `stationsFor` reads; `labelOf` gives the lines and the box of a label, with the alert before the first line and the lock after the last, as in the button; `busOf` names the bus of a device (its subnet, its floor, or the kind of the world).
- `groups.ts`: floors, rows, buses and trunks. `floorsOf` lists the floors that have a drawn device, with the parts of their names (the name of the LAN, and its block, which declares the term `cidr`: `shownParts` leaves a part out until its term is known, and the text itself, «Oficina», «VPC», the separator, comes from `src/copy/` in Task M11). `stackFloors` puts the boxes one under the other (a box per floor, 14 px apart; the box of the office or the VPC around them from two floors, and around any subnet), a row of 100 px for each plugged device, the bus of each floor, the outline of each run of rows marked `insideBlock` and, under all the boxes, the devices with no cable. `trunkLane` and `trunkOf` give the trunks of several floors: parallel, 8 px apart, centered on the main line, joining at the head.
- `assemble.ts`: turns the roads of the map (lists of waypoints, named or not) into the graph that `route` walks, adds the middle of each pair of stops (`mid`) by splitting the link it falls on at half its length, and places the marks. `graphOf` gives the `Graph` of a layout.
- `row.ts`: `layoutRow`, which builds the roads and the marks and hands them to `assemble`.

## The row

Left to right, measured in px from the left edge of the drawing (`OUTER`, 16 px of margin on the four sides):

- **A device** is a row of 100 px: its label (right-aligned, 6 px from the icon), its icon of 48 px, and its link to the bus of its floor. All icons stand in one column, all buses at one `x`, and the link has the same length for every device: 102 px (the doorstep at 62 px plus the 40 px between marks) when no firewall is drawn, and, with a firewall, enough for the bar (87 px from the edge), its label (under the link, past the bar, 10 px under it) and a stamp. The label of a firewall is two lines (`labelOf`); the bar is 6 px by 40 px.
- **Stations of a device**: `door` (the edge of the icon), `doorstep` (62 px from it), `firewall` (the bar), `road` (where the link meets the bus). Every device has the four, with its bar drawn or not (`hiddenStop` still names the firewall of a device). The bus is a vertical line through the junctions of its rows; `bus-end` is a stub of 40 px past the last one; `bus` is the point where its trunk leaves it (the point of the bus nearest to its lane on the main line).
- **The main line** is horizontal, through the center of the rows (the middle between the first row and the last): the trunks join at the head, 44 or 32 px from the bus; then the stops in order (the lone edge, or the routers from the office outward, then the internet), each an icon of 48 px with its label above it, centered, 6 px up. The first router (or the lone edge) stands on the right border of the box of the office: it is inside and outside of it. The distance between two stops is at least `STOP_PITCH` (172 px) and never lets two labels touch (the label of the first stop starts 47 px from the bus, clear of the fans of the roads and of the end of the bus): the middle of the link between two stops (`mid`, where a packet ends when a router the map does not draw dropped it) stands 40 px from the mark of the next stop, which stands 22 px before it.
- **Servers and clients** are lanes of 100 px on the right of a vertical bus (`join:servers`), centered on the main line, servers first and then clients, in the order of the world: a lane of 66 or 52 px from the bus to the door of the icon, and the label 6 px to the right of it. The bus is 72 px (`SERVERS_GAP`) from the last stop, or from the end of its label if that is farther. `line-end` hangs 92 px below the last lane; with no lanes it is the end of a stub of 92 px past the last stop; with nothing on the line at all, the trunks end at it. It is drawn on every map (`kind: 'stub'`): a packet dropped past the last thing drawn travels a drawn link.
- **A device with no cable** (`connected: false`) stands under the boxes, in the column of the rest, with its short cable that reaches no bus (`kind: 'unplugged'`). Its station names are the same.
- **The other office** (`gateways`, the routers after the first that face a floor the map draws) stands in a second group under the office: the boxes of the floors it faces, with their own bus, and the router at the right of that bus, on the same `x` as the first router, joined to it by the private link (`kind: 'private'`, vertical). The main line does not move for it.

## The marks

A mark is where the stamps of a place stand: `{ station, point, axis, casillas }`. `point` is where the stamp of the middle casilla is; `axis` is the axis along which the fan opens, across the link (`y` on a link that runs left to right, `x` on a bus end); `casillas` is how many stamps of 36 px (25 px apart) fit, at most 3: it drops when a label or an icon, or the fan of a mark placed before it, is in the way. Where a mark stands:

- A place that is a piece or a bar (`door:<id>`, `router:<id>`, `internet`, `edge`, `firewall:<id>`): 22 px before the edge of the piece, on the side of its link that faces the bus or the office; for the bar, 22 px before its edge toward the bus (where a packet that reaches the device comes from).
- A place that is a point of a link (`doorstep`, `road`, `bus-end`, `mid`, `line-end`): on that point.
- Two marks of one link are at least 40 px apart, and the fan of one never covers a label or an icon, nor the fan of another: the tests sweep nine maps in both densities. The exceptions are listed under «Known limits».

## Known limits

The spec's numbers do not all fit together; these are the places where the tests do not hold and why.

- A trunk of 44 or 32 px is shorter than a fan: `mid:bus:<bus>-<stop>` (a packet lost between its bus and the first stop drawn) falls 34 or 28 px from the bus, closer than 22 px to the icon of that stop; and the mark of the first stop stands 22 px from the bus, so its fan touches the one of `road:<id>` of the row that is at its height. `bus-end` (40 px past the last row) touches the fan of `road:<id>` of the last row. The fans hold one casilla where that avoids it, and the sweeps of Task M8 will say if a state of a registered incident shows two stamps there.
- `mid:<a>-<door of a server>` and `mid:<door of a server>-line-end` lie along the bus of the lanes and can come within 40 px of a mark of a lane; `line-end` of a map with lanes is 92 px under the last one.
- The natural width is what these measures give, not what the prototype of the spec measured. 01-08 is 945 px wide in a row and 923 in the compact row (spec: 845 and 819). With the labels of the spec, which carry no lock, it is 893 and 867 (the locks are 52 and 56 px: `LOCK_WIDTH` of `metrics.ts`, which Task M21 measures); with a distance between stops that comes from the labels alone and not from `STOP_PITCH`, 859 and 845 (the 172 px between two stops keep the middle of the link 40 px from the mark of the next stop, and cost 34 and 22 px there). The 14 and 26 px that are left are not explained by anything in the repository. At 1280 px (a map of 900 px) 01-08 does not fit in a row, nor in the compact one, and Task M5 will choose the snake for it.

Not here: where the stamps go once there are some (`marks/`, Task M6), the snake and the mode (Task M5), the Spanish text of a group, a label or a card (`src/copy/`), drawing. May import: `@/copy` (types and `labelLines` through `metrics.ts`), `@/engine` (types only) and `@/scene/map2d/layout/<file>`. Its tests also read `@/engine`, `@/levels` and `@/testing`.
