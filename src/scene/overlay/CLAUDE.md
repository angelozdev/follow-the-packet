# scene/overlay

What the map puts on its HTML layer (`.html-layer`, the layer above the svg): the text and the controls the player reads and presses, at the pixels the layout gives. Everything here is plain DOM that a screen reader reads; the art under it is the svg (`../entities/`). The card the screen opens beside a piece is in `card/`.

- `piece-label.tsx`: `PieceLabel`, `MapPiece` and `pieceDomId`, the label of a piece as a button; `inspect-trigger.ts` is the type of the props of an inspect trigger (`InspectTrigger`, `TriggerProps`). A label shows a text the game already made safe for the ladder; it never decides what a piece is or whether it can be changed. It shows the lock of a fixed piece and the alert «!» of a device under attack, both also read as text, so neither can cover another label. A label has one button, however many cards its piece has: the card that button opens carries a tab for each (`src/ui/incident/map-cards/piece-card.tsx`), so a label never grows and has no second entry. A label gets the props of its trigger from the screen through the scene (a function from the piece id to them: the scene declares their type and imports nothing from the UI) and spreads them, so hovering, focusing or clicking it inspects the piece, and its own click calls the trigger's click and then opens its card; the trigger's `data-pinned` mark draws the ring of a pinned label. A resource's own name in the label (`[[servidor2]]`) is drawn as a name tag (`NameTag` of `@/design`), apart from the entity; the label's look (`map-button`) is in the design system's stylesheet, except the ring of a pinned label: one rule in `src/styles.css` serves it and the pinned stamp (`.stamp`) together, with an outline in forced colours.
- `group-captions.tsx`: `GroupCaptions`, the name of every group that has something to say, as a `span.caption` at the box `captionBoxes` (`../layout/captions.ts`) keeps for it, so the guarantee of the sweep of Task M8 holds for what is drawn. A name is the parts of `Group.name` whose term the player knows (`shownParts`), joined by a space: «Oficina» for an office (always), «VPC» for a VPC (once `vpc` is known), the name of a floor (always) or of a subnet (once `vpc` is known), and the block `192.168.1.0/24` once `cidr` is known. The words of the offices and the VPCs are `MAP_TEXT.groups` in `src/copy/`, and the layout puts them in `Group.name` (`../layout/arrange/groups.ts`), so the box of the name is as wide as the name can ever be. A group left with no part to show draws nothing. `BlockNames` writes the name of the block (`blockName`, which `incident-screen.tsx` takes from the card that classifies) on the tab of each outline of a block, as a `span.caption.map-tab-name` at the box `blockTabBoxes` keeps for it: the caller draws it only once `cidr` is known, as `MapStage` does.
- `map-labels.tsx`: `MapLabels`, the label of every piece the layout holds a box for. Each is the button the map has always had (`PieceLabel`, `piece-label.tsx`: its name, its lock, its alert, its trigger and its click), inside a `div.map-label` at the pixels of the box of the label with the frame added, in the order the Tab key must walk the map (`readingOrder`, whatever the order of the pieces it is given). The holder says how many lines the label has (`data-lines`, from `labelLines`): the label of a firewall is in two lines, its term and its resource, and the stylesheet breaks the line before the name tag; its width is the box of the layout and its height too, but the button inside has the width of its text, which the browser test of Task M21 compares with the model. Every label of the map is monospaced, 12 px on a line of 16 px, at the width the model counts (`.map-stage .map-button`: the name tag inside it has no border, padding or shade, so its width is its characters), and the font is the token `--font-mono` that `.map-stage` redefines without `ui-monospace` (`tokens.css` does not change). The stamps join the same order: `MapLabels` takes, besides, the `fans` of stamps (none by default) and `stamp`, what draws one stamp or «+N» of the reading order, and puts each right after the label of the piece that stands nearest to its fan (`readingOrder(layout, fans)`), so one walk of the Tab key goes from a piece to the stamps beside it and on to the next piece, and a `MapStamps` placed after `MapLabels` would have walked every label before the first stamp.
- `map-stamps.tsx`: `stampButtons`, which takes the fans, the runs they were made from (`DrawnRun`: a `StampedRun` with its piece id and its label), the frame and two triggers, and returns what `MapLabels` calls for each stamp and «+N» (it draws one entry of the list, for the reason above). A stamp is the button `StampButton` (`../entities/stamp.tsx`), named by its packet and how it ended (`stampLabel` of the plain label and `stampOf(run)`), with the trigger of its packet (`inspectTrigger(run.pieceId)`); a «+N» is `StampMore`, with the trigger of its list (`inspectStampsTrigger(more.ids)`: the ids of every packet of the place, which the screen turns into the id of the list with `PIECE_ID.stamps`, because `scene` cannot import `@/game`). Each stands in a `div.map-stamp` at the center of its place in the fan with the frame added (`translate(-50%, -50%)`, so no size of the stamp is written twice). The one under the pointer or with the focus comes to the front (`.map-stamp:hover`, `.map-stamp:focus-within`: `z-index: 1`), and, otherwise, the paint order is the order of the DOM: each stamp covers the edge of the one before it and the «+N», which is last in its fan, is on top.
- `card/` holds the anchor of the card the screen opens beside a piece (`card-anchor.tsx`) and the hook that brings it into view (`use-reveal-card.ts`), with their own guide.
- The HTML layer clips what hangs past its edge (`.html-layer`, `overflow: clip` in `src/styles.css`) and is never a scroll container: with `overflow: hidden` a browser scrolls it to show an element that takes the focus (the terminal of a laptop at the right edge of a 390 px map scrolled it 55 px), and from then on every label and card stood off its piece. `a card opened on a narrow map never scrolls the layer of the labels, so the labels keep their place over the drawing`, in `src/ui/incident/geometry/card-layout.browser.test.tsx`, guards it.
- The layer keeps the classes the helpers of the browser tests (`src/testing/`), the end-to-end tests and the inspection box read: `.caption` (not `.caption--soft`, which `MAP_MARKS` leaves out), `.map-button`, `button.stamp` (the stamps and «+N») and `.map-card`. The names of the groups are the direct children of the layer and the stylesheet places `.html-layer > .caption`, not every `.caption` under the stage: the card, which lives in the layer, holds `.caption`s of its own (the names of its trays) that must stay in its flow.
- Not here: the svg (`../entities/`), where anything goes (`../layout/`), where a card sits (`../cards/`), which stamps stand at a place (`../marks/`), the card itself (`src/ui/incident/map-cards/`), the Spanish text (`src/copy/`).
- May import: `@/copy`, `@/levels`, `@/design`, only types from `@/engine`, and other files of `src/scene/` (`@/scene/<folder>/<file>`).
