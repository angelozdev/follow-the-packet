# scene/overlay/card

The card the screen opens beside its piece on the map: the anchor that places it in the layer and the hook that brings it into view. The card itself (what it holds) is `src/ui/incident/map-cards/`; where it sits is decided by `../../cards/`; `MapStage` (`../../map-stage.tsx`) decides whether a card is open.

- `card-anchor.tsx`: `CardAnchor`, the card the screen opens beside its piece, as one `div.map-card` at the middle of the icon of its piece (the layout's `icons` box with the frame added) in the layer. It takes the piece, the others, the layout, the frame, the layer (the sheet's width and its height, with the room under the drawing) and the height of the window, asks `cardPlacement` (`../../cards/placement.ts`) how the card anchors and `cardSlot` (`../../cards/slot.ts`) where it sits, and writes the answer in the CSS variables of the card (`--card-width`, `--card-gap`, `--card-x`, `--card-y`, `--card-top` and `--card-room`), which `.map-card--left`, `.map-card--right` and `.map-card--dock` read in `src/styles.css`. The slot is worked out in each render from the layout it is given, never kept in a state: when the container changes size and the layout changes mode, the card follows its piece to the new place, and it is one element whose class is the only thing that changes with the side, so what the card holds (the picks of a terminal, the tab of a card) never remounts. It draws what it is given: `MapStage` is the one that decides whether a card is open (a piece the layout holds no icon box for opens none). The holder is `.map-card` (`MAP_CARD` in `../../html-layer.ts`), which the inspection box watches; `z-index: 30` puts it over the labels, the stamps and the labels that fly and under the inspection box and the title bar.
- `use-reveal-card.ts`: `useRevealCard`, which asks the browser to bring the card into view (`scrollIntoView`, `block: 'nearest'`; the card's scroll margins are the title bar and the dock margin) when it mounts and when another piece opens it, before the browser draws, and again, with a `ResizeObserver` on the card and on the layer that holds it, each time either changes size (Task M17: a terminal that grows with its output, or a drawing that grows under a docked card because the traceroute adds routers, took the foot of the card out of reach in a stage that scrolls): a docked card hangs from the bottom of a layer that is taller than the window, and a card beside a piece low in a drawing taller than the window would otherwise open out of sight. A change of size does not record the scroll positions again, so closing the card still puts the stage and the page back where they were when it opened. It scrolls the stage and the page and never the layer, which clips and does not scroll, and when the card goes (or another piece opens) every scroller above it goes back to where it was before the card came into view, so closing a card leaves the map where the player had it.
- Tests: `card-anchor.test.tsx` (jsdom: the side, the dock, the labels it avoids, the change of mode, the element the inspection box watches and the request to scroll into view) and `card-anchor.browser.test.tsx` (Chromium, with the real stylesheet: the card stays inside the map with its foot in view, is brought into view again when it or the drawing grows, leaves the stage and the page where they were when it closes, covers no label or piece, and stands above the labels that fly and below the inspection box and the title bar).
- The `.map-card` class the anchor gives the holder is `MAP_CARD` in `../../html-layer.ts`: the inspection box watches that element to choose its place, so renaming the class would leave the box blind to the card. The anchor knows nothing of the box.
- Not here: the card itself (`src/ui/incident/map-cards/`), where a card sits (`../../cards/`), the labels and the stamps (`../`).
- May import: `@/copy`, `@/design`, only types from `@/engine`, and other files of `src/scene/` (`@/scene/<folder>/<file>`).
