# scene

The isometric paper-and-ink map drawn with React Three Fiber: the entity catalog (`entities/`), where each piece sits (`layout.ts`: one column per subnet or a single row for an office, and the edge, the internet and the actors on the left while the map shows the outside), the road (`road.tsx`), labels and the cards that open beside a piece on an HTML layer (`overlay/`), live traffic (`traffic/`) and the canvas of the `/entidades` page (`catalog/`). `map-canvas.tsx` is the canvas both maps are drawn on; `ink.tsx` holds the paper box with ink edges that every piece is built from and the frame of every icon; `theme.ts` names the colours and line widths the map takes from the design tokens (`@/design`).

- A card opens beside its piece, on the side `cardPlacement` and `terminalSide` pick in `layout.ts`. A firewall's card sits 110px from it, on the side with more room. The terminal of a device or an actor sits 68px from it (just past its mat and its label) and opens on the side where it stays inside the map and covers no label, not even its own (the label of a house hangs to the left of it), so in an office it opens toward the end of the row with no other device. If neither side is clear (01-03, where the outside is drawn on the left of the row), it docks in the bottom left corner of the map, 6px from both edges and hanging from its bottom, so its height does not matter: the map is a band from the upper left to the lower right, so that corner has no label and no stamp unless the window is short: at 1280×800 and 1366×768 the card of Casa de Lucía, taller because its title takes two lines, still covers the bottom of the lost stamp. A map too narrow to hold the card keeps the side with more room. `cameraPosition` and `CAMERA_ZOOM` are the map's one camera, which the layout tests use to project positions to pixels.
- Draws what the game computed: piece labels, locks, states and every packet's outcome come in as props; it never decides an outcome.
- Not here: panels and screens (`src/ui/`).
- May import: `@/levels`, `@/copy`, `@/design`, and only types from `@/engine`.
