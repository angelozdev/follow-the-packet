# ui/shell/systems

The bar of systems, in the second row of the bottom strip (`../strip/`): the buttons that open what the player works with. `systems-bar.tsx` (`SystemsBar`) draws a row of `small` `Button`s from the list the screen gives it (`System`: an id, the label and what pressing it does) and `use-systems.ts` (`useSystems`) builds that list.

- **Terminal** and **Navegador** (`SYSTEMS_TEXT`, the same words as the tabs of the panel, so the same thing is never called two ways) are shortcuts to a tab that already exists in the panel of a piece: they have no content of their own. Each is drawn only when some origin piece has that tab (`panelOf`, over `originPieces`, the pieces of `send.from`): a provisional rule until the table of contents of the shell says what each incident offers. Once the incident is over no piece has the tab, so neither button is drawn. What the incident does not offer is hidden, not disabled (`caller-controls-visibility`: the screen draws the bar only when `useSystems` returns a button).
- Pressing one closes the system window that is open (whatever it is) and then does its own thing; it chooses the piece of the last origin the player had in view in a Terminal or Navegador tab (chosen in the tray of origins of the terminal, or used to send, or opened from the panel); before any, the first of `send.from` that has the tab. It sets that piece's tab (`usePanelChoice`), chooses the piece with `selection.showPiece` (which brings the focus to the heading of the panel, as «Ver fila en la tabla de rutas» does) and, when that piece already shows that tab, does nothing more, not even a move of the focus. The last origin is kept while rendering, as `useLuciaFold` keeps its news: it is the state of the screen and is saved nowhere. The browser has no tray of origins, so Navegador follows the one the terminal has chosen when that piece has a browser, and the first that has one when it has not.
- The row is reserved by the strip, so the bar is drawn centered in it when there is a button and nothing is drawn when there is none; the page never scrolls sideways at 390 px.
- **Registro (N)** (`REGISTRY_TEXT.open`, after Terminal and Navegador) is drawn only while the registry has a line, and opens and closes the registry window (`../windows/`: `useSystemWindow` holds the state, which the screen shares with the window, so the button takes `expanded`, `domId` and `controls` from the `System`, which `useSystems` fills from `buttonId` and `controlsId`). The same button closes the window in one click. It stays when the incident is over.
- Tests: `systems-bar.test.tsx` is the screen (what each incident offers, the piece each button chooses, the focus, nothing more when the tab is in view, the end of the incident, the button of the registry) and `systems-bar.browser.test.tsx` the geometry at 390 px; `src/levels/shell-names.test.ts` asks that a text that names a «botón» names one of these.
- Not here: what the tabs hold (`../panel/`), the choice of a piece (`../use-selection.ts`), the row of the strip (`../strip/`), the Spanish text (`src/copy/`).
