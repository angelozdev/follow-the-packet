# ui/shell/panel

The panel of what the player chose, in its zone at the right of the map (`.incident__panel`): the panel of a piece with its tabs, and the box of a packet or of a list of stamps. `shell-panel.tsx` (`ShellPanel`) is always on the screen and holds what must outlive the piece chosen, `piece-panel.tsx` (`PiecePanel`) draws one piece, `frame-of-part.ts` (`frameOfPart`) turns each part of a tab into its frame, `panel-handlers.ts` (`panelHandlers`) wires what the cards ask to the reducer of the session, `use-panel-drafts.ts` (`usePanelDrafts`) keeps what the player half did and `use-panel-choice.ts` (`usePanelChoice`) keeps the tab and the ficha chosen on each piece.

- What the panel offers is the model of `@/game` (`panelOf`, see its guide): the tabs of a piece in the fixed order «Estado», «Ajustes», «Tablas», «Terminal», «Navegador» and «Preguntas», only the ones it has, and the tab it opens on. This folder draws it and decides nothing about it.
- A piece is a `Card` named by its heading (an `h2` with the icon and the name of the piece; the `ref` is the one `useSelection` focuses on a choice with the keyboard), a region and never a dialog. Under the stripe, the roles of a server of the internet in one line, once, and the six tabs as the `Tabs` of `@/design` (named `PIECE_TEXT.tabsName`). «Estado» draws the fields of `inspect`. A tab that holds `sections` («Ajustes») draws its parts one under the other, each under its name (`PIECE_TEXT.parts`, an `h3`); a tab that holds `fichas` («Tablas», «Preguntas») draws them as a nested `Tabs` of the `underline` variant (so they do not read as six more tabs of the piece), named after the tab, when it has several, and, with one, the part alone under its name, except the rules of a firewall, which carry their own two tabs of direction (`TASK_CARD_TEXT.tabsName`); the terminal and the browser fill their tab.
- The foot of the panel is the foot of the part chosen (the actions of its frame, such as «Enviar» or «Agregar ruta», and «Listo» only where the frame has its own way out: the questions of a device, whose «Listo» confirms the picks and, with nothing more to show, releases the choice). It stays in view while the body scrolls above 900 px.
- Radix mounts only the panel of the tab chosen, so what the player has half done lives above it, in `usePanelDrafts`: the draft of the terminal and of the browser (shared by every piece), the picks of the questions of each device, the item picked up to classify, the trays of every table and the slots of the rules of each firewall. `ShellPanel` outlives the piece (it is the help when nothing is chosen), so a draft survives a change of tab, of ficha and of piece; the screen keys it by the case of a diagnose, which starts them again. The tab and the ficha chosen on each piece are `usePanelChoice`, state of the shell and not of `Tabs`, so a link from outside (the menu of a piece, «Ver fila en la tabla de rutas» of the registry) can set them: the screen calls the hook itself and gives the panel `choice` (a panel with none keeps its own, as the tests of the cards draw it), and gives it `markedSend` too, the send whose row the table of routes marks; a choice the panel no longer offers gives way to the tab it opens on.
- Picking another origin in the terminal chooses the piece of that origin (`onChoose`) and its «Terminal» tab, and keeps the other picks.
- `frameOfPart` is the one `switch` over the twelve kinds of part, provisional until the table of contents of the shell replaces it with `panelOf`.
- Tests: `piece-panel.test.tsx` draws `ShellPanel` over a session in jsdom (the tabs of every piece of every registered incident in order, sections, fichas, the locked piece, the finished incident, the drafts across tabs and pieces); the cards in `../../incident/map-cards/` are tested through it. `piece-panel.browser.test.tsx` draws the real screen and reads by computed style that the six tabs keep their pills and that the tabs inside «Tablas» and «Preguntas» (and the two directions of a firewall) are the underlined row, on one line at 1280×800 and at 390×844.
- Not here: the cards a part holds (`src/ui/incident/map-cards/`), the choice of a piece or a stamp (`../use-selection.ts`), the model of the tabs (`@/game`), the Spanish text (`src/copy/`).
- May import: `@/game`, `@/copy`, `@/design`, `@/scene`, `@/ui/incident/inspection-box`, `@/ui/incident/map-cards/*`, `@/ui/shell/inspect-help`, `@/ui/tagged-text`.
