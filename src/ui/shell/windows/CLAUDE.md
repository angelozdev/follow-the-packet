# ui/shell/windows

The system windows of the incident screen: the big windows the player opens from the bar of systems and that lie between the top strip and the bottom strip. Today two, the registry and the Almanaque. `use-system-window.ts` (`useSystemWindow`) is the state, `system-window.tsx` (`SystemWindow`) is the frame every window shares, `registry-window.tsx` (`RegistryWindow`) is the registry in it and `almanac-window.tsx` (`AlmanacWindow`) is the Almanaque, with `almanac-terms.tsx` (`AlmanacTerms`) and `almanac-lessons.tsx` (`AlmanacLessons`, `lessonsOf`).

- `useSystemWindow(available)` holds which window is open, or none (`open`): only one at a time, so `toggle(id)` opens that window and closes the one that was open, or closes it when it is the open one; `close()` closes whichever is open. `available` is what the incident offers now: a window that is open and stops being available closes (and stays closed when it comes back, as a registry that ran out of lines does), so the state is derived while rendering and a window that is not offered is never open. The state is the screen's: it is not saved and the next incident starts with none. `buttonId(id)` and `controlsId(id)` are the ids that tie the button of the bar to the window (the button, which names the list; and what it controls, the list of the registry), one pair for each window (the Almanaque has no `controlsId` use: its button, in the top strip, carries `aria-expanded` and no `aria-controls`). `SYSTEM_WINDOW` names the ids; a window of a later task adds its id there. The screen offers the Almanaque in every incident and the registry only while it has a line, so changing from one to the other is `toggle`.
- `SystemWindow` is `Dialog` of `@/design` with `modal={false}`: no overlay, no trapped focus and no dismissal by a press or the focus outside, so the top strip, the newest line and the bar stay in view and operable with a window open and using them never closes it; Escape, the X (`WINDOW_TEXT`, «Cerrar la ventana») and the button of the window close it (the screen toggles it in `useSystems`). Radix does not make the rest of the page inert in a non-modal dialog, so the screen marks `inert` the zone of Lucía, the box of the map and the panel while a window is open (`inert` on `.incident__lucia`, `.incident__map` and `.incident__panel`): neither the keyboard nor the pointer reaches what the window covers. Radix keeps Tab inside the window while the focus is in it (`FocusScope` loops it): Escape leaves it, and Tab from the strip or the bar passes through the window, never through the covered zones. The focus enters the window when it opens; when it closes, `giveTheFocusBack` puts it on the button of the window only when it ended on the page (`document.body`): a window closed by Terminal or Navegador, or by choosing a row, leaves the focus where that choice put it (the heading of the panel).
- The window is a child of the bottom strip (the `box` slot, so it comes after the bar in the DOM and Tab goes from the button into it) and takes its place from the screen's grid (`src/styles.css`): above 900 px it lies in the area of Lucía, the map and the panel, between the two strips, at nine tenths of the width and centered (`.incident__status > .dialog`, `position: absolute` in `main.incident`, which is the box it measures from); at 900 px and under it is `position: fixed`, fills the window above the bottom strip (`--status-bar-room` is the height of the strip) and covers the top strip, until the sheets of a later slice replace it. It takes no room from any zone: the map, the panel and both strips keep their rectangles, and the list scrolls inside the window (the body of its `Card`).
- The registry window shows `Registry` (`../status/`) with the lines of the incident, named «Registro»: choosing a row (the packet of a line) or pressing «Ver fila en la tabla de rutas» closes the window, because the result is in the panel, which the window covers; the screen does it for the line of the bottom strip too (`chooseLine` and `seeRow` close any window that is open). Terminal and Navegador (`../systems/`) close the open window and do their own thing. A registry with no line has no window: «Registro (N)» is not drawn and the window closes.
- The Almanaque window (`ALMANAC_TEXT`) holds `Tabs` of `@/design` with «Términos» and «Lecciones», and remembers its last tab in its own state, which lasts as long as the screen does (the window stays mounted while closed, so the state survives a close and the next incident starts on «Términos»). Radix keeps Tab inside the window while the focus is in it, and the `Tabs` keep their roving focus: the arrow keys, Home and End move between the two tabs and Tab leaves the tab list for the panel, never for the page. «Términos» is the glossary that used to be in Lucía's card: every term the player knows (`session.known`), the terms this incident introduces first and marked «nuevo», then the earlier ones, each group by name, each with the icon of the entity it names. «Lecciones» is the `takeaway` of each incident that has one star or more in the `progress` the screen gives and comes before or is the one being played, in the order of the course (`lessonsOf`), each under the title of its incident, and, with none, the sentence `ALMANAC_TEXT.noLessons`; a later incident would use terms the player has not met. The screen passes `progress` down every render, so the window of an incident the player has just finished shows its own lesson.
- Tests: `use-system-window.test.ts` (the state), `registry-window.test.tsx` (the screen: the window opens and Esc, the X and the button close it with the focus back on the button, the inert zones, Terminal, choosing a row, «Ver fila», a registry that runs out of lines), `almanac-window.browser.test.tsx` (the real Almanaque: where it lies at 1280×720 and 1280×600, the terms that scroll inside it, one real click on its button, and the arrow keys, Home, End, Tab and Shift+Tab with the window open) and `registry-window.browser.test.tsx` (the real window: where it lies at 1280×720, 1280×600 and 390×844, the list that scrolls inside it, one real click on the button of the open window and the Tab key with the window open). The screens that need a real press, Tab or a layout are in the browser; jsdom does not know `inert`, so it reads the attribute.
- Not here: the registry list and its lines (`../status/`, `src/game/view/registry/`), the buttons of the bar (`../systems/`) and the Almanaque button (`../strip/`), the frame of the window (`Dialog`, `src/design/`), the Spanish text (`src/copy/`). The screen tests of the Almanaque are in `../strip/top-strip.test.tsx`, because they begin at its button.
