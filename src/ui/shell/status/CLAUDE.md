# ui/shell/status

The status bar of the incident screen, fixed at the bottom of the window in every incident. `status-bar.tsx` (`StatusBar`) draws the newest line of the registry (`registryLines` of `@/game`, whose lines the screen gives it) in a region with `role="log"` (a live region that announces what is added; `role="status"` would have been the other fit, but the screen already holds one in Lucía's reply, which its tests look up by role) and, beside it, «Registro (N)», which opens the whole registry; `line-text.tsx` (`LineText`) draws one line; `registry.tsx` (`Registry`) is the open list.

- The bar says one line and nothing else. A packet line (a send of the player or a flow of the incident) is one `Button` of variant `text` with its label, its result and, when the player can place where it stopped, the reason, in the words of its box (`tips={false}`, as the box writes them: a definition would float over a button); pressing it chooses its packet as its stamp does, through `selection.trigger(pieceId, {})`, so the panel shows its box and hover previews nothing from here. The change that emptied the sends is a `Message` of variant `note` with `RESTART_TEXT.notice` as it is, which is the only place the notice is told (Lucía's card and the case card no longer carry it); the refusal of an entry of a table is a `Message` of variant `error` and the table keeps saying it too. With no line the bar says `SCREEN_TEXT.statusEmpty`, which names no term.
- «Registro (N)» (`REGISTRY_TEXT.open`) is a `Button` of variant `small` with `aria-expanded`; N is every line of the registry, the packets, the change that emptied the sends and the refusals. It is outside the `log` region, so that region holds the newest line only. With no line the button is disabled and the line that says so is beside it. Open, the registry is a list (`ol.incident__registry`, named by the button) above the line, inside the bar, so it takes its height from the page and is never a layer over the map: above 900 px the second row of the grid grows and the map and the column shrink, and at 900 px and under the bar, which is sticky and in the flow, grows upward and the page grows with it, so the last control of the page still ends above it. The list is capped at a quarter of the window (`25vh`) and scrolls inside. The newest line is first; a change of the network while it is open leaves the list with the line of the change and N counts again.
- A row is a line, or a group: the packet lines that follow each other and print the same text (`registryRows` of `@/game`) are one row, the newest line plus a `Button` of variant `small` that says how many times it happened (`REGISTRY_TEXT.repeats`, `aria-expanded`) and unfolds the older lines, one row each, below it. A line of change or of refusal is never in a group. Which groups are unfolded is the state of `Registry` keyed by the printed text, so it survives a new line at the top. Every packet line, in a row or unfolded, chooses its packet.
- «Ver fila» (`REGISTRY_TEXT.seeRow`, a `small` `Button`) comes after a packet line that has `row`, in the bar (outside the `log` region), in a row and in an unfolded line; it is absent from every other line (it is hidden, not disabled). Pressing it does what the screen says (`seeRow`): chooses the piece of the row, opens its «Tablas» tab and the ficha of routes, marks the row of that send in the table, and leaves the focus on the heading of the panel.
- Placement is the screen's: above 900 px `.incident__status` is the second row of the grid of `main.incident`, under the map and the column, so both end where it begins and the page does not scroll; at 900 px and under `main.incident` is a column of flex items (the map, the box that previews, the column) with the bar last and `position: sticky` at the bottom of the window, so the page scrolls under it and, at the end, its last control ends above the bar with no padding to measure. The bar grows with its line (a notice is taller than a send) and with the open registry; the column and the map take what is left.
- Tests: the screen tests are in `src/ui/incident/incident-screen.test.tsx` (`the status bar of the incident screen`, `the registry of the status bar` and the tests of the notice), the grouping is also tested in `src/game/view/registry/lines.test.ts`, and the geometry is `status-bar.browser.test.tsx` here.
- Not here: the lines, their order and their grouping (`src/game/view/registry/`), the choice of a packet (`../use-selection.ts`), the Spanish text (`src/copy/`).
- May import: `@/game`, `@/copy`, `@/design`, `@/scene` (types), `@/ui/tagged-text`.
