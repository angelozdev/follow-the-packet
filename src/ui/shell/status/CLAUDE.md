# ui/shell/status

The newest line and the registry of the incident screen. `newest-line.tsx` (`NewestLine`) draws the newest line of the registry (`registryLines` of `@/game`, whose lines the screen gives it) in a region with `role="log"` (a live region that announces what is added; `role="status"` would have been the other fit, but the screen already holds one in Lucía's reply, which its tests look up by role); `status-bar.tsx` (`StatusBar`) puts it in the bottom strip (`../strip/bottom-strip.tsx`) with «Registro (N)» and the open registry for now, and the bar of systems that the screen gives it (`../systems/`); `line-text.tsx` (`LineText`) draws one line; `registry.tsx` (`Registry`) is the open list.

- The line says one thing and nothing else. A packet line (a send of the player or a flow of the incident) is one `Button` of variant `text` with its label, its result and, when the player can place where it stopped, the reason, in the words of its box (`tips={false}`, as the box writes them: a definition would float over a button); pressing it chooses its packet as its stamp does, through `selection.trigger(pieceId, {})`, so the panel shows its box and hover previews nothing from here. The change that emptied the sends is a `Message` of variant `note` with `RESTART_TEXT.notice` as it is, which is the only place the notice is told (Lucía's card and the case card no longer carry it); the refusal of an entry of a table is a `Message` of variant `error` and the table keeps saying it too. With no line it says `SCREEN_TEXT.statusEmpty`, which names no term.
- The line has a box of a fixed height: the region with `role="log"` takes what is left of the row beside «Ver fila en la tabla de rutas» and «Registro (N)», never grows past the row (`--newest-room`, three lines of text) and scrolls by itself (`overflow-y: auto`) when a line does not fit. It has `tabIndex={0}`, so the keyboard reaches it and scrolls it, and it stays the one `log` of the page. A `Message` in it has a margin of its own above, where its tab sits, so that the tab is inside what scrolls; a notice of a line (at 1280 px) fits whole, and at 390 px the box scrolls to its end.
- «Registro (N)» (`REGISTRY_TEXT.open`) is a `Button` of variant `small` with `aria-expanded`; N is every line of the registry, the packets, the change that emptied the sends and the refusals. It is outside the `log` region, so that region holds the newest line only. With no line the button is not drawn (hidden, not disabled: it is no action button) and the line that says so stands alone; a registry that was open when the lines ran out stays closed when a line comes back (`StatusBar` closes it while it has no line). Open, the registry is a list (`ol.incident__registry`, named by the button) in a box over the strip, a layer over the bottom of the map that takes no room: the strip keeps its height, and the box is capped at a quarter of the window (`25vh`) and scrolls inside. The newest line is first; a change of the network while it is open leaves the list with the line of the change and N counts again.
- A row is a line, or a group: the packet lines that follow each other and print the same text (`registryRows` of `@/game`) are one row, the newest line plus a `Button` of variant `small` that says how many times it happened (`REGISTRY_TEXT.repeats`, `aria-expanded`) and unfolds the older lines, one row each, below it. A line of change or of refusal is never in a group. Which groups are unfolded is the state of `Registry` keyed by the printed text, so it survives a new line at the top. Every packet line, in a row or unfolded, chooses its packet.
- «Ver fila en la tabla de rutas» (`REGISTRY_TEXT.seeRow`, a `small` `Button`) comes after a packet line that has `row`, beside the log (outside the `log` region), in a row and in an unfolded line; it is absent from every other line (it is hidden, not disabled). Pressing it does what the screen says (`seeRow`): chooses the piece of the row, opens its «Tablas» tab and the ficha of routes, marks the row of that send in the table, and leaves the focus on the heading of the panel.
- Placement is the strip's (`../strip/`): above 900 px the bottom strip is the last row of the grid of `main.incident`, under Lucía, the map and the panel, so all three end where it begins and the page does not scroll; at 900 px and under it is the last flex item of `main.incident`, sticky at the bottom of the window.
- Tests: the screen tests are in `src/ui/incident/incident-screen.test.tsx` (`the status bar of the incident screen`, `the registry of the status bar` and the tests of the notice), the grouping is also tested in `src/game/view/registry/lines.test.ts`, the geometry of the strip is `../strip/bottom-strip.browser.test.tsx` and the one of the open registry is `status-bar.browser.test.tsx` here.
- Not here: the lines, their order and their grouping (`src/game/view/registry/`), the choice of a packet (`../use-selection.ts`), the strip that holds them (`../strip/`), the Spanish text (`src/copy/`).
