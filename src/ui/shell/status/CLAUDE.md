# ui/shell/status

The status bar of the incident screen, fixed at the bottom of the window in every incident. `status-bar.tsx` (`StatusBar`) draws the newest line of the registry (`registryLines` of `@/game`, whose first line the screen gives it, or null) in a region with `role="log"` (a live region that announces what is added; `role="status"` would have been the other fit, but the screen already holds one in Lucía's reply, which its tests look up by role).

- It says one line and nothing else. A packet line (a send of the player or a flow of the incident) is one `Button` of variant `text` with its label, its result and, when the player can place where it stopped, the reason, in the words of its box (`tips={false}`, as the box writes them: a definition would float over a button); pressing it chooses its packet as its stamp does, through `selection.trigger(pieceId, {})`, so the panel shows its box and hover previews nothing from here. The change that emptied the sends is a `Message` of variant `note` with `RESTART_TEXT.notice` as it is, which is the only place the notice is told (Lucía's card and the case card no longer carry it); the refusal of an entry of a table is a `Message` of variant `error` and the table keeps saying it too. With no line the bar says `SCREEN_TEXT.statusEmpty`, which names no term.
- Placement is the screen's: above 900 px `.incident__status` is the second row of the grid of `main.incident`, under the map and the column, so both end where it begins and the page does not scroll; at 900 px and under `main.incident` is a column of flex items (the map, the box that previews, the column) with the bar last and `position: sticky` at the bottom of the window, so the page scrolls under it and, at the end, its last control ends above the bar with no padding to measure. The bar grows with its line (a notice is taller than a send); the column and the map take what is left.
- Tests: the screen tests are in `src/ui/incident/incident-screen.test.tsx` (`the status bar of the incident screen` and the tests of the notice), the geometry is `status-bar.browser.test.tsx` here.
- Not here: the lines and their order (`src/game/view/registry/`), the choice of a packet (`../use-selection.ts`), the Spanish text (`src/copy/`).
- May import: `@/game`, `@/copy`, `@/design`, `@/scene` (types), `@/ui/tagged-text`.
