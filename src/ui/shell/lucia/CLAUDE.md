# ui/shell/lucia

The zone of Lucía, at the left of the incident screen. `lucia-zone.tsx` (`LuciaZone`) holds what the caller puts in it: her card and, under it, the case, the question and the result, as direct children of the zone; the screen tells it to be `inert` while a system window covers it (`../windows/`). `use-lucia-fold.ts` (`useLuciaFold`) is the fold of the zone and `lucia-news.ts` (`luciaNews`, `hasNews`) says what is new for her.

- The zone (`.incident__lucia`) has a fixed width (`--lucia-width` in `src/styles.css`) and a height capped at the room between the strip and the bottom strip, with a scroll of its own; the text of her cards may grow and never moves the box of the map, because the box is a cell of the grid and the width of the zone does not change. Under 901 px it has no cap and no scroll of its own and lies under the strip, above the map.
- The zone folds to her band: the arrow in the stripe of her card (a `small` `Button` with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` that names the zone, `LUCIA_TEXT.foldLabel`) asks `useLuciaFold`, which starts unfolded in every incident, keeps the fold in the state of the screen (nothing is saved: the next incident starts unfolded) and gives the zone its `id`. Folded, the body of her card (`Card` with `folded`) and the cards under it (each takes `hidden` from the screen, which is the one that knows the fold) stay mounted but hidden, so they leave the Tab order and the accessibility tree and keep their state: the hint line that takes the focus when it mounts does not take it again on unfolding. Folding changes the height of the zone and never the box of the map.
- The zone opens by itself when she says something new, and only then (spec call M2 of 2026-10-29). `luciaNews(session)` is a count for each thing that is news when it grows: the hints shown in the case, the attempt of her reply to a rejected solution, the alerts of an intruder that started, the cases opened, whether her question is asked and whether the result is shown; `hasNews(before, after)` is true when any count grew. A count that falls back is not news: her reply goes away when a send, an answer or a change of the network clears it, and the hints start again at the next case, which is news by its own count; a task ticked, an option chosen and an alert that ends change no count. `useLuciaFold(session)` compares the counts of every render with the ones it saw and, when one grew, unfolds in that same render (the state is set while rendering, not in an effect), so her alert, her question and her result are never drawn hidden, a hint line that takes the focus when it mounts finds its zone shown, and the first render never unfolds anything. If the player folds her again she stays folded until the next thing new. Her alert comes from the panel (a rule that lets an intruder in) while she is folded; her reply, her hints, her question and her result come from buttons inside her zone, so today they arrive with her unfolded, and the fold would open for them all the same.
- The zone draws nothing of its own: the screen decides what goes in it (`caller-controls-visibility`).
- Not here: what the cards show (`src/ui/incident/side-cards/`), the Spanish text (`src/copy/`).
- May import: `react`, `@/game` and `@/levels` (the session and `isGuided`, for what is new).
