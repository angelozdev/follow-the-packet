# ui/shell

The fixed places of the incident screen that do not hold a map: for now, the column at the right of the map and what the player chooses to read in it. `shell-column.tsx` (`ShellColumn`) is the column, `use-selection.ts` (`useSelection`) is the choice, `inspect-help.tsx` (`InspectHelp`) is the panel when nothing is chosen, `panel/` (which has its own guide) draws the panel of what is chosen: a piece with its tabs, or the box of a packet, and `status/` (which has its own guide) is the status bar fixed at the bottom of the window.

- The column has two regions, one under the other. The region of Lucía (`.incident__lucia`) holds her card, the clock, the case, the question and the result, as the side panel held them. The panel is the element the caller gives, a direct child of the column (`.incident__side`): the help, or the card of what the player chose.
- Above 900 px the column is as tall as the window less the status bar (the grid gives the bar its own row, so the column and the map end where the bar begins) and the page does not scroll: the region of Lucía takes what her cards need up to 60 % of the column and scrolls by itself, and the panel takes the rest, with at least a third of the column, and scrolls its own body under its stripe (the tab bar of a piece stays at the top of that body and the foot of the chosen part, its actions and its «Listo», stays under it). The column itself never scrolls. At 900 px and under it keeps the stacking of before: the map, Lucía and the panel under it, with no cap and no scroll of their own, and the page scrolls under the status bar, which sticks to the bottom of the window.
- The player chooses a piece, a stamp, a packet or a «+N» with a click, a tap, Enter or Space; hovering or focusing only previews its box over the map (`../incident/use-inspection.ts`). `useSelection` holds the key chosen and the element that carried the click, and gives the screen the triggers it needs: `trigger(key, hover)` keeps the hover and focus props, replaces the click with the choice and wears `data-pinned` (the ring of the label or of the stamp) while the key is the chosen one; `choosePiece(key)` chooses a piece with no click, as the terminal does when the player picks another origin. The choice lasts until another is chosen or «Cerrar» is pressed in the box of a packet or «Listo» closes the questions of a device; a piece has no «Cerrar». It is released when `inspect` has nothing for it any more (the piece or the packet is no longer drawn). The ring of a piece on the map is `activePieceId`, the piece chosen (`pieceId`).
- A choice made with the keyboard moves the focus to the heading of the panel, which is a region named by it and never a dialog. Escape from inside the panel gives the focus back to the piece or the stamp and keeps the choice, and nothing else: it closes no card, because no card opens beside a piece any more; the focus that comes back opens no preview. Under 901 px a choice brings the panel into view, the least the player has to scroll. A hover never moves the focus or the page.
- The column draws nothing of its own: the caller decides what goes in each region (`caller-controls-visibility`).
- Not here: what the cards show (`src/ui/incident/side-cards/`, `src/ui/incident/map-cards/`), the box that previews (`src/ui/incident/`), the map (`src/scene/`), what a piece says about itself (`inspect`, `panelOf`, `src/game/`), visible Spanish text (`src/copy/`).
