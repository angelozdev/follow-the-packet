# ui/shell

The fixed places of the incident screen that do not hold a map: for now, the column at the right of the map and what the player chooses to read in it. `shell-column.tsx` (`ShellColumn`) is the column, `use-selection.ts` (`useSelection`) is the choice, `selection-panel.tsx` (`SelectionPanel`) is the panel of what is chosen and `inspect-help.tsx` (`InspectHelp`) is the panel when nothing is.

- The column has two regions, one under the other. The region of Lucía (`.incident__lucia`) holds her card, the note of the box with no room, the clock, the case, the question and the result, as the side panel held them. The panel is the element the caller gives, a direct child of the column (`.incident__side`): the help, or the card of what the player chose.
- Above 900 px the column is as tall as the window and the page does not scroll: the region of Lucía takes what her cards need up to 60 % of the column and scrolls by itself, and the panel takes the rest, with at least a third of the column, and scrolls its own body under its stripe. The column itself never scrolls. At 900 px and under it keeps the stacking of before: the map, Lucía and the panel under it, with no cap and no scroll of their own, and the page scrolls.
- The player chooses a piece, a stamp, a packet or a «+N» with a click, a tap, Enter or Space; hovering or focusing only previews its box over the map (`../incident/use-inspection.ts`). `useSelection` holds the key chosen and the element that carried the click, and gives the screen the triggers it needs: `trigger(key, hover)` keeps the hover and focus props, replaces the click with the choice and wears `data-pinned` (the ring of the label or of the stamp) while the key is the chosen one. The choice lasts until another is chosen or «Cerrar» is pressed in the box of a packet; a piece has no «Cerrar». It is released when `inspect` has nothing for it any more (the piece or the packet is no longer drawn). The ring of a piece on the map is `activePieceId`, the one an open card gave it (`pieceId`).
- A choice made with the keyboard moves the focus to the heading of the panel, which is a region named by it and never a dialog. Escape from inside the panel gives the focus back to the piece or the stamp and keeps the choice; the focus that comes back opens no preview. Under 901 px a choice brings the panel into view, the least the player has to scroll. Neither happens when the same click opens a card (`opensCard`): the card keeps the focus and its own reveal. A hover never moves the focus or the page. These are the rules that the cards beside the pieces keep until they leave.
- The panel of a piece is a `Card` with its icon and its name on the stripe and the fields of its inspection under it; the panel of a packet or of a list of stamps is the inspection box with «Cerrar» (`InspectionBox` of `../incident/`).
- The column draws nothing of its own: the caller decides what goes in each region (`caller-controls-visibility`).
- Not here: what the cards show (`src/ui/incident/side-cards/`), the box that previews (`src/ui/incident/`), the map (`src/scene/`), what a piece says about itself (`inspect`, `src/game/`), visible Spanish text (`src/copy/`).
