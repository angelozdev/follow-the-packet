# ui/shell

The fixed places of the incident screen around the map: the strips across the top and the bottom (`strip/`, with its own guide), the zone of Lucía at the left (`lucia/`, with its own guide), the panel of what the player chooses at the right (`panel/`, with its own guide, and `inspect-help.tsx`, `InspectHelp`, when nothing is chosen) and, in the bottom strip, the newest line and the registry (`status/`, with its own guide), the bar of systems (`systems/`, with its own guide) and the system windows (`windows/`, with its own guide). `use-selection.ts` (`useSelection`) is the choice. The map and the box that previews over it are in `src/scene/` and `../incident/`.

- `main.incident` has five zones, and their order in the DOM is their order of reading, of Tab and of the stacking under 901 px: the strip (`.incident__strip`), the zone of Lucía (`.incident__lucia`), the box of the map (`.incident__map`, with the holder of the preview `.incident__inspection` after it), the panel (`.incident__panel`, an `aside` whose direct child is the help, the box of a packet or the panel of a piece) and the bottom strip (`.incident__status`). Nothing floats over the map but the preview and, while a system window is open (`windows/`, a child of the bottom strip that lies between the two strips), that window, which covers the map, the zone of Lucía and the panel: the screen marks those three `inert` while it is open, so neither Tab nor the pointer reaches them, and the strips and the bar stay in view and operable.
- Above 900 px the zones are the cells of a grid as tall as the window: the strip on top, the bottom strip (a fixed height for the whole incident) at the bottom, and between them Lucía (a fixed width, `--lucia-width`), the box of the map (what is left) and the panel (`--panel-width`: 24 % of the window between 300 and 380 px). The page does not scroll. The zone of Lucía is as tall as her cards need up to the room between the strip and the bottom strip and scrolls by itself; the panel keeps all that room and scrolls its own body under its stripe (the tab bar of a piece stays at the top of that body and the foot of the chosen part, its actions and its «Listo», stays under it); the box of the map is the free gap and holds the stage of the map, which does not scroll and is not the size of the box: it is the whole window under the top strip, so the map is drawn also under the card of Lucía, the panel and the bottom strip, which lie over it (`z-index`, and a click on them reaches them and not the map) and the empty room under a short card of Lucía is map too; the camera (`src/scene/camera/`) fits the drawing into the box, the free gap, and moves it (between 901 px and the width the fixed zones leave the map 360 px, the drawing keeps 360 px and the fit shrinks it into the box). The fold of Lucía (`lucia/`, which also unfolds by itself when she says something new), the text of Lucía, the choice of a piece and a hover, a line of the registry that arrives, a notice and the first button of the bar never change the rectangle of the box of the map: it is a cell, and the sizes of its neighbours are fixed. The free gap is still only the cell to the right of her zone, not the room under her card: the fit leaves nothing there.
- At 900 px and under the page keeps the stacking in the reading order: the strip, Lucía, the map, the panel and the bottom strip last, sticky at the bottom of the window with the same height; the zones have no cap and no scroll of their own and the page scrolls under the strip.
- The player chooses a piece, a stamp, a packet or a «+N» with a click, a tap, Enter or Space; hovering or focusing only previews its box over the map (`../incident/use-inspection.ts`). `useSelection` holds the key chosen and the element that carried the click, and gives the screen the triggers it needs: `trigger(key, hover)` keeps the hover and focus props, replaces the click with the choice and wears `data-pinned` (the ring of the label or of the stamp) while the key is the chosen one; `choosePiece(key)` chooses a piece with no click, as the terminal does when the player picks another origin. `showPiece(key)` chooses a piece for a button of the bar of systems and always moves the focus to the heading of the panel. «Ver fila en la tabla de rutas» of a send (`showRow(key, send)`) is `showPiece` that also keeps the number of the send for the table of routes to mark (`markedSend`, dropped by any other choice and once the history no longer holds that send, as after a change of the network) and always moves the focus to the heading of the panel. The choice lasts until another is chosen or the X of a piece, the «Cerrar» of the box of a packet or the «Listo» that closes the questions of a device releases it. It is released when `inspect` has nothing for it any more (the piece or the packet is no longer drawn). The ring of a piece on the map is `activePieceId`, the piece chosen (`pieceId`).
- A choice made with the keyboard moves the focus to the heading of the panel, which is a region named by it and never a dialog. Escape from inside the panel gives the focus back to the piece or the stamp and keeps the choice, and nothing else: it closes no card, because no card opens beside a piece; the focus that comes back opens no preview. Under 901 px a choice brings the zone of the panel into view, the least the player has to scroll (`revealThePanel`: the zone and not the card, so the page ends right under the panel and a preview above it that closes moves nothing under the pointer). A hover never moves the focus or the page.
- The screen decides what goes in each zone (`caller-controls-visibility`); the zones draw nothing of their own.
- Tests: `shell-frame.test.tsx` is the frame in jsdom (the X of the panel of a piece, the reading order of the zones, and the fold of the zone of Lucía: it starts unfolded, folded she is her band, she unfolds by herself when something new arrives and a ticked task does not unfold her) and `shell-frame.browser.test.tsx` the frame in a real window (the page does not scroll at 1280×720, 1440×900 and 1280×600, at the fit in four windows no label of any registered incident lies under a zone or outside the free gap (the incidents that do not fit at the minimum zoom are named in the test) and the map is drawn under the card of Lucía, the panel and the bottom strip, which take the click, the box of the map keeps its rectangle, the panel is a quarter of the window between its floor and its ceiling, the drawing keeps 360 px and the camera shows its right edge between 901 px and the width the zones need, and, at 900 px and under, the stacking order, the fold at 390 px and a chosen panel brought into view at 390×844 and 390×300); `use-selection.test.tsx` tests the choice.
