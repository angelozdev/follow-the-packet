# ui/shell/strip

The two strips of the incident screen. `top-strip.tsx` (`TopStrip`) is the strip across the top: «← Roadmap» (`SCREEN_TEXT.back`, a `text` `Button` that links to `#/`), the title of the incident as the `h1` of the page and the «Sin animaciones» checkbox (`MotionToggle`, at the right). `bottom-strip.tsx` (`BottomStrip`) is the strip across the bottom, `.incident__status`, with `use-room-for-the-bar.ts` (`useRoomForTheBar`).

- The top strip is a row of its own, the first zone of `main.incident` (`.incident__strip`), so it floats over nothing and the map box starts under it. It is first in the reading order: strip, Lucía, map, panel and bottom strip. It takes its facts from the caller (the title, whether animations are off and what changes that) and decides nothing about them.
- The bottom strip is a band of the full width and a fixed height for the whole incident: two rows, the one of the newest line (`--newest-room`, 4.75 rem, up to three lines of text) and the one of the bar of systems (`--systems-room`, 2.5 rem), both in `src/styles.css`. The caller gives it three places, `line`, `bar` and `box` (the line is `../status/`, the bar is `../systems/` and the box is the open registry), and it draws them as they come: the line in the first row, the bar in the second and the box over the strip. The row of the bar is reserved whether or not the caller gives it a bar, so no line that is long, no notice and no first button changes the height of the strip, and so the box of the map above it never moves. Nothing in it changes its height: a line that does not fit scrolls inside its own box (`../status/`).
- The box is positioned over the strip (`bottom: 100%` of it), between the zone of Lucía and the panel above 900 px and across the full width at 900 px and under: it covers what is under it and takes no room from any zone. Under 901 px the strip is `position: sticky` at the bottom of the window and `useRoomForTheBar` keeps `--status-bar-room` equal to its height, so what the browser scrolls into view stops above it.
- Not here: the newest line and the registry (`../status/`), the Spanish text (`src/copy/`), the clock and the almanac, which arrive in later tasks of the shell.
- May import: `react`, `@/copy`, `@/design`, `@/ui/motion-toggle`, and `@mantine/hooks`.
