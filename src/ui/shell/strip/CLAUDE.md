# ui/shell/strip

The two strips of the incident screen. `top-strip.tsx` (`TopStrip`) is the strip across the top: «← Roadmap» (`SCREEN_TEXT.back`, a `text` `Button` that links to `#/`), the title of the incident as the `h1` of the page, the «Almanaque» button (`ALMANAC_TEXT.open`, a `small` `Button`; the caller gives its `id`, whether its window is open and what pressing it does), the clock of the game when the caller gives one (`clock`, a slot) and the «Sin animaciones» checkbox (`MotionToggle`, at the right). `game-clock.tsx` (`GameClock`) is the clock. `bottom-strip.tsx` (`BottomStrip`) is the strip across the bottom, `.incident__status`, with `use-room-for-the-bar.ts` (`useRoomForTheBar`).

- The top strip is a row of its own, the first zone of `main.incident` (`.incident__strip`), so it floats over nothing and the map box starts under it. It is first in the reading order: strip, Lucía, map, panel and bottom strip. It takes its facts from the caller (the title, whether animations are off and what changes that, and the clock) and decides nothing about them. Its height is fixed (`--strip-room` in `src/styles.css`) whatever it holds, so a clock in it never moves the box of the map. Above 900 px it is one row, whatever it holds: nothing in it shrinks but the title, which cuts with «…» when the row is short (the narrowest desktop, 901 px, holds the title of 02-12, the Almanaque and the clock with a few pixels to spare); under 901 px it may grow to more rows (`flex-wrap`), because five things do not fit one row of a phone, and the page does not scroll sideways.
- The bottom strip is a band of the full width and a fixed height for the whole incident: two rows, the one of the newest line (`--newest-room`, 4.75 rem, up to three lines of text) and the one of the bar of systems (`--systems-room`, 2.5 rem), both in `src/styles.css`. The caller gives it three places, `line`, `bar` and `box` (the line is `../status/`, the bar is `../systems/` and the box is the system window, `../windows/`), and it draws them as they come: the line in the first row, the bar in the second and the box over the strip. The row of the bar is reserved whether or not the caller gives it a bar, so no line that is long, no notice and no first button changes the height of the strip, and so the box of the map above it never moves. Nothing in it changes its height: a line that does not fit scrolls inside its own box (`../status/`).
- The `box` slot holds a system window (`../windows/`): it is a child of the strip in the DOM, after the bar, so Tab goes from a button of the bar into it, and it is not part of its box: it takes its place from the grid of `main.incident` (above 900 px it lies in the area of Lucía, the map and the panel, between the two strips; at 900 px and under it is `position: fixed` above the strip), covers what is under it and takes no room from any zone. For that the strip is not positioned above 900 px (`main.incident` is the box the window measures from); under 901 px the strip is `position: sticky` at the bottom of the window and `useRoomForTheBar` keeps `--status-bar-room` equal to its height, so what the browser scrolls into view, and the fixed window, stop above it.
- The «Almanaque» button opens and closes the window of the Almanaque (`../windows/`) like the buttons of the bar do theirs: `aria-expanded`, an `id` that the window gives the focus back to, and no `aria-controls`, because the window is a `dialog` whose element the screen does not name. The window covers the zone of Lucía, the map and the panel above 900 px and leaves this strip in view and operable, so the same button closes it and the button of «Historial» changes the window in one click; under 901 px the window fills the screen over this strip (an interim of the shell), so there the X and Escape close it.
- The clock is a region named «Reloj del juego» (`CLOCK_TEXT.title`, as its `aria-label`; the name is not drawn and neither is a title or an icon: the room is the chips'): the seconds the game clock has run as «N min SS s» (`CLOCK_TEXT.time`, a live region) and one chip for each step of the incident (`ChipTray` with no `picked`, the group named «Esperar» but not drawn: the chips already say it), which the screen turns into `wait`. The screen gives it only in an incident that has a clock (`clockSteps` is not empty) while it is open, and the strip stays one row: at 901 px every registered incident has the same strip height. The time is the game's and no piece's, so neither the terminal nor the cache has wait chips, and waiting keeps the sends and the last answer of the terminal.
- Not here: the newest line and the registry (`../status/`), the Almanaque window (`../windows/`), the Spanish text (`src/copy/`).
- May import: `react`, `@/copy`, `@/design`, `@/levels` (the type of a clock step), `@/ui/motion-toggle`, `@/ui/tagged-text`, and `@mantine/hooks`.
- Tests: `top-strip.test.tsx` (the screen: the clock is a region of the strip, only in an incident with a clock and while it is open, and the Almanaque from its button: the window and its tabs, Esc and the X, the change with Historial, the last tab, Lecciones and Términos) `top-strip.browser.test.tsx` (the strip is one row of one height at 901 px in every registered incident, with the clock and the Almanaque) and `bottom-strip.browser.test.tsx` (the strip keeps its height with no line, a long line, the notice and the first button; a line that does not fit scrolls inside its box, which the keyboard reaches; the notice reads whole at 1280×720; the box of the map keeps its rectangle; the row of the bar stays reserved; the open registry covers no control of the strip).
