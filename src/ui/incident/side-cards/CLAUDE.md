# ui/incident/side-cards

The cards of the zone of Lucía of the incident screen (`src/ui/shell/lucia/`), at the left of the map and, under 901 px, above it: Lucía's card (its stripe carries the arrow that folds her zone when the screen gives it a `fold`, and a folded card shows only the stripe; tasks, the "Enviar solución" button and her reply and hints; the report and the tasks mark each new term with «nuevo» once, at its first appearance on the card, however many times they use it; a diagnose has no tasks or button, and its hints are those of the case), Lucía's question (the incident's own, or «¿Qué pasó?»; `explanation-card.tsx`), the card of the case a diagnose is on (`case-card.tsx`) and the result (`result-card.tsx`). The case, the question and the result take an optional `hidden`, which the screen sets while the zone of Lucía is folded: the card stays mounted and leaves the page, the Tab order and the accessibility tree. The family of the cards that open beside a piece of the map is `../map-cards/`.

- The case card sits in the zone of Lucía, below her card, in a diagnose incident until the result: it shows the case's report and the tests the origin can send (the list of the tasks, ticked once sent), and, in a case with a fix, a section «Arréglalo para cerrar el caso» with where it is fixed (the title of the card of each table, which the player opens from its piece on the map) and its tasks ticked once they hold, built with the same list as the tests (`TickList`); the primary button «Ya sé la causa» opens the one question of the case, with its options as the lettered `Choice` options of `OptionList` (`explanation-card.tsx`, which Lucía's explanation uses too). A wrong option shows its feedback and is locked; the right one shows its feedback, locks them all and offers «Siguiente caso» (in a case with a fix it offers it only once the tasks hold; until then the card says the cause is named and the case closes when it is fixed too) (or «Ver resultado» after the last, which saves the stars as the explanation's «Ver resultado» does). The screen keys it by the case, so each case starts with its question closed, and the next case closes any card open on the map. The result card of a diagnose words its three stars for the cases and lists the cases that were not right at the first try (`cases`: `caseSummary`).
- The notice that a change to the network cleared sends that were not empty (`RESTART_TEXT`) is no longer in these cards: the status bar says it (`src/ui/shell/status/`), as the first line of the registry until the player sends again. Lucía's reply region (`role="status"`, always mounted so it is announced) holds only her answer to a rejected solution, and `CaseCard` carries no `restart`.
- Every message to the player is a `Message` of `@/design`: Lucía's alert (`error`, `role="alert"`, mounted again with each episode), her reply to a rejected solution (an `error` per failing task and one for an intruder, inside the `status` region that is always mounted) and the feedback of an option (`success` under «Correcto.», `error` under «No es eso.»). The guard is a rendered-DOM test, `every warning and error an incident can trigger is drawn by Message and no alert is drawn outside it` in `incident-screen.test.tsx`.
- Not here: the cards of a piece, drawn in its panel (`src/ui/incident/map-cards/`, `src/ui/shell/panel/`), the screen that lays them out (`src/ui/incident/incident-screen.tsx`), what each one shows (`src/game/`) and its Spanish text (`src/copy/`).
- May import: `@/game`, `@/levels`, `@/copy`, `@/design`, other files of `src/ui/` (`@/ui/<file>`, `@/ui/incident/use-focus-on-mount`), and only types from `@/engine`.
