# ui/incident/side-cards

The cards of the side panel of the incident screen, below the map: Lucía's card (tasks, the "Enviar solución" button and her reply, the folded glossary with this incident's new terms first, and hints; the report and the tasks mark each new term with «nuevo» once, at its first appearance on the card, however many times they use it; a diagnose has no tasks or button, and its hints are those of the case), Lucía's question (the incident's own, or «¿Qué pasó?»; `explanation-card.tsx`), the card of the case a diagnose is on (`case-card.tsx`) the clock of the game (`clock-card.tsx`) and the result (`result-card.tsx`). The family of the cards that open beside a piece of the map is `../map-cards/`.

- The case card sits in the side panel, below Lucía's card, in a diagnose incident until the result: it shows the case's report and the tests the origin can send (the list of the tasks, ticked once sent), and, in a case with a fix, a section «Arréglalo para cerrar el caso» with where it is fixed (the title of the card of each table, which the player opens from its piece on the map) and its tasks ticked once they hold, built with the same list as the tests (`TickList`); the primary button «Ya sé la causa» opens the one question of the case, with its options as the lettered `Choice` options of `OptionList` (`explanation-card.tsx`, which Lucía's explanation uses too). A wrong option shows its feedback and is locked; the right one shows its feedback, locks them all and offers «Siguiente caso» (in a case with a fix it offers it only once the tasks hold; until then the card says the cause is named and the case closes when it is fixed too) (or «Ver resultado» after the last, which saves the stars as the explanation's «Ver resultado» does). The screen keys it by the case, so each case starts with its question closed, and the next case closes any card open on the map. The result card of a diagnose words its three stars for the cases and lists the cases that were not right at the first try (`cases`: `caseSummary`).
- When a change to the network clears sends that were not empty (`restartNotice`, `src/game/`), Lucía's card draws a `Message` of variant `note` first inside her reply region (`role="status"`, already mounted, so it is announced) and the case card draws one under the case's report; it names the device and says the tests before were with the network before (`RESTART_TEXT`), and it goes with the next send.
- Every message to the player is a `Message` of `@/design`: Lucía's alert (`error`, `role="alert"`, mounted again with each episode), her reply to a rejected solution (an `error` per failing task and one for an intruder, inside the `status` region that is always mounted) and the feedback of an option (`success` under «Correcto.», `error` under «No es eso.»). The guard is a rendered-DOM test, `every warning and error an incident can trigger is drawn by Message and no alert is drawn outside it` in `incident-screen.test.tsx`.
- The clock card sits below Lucía's card and appears only in an incident with a clock (`clockSteps` is not empty) while it is open (V16 = C). It shows the seconds the game clock has run as «N min SS s» (`CLOCK_TEXT.time`) and one chip for each step of the incident (a `ChipTray` with no `picked`: the steps are actions, announced as plain buttons) that reports the seconds of the step, which the screen turns into `wait`. The time is the game's and no piece's, so neither the terminal nor the cache has wait chips, and waiting keeps the sends and the last answer of the terminal. It has no height cap of its own: it is not a card on the map and the side panel scrolls.
- **The note of the inspection box with no room (task 28 bis, decided 42 and 43 of the plan).** When an open card takes both places of the inspection box, the screen writes a `Message` of variant `note` (`SCREEN_TEXT.noRoomForTheBox`, «La tarjeta ocupa el sitio de la caja de inspección. Ciérrala con «Listo» o haz más grande la ventana.») right under Lucía's card, in the side panel: no card of the map can cover it there. It is not a card and the screen draws it, when `useInspection` says `noRoom`; it stays while the card is open, and the cards below it (clock, case, explanation) move down once. On the 2D map above 900 px the note of the inspect help (`InspectHelp` in `incident-screen.tsx`) comes right after it, so this one is the first under Lucía's card while a card is open. Measured on 2026-10-17 on the build of the worktree, 01-08 with the terminal open: at 1000×700 the note lies at y 639 to 702 of the window, at its foot, and at 1000×600 and 1000×500 it lies below the foot of the panel and shows only when the panel scrolls (backlog J18). The text says «más grande» and not «más ancha» because at 1280×360 what is missing is height.
