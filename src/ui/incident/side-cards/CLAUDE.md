# ui/incident/side-cards

The cards of the side panel of the incident screen, below the map: Lucía's card (tasks, the "Enviar solución" button and her reply, the folded glossary with this incident's new terms first, and hints; the report and the tasks mark each new term with «nuevo» once, at its first appearance on the card, however many times they use it; a diagnose has no tasks or button, and its hints are those of the case), Lucía's question (the incident's own, or «¿Qué pasó?»; `explanation-card.tsx`), the card of the case a diagnose is on (`case-card.tsx`) and the result (`result-card.tsx`). The family of the cards that open beside a piece of the map is `../map-cards/`.

- The case card sits in the side panel, below Lucía's card, in a diagnose incident until the result: it shows the case's report and the tests the origin can send (the list of the tasks, ticked once sent), and the primary button «Ya sé la causa» opens the one question of the case, with its options as the lettered `Choice` options of `OptionList` (`explanation-card.tsx`, which Lucía's explanation uses too). A wrong option shows its feedback and is locked; the right one shows its feedback, locks them all and offers «Siguiente caso» (or «Ver resultado» after the last, which saves the stars as the explanation's «Ver resultado» does). The screen keys it by the case, so each case starts with its question closed, and the next case closes any card open on the map. The result card of a diagnose words its three stars for the cases and lists the cases that were not right at the first try (`cases`: `caseSummary`).
- Every message to the player is a `Message` of `@/design`: Lucía's alert (`error`, `role="alert"`, mounted again with each episode), her reply to a rejected solution (an `error` per failing task and one for an intruder, inside the `status` region that is always mounted) and the feedback of an option (`success` under «Correcto.», `error` under «No es eso.»). The guard is a rendered-DOM test, `every warning and error an incident can trigger is drawn by Message and no alert is drawn outside it` in `incident-screen.test.tsx`.
- Not here: the cards that open beside a piece (`src/ui/incident/map-cards/`), the screen that lays them out (`src/ui/incident/incident-screen.tsx`), what each one shows (`src/game/`) and its Spanish text (`src/copy/`).
