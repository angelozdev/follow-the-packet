# ui/incident/geometry

The browser tests of the incident screen that need a real layout and the real CSS (Tasks M21 and M23 of the 2D plan): what the cards, the inspection boxes, the labels and the packets of the map do. They moved here from `tests/e2e/` (`card-layout.spec.ts`, `chapter-1/chapter-1.spec.ts`, `incidents.spec.ts` and `roadmap.spec.ts`), keeping their titles, and run in `pnpm test:browser`, in headless Chromium, with the whole `IncidentScreen` over the real stylesheet and no network and no build. The folder exists because `src/ui/incident/` holds its 12 of 12 files, and its name says where it began: it also holds what the geometry e2e tests of the pin of the box and of the sends in flight became.

- `card-layout.browser.test.tsx` plays a scene for each kind of card (Lucía, the question before and after answering, the result, a case before and after answering, the terminal, the card of a device, the terminal, the network settings, the services, the tables and the card that classifies as tabs of one card, and a firewall with its tabs) with the mouse of Playwright (`userEvent` of `vitest/browser`) and checks: no card clipped and none over another (`cardProblems`, at 390 px and 1440×900, with the inspection help re-pointed, see below), the last element of each card reached by the wheel alone (`reachWithWheel`, at 390×700 and 1280×720), a card beside a piece inside the map with its foot in reach (`MAP_SCENES` at 1280×360, 1000×700, 700×600 and 844×460, measured right after the click, with the grace of the inspection box held, and at rest, once it has ended), the body of the card that classifies scrolling to its last bin, the inspection box that does not fit scrolling to its last line (and, at 390×844, the pin bringing its holder into view, which is what the 2D map does: a hover never moves the page), the tab of a `Message`, the layer of the labels never moving when a card opens on a narrow map, and the inspection box covering no card of the map (`the inspection box covers no card of the map, right after the click and at rest, in a low or narrow window`: each scene of `MAP_SCENES` with the grace held, `inspectionBoxOverCards` read right after the click, after every frame the page draws (`standStill` with `onFrame`) and again once the grace has passed; it also asserts per window whether the box was on show in some frame at all, `boxShows`, so it cannot pass by never meeting a box, and under 901 px the box is a block under the map, so only 1280×360 and 1000×700 can catch a box over a card). A scene is `{ name, level, reach, last }`; `inTheScene` draws the screen, reaches the scene and measures, in one of three modes (`live`, `right-after-the-click`, `at-rest`).
- `map-boxes.browser.test.tsx` holds what the cards and the boxes cover: in 01-01, 01-02, 01-06, 01-07 and 01-10 at 1280×800 and 1440×900 (the printer, the terminal and the settings card against the pieces of the office, their labels and what the map draws of them), and in 04-01 the labels and stamps (never two overlap, but two stamps of one fan), the card against its Security Group, the inspection box of each of the nine things the player hovers (a describe per window) and the definition of a term; plus the 360 px case: in 01-14 case 4 the drawn shape that the 40 px model box overhangs by 4.0 px (the named exception of `map-sweep.test.ts`) lies inside the drawing.
- `label-width.browser.test.tsx` compares the width of every label of every registered incident, in the state the player starts it in and with every combination of the lock and the alert, with the width the layout holds for it (`labels[id].width` of `layoutMap`): the drawn label is never more than 0.5 px over the model and at most 2 px under it, and the font of the labels advances 0.6 em. The lock is 22 px and the alert 23 px, measured on 2026-10-20 (21.6 and 22.2 px with the fallback monospaced font of the browser of the tests). It lives here and not in `src/scene/map2d/layout/` because it reads the real pieces with `pieceViews` of `@/game`, which a test of `src/scene/` may not import.
- `pinned-box.browser.test.tsx` holds the pin of the inspection box, in 01-08 (the lost ping to 10.0.0.10 of the terminal of `tu-laptop`, with the terminal closed again so the box has its usual place): `a pinned box survives a human-paced trip across another piece and scrolls under the mouse` (one `describe` per window, 1440×900, 1280×800 and 1000×700: the click on the stamp pins the box, the mouse walks 30 steps of 30 ms to the nearest corner of the box over the label of another piece, the straight path or, where it crosses none, a route through the label nearest to it, with the timers held and `passTime` after each `moveTheMouseTo`, so the 900 ms of the trip are the page's and not the machine's; the box is still there and pinned, the wheel brings its last line into view and Escape closes it), `with the keyboard, Enter pins the box and PageDown scrolls it; Escape gives the focus back` (at 1440×900; the timers are held only before Escape, because the only timer it needs is the grace that Escape starts), `a click on a piece that opens a card opens it, pins the piece's box, and a click in the card releases the box` and `a term definition can be reached with the mouse, and Escape closes it` (the mouse crosses the gap in 10 steps of 25 ms, shorter than the grace; it goes back from the definition to its term in one jump, because the definition is portaled and a path in steps would reopen it and hide a bug). At 1440×900 the hand crosses `internet`, so the test asserts that the trip passes over a label of another piece and not which.
- `inspection-beside-cards.browser.test.tsx` holds the side the box takes beside an open card and what it does with no room: `with a card open and room beside it, the box of another piece takes the side the card leaves free` (at 1440×900 the terminal of 01-08 and the card of the firewall of 01-13 dock at the left of the 2D map and leave the right; the card of the printer of 01-01, in the middle, leaves the left) and `with a card open and no room beside it, a hover shows no box and a pinned box is released` (01-08 at 1000×700: no box on hover, the reason written in the side panel and the pinning sentence gone from the help, then a click that shows no box and leaves no pin). A locator by the text of a message needs `{ exact: false }`: the text of the whole element carries the tab «Nota» before the words.
- `sends-in-flight.browser.test.tsx` holds `in 03 the same ping sent three times is one stamp and, on the 2D map, three packets in the air at once, and the first two go when their own trips end`: the real screen of 01-03, three sends of the ping to the printer a second apart on a held clock (`holdTheClock`: `setTimeout`, `clearTimeout` and `Date`, because `useSendsInFlight` arms its timer from `Date.now()`), the `g.map-flight` groups counted after each send and while the time passes: the trip lasts the `dur` of the `animateMotion` of a flight, none ends before it, the first and then the second go when their own trips end, and the last stays, finished and invisible, as does the one stamp.
- The helpers are in `src/testing/`: `browser-play.ts` (the mouse: `openCard`, `openTerminal`, `sendTo`, `sendMessage`, `confirm`, `openByKeyboard`, `moveTheMouseTo`; the waits: `standStill`, which counts frames and calls `onFrame` after each; the grace of the inspection box: `holdTheGrace`, `passTheGrace`, `releaseTheGrace`, fake `setTimeout` and `clearTimeout` only, so frames and observers run as in the page, and `passTime(ms)` for a time of your own; `holdTheClock`, the same with `Date`; the wheel: `reachWithWheel` and `bringTheLastLineIntoView`), `card-boxes.ts` (`cardProblems`, `cardsOutsideTheMap`, `isReachable` and `inspectionBoxOverCards`: they read the page they run in) and `map-boxes.ts` (the boxes of the labels, the stamps, the floor names and the drawings of the pieces).
- A locator by the name of a label of two lines (a Security Group, a firewall) cannot use the plain string: the line break that the label draws with CSS is a space for the engine of Playwright, which runs `userEvent.click`, and the characters `\a` for the engine of the page, which runs `.element()` and `expect.element`. `twoLineName('Security Group', 'servidor2')` is the pattern both match.
- What a test here asserts about the 2D map and the e2e did not: the inspection help is a note in the side panel above 900 px (`.incident__help-line`) and a paragraph under the map at 900 px and under (`.incident__inspection > p`), and `cardProblems` looks at both; the pieces a card must not cover are the drawings of the svg (`g.map-piece`), not a box inferred under each label; two stamps of one fan overlap on purpose (their centres one `FAN_PITCH` apart).
- A test sets its window with `page.viewport` (a `beforeEach` per window, so a title stays whole in a `describe` named after the window) and puts the initial one back in `afterEach`; a test that holds the grace gives it back in the same `afterEach`. No `setTimeout`, no retries, no skips: lint rejects them. While the timers are held, a `expect.element` that does not hold waits for the timeout of the test and not for its own (its retries use the timers the test holds), so the tests of the pin and of the sides give themselves 60 s and not the 300 s of the scene sweeps, and `expect.poll` (which gives up after the 10 s of the project) is what the test of the sends in flight uses.
- Not here: the pure tests of the layout (`map-sweep.test.ts`), the box of the inspection next to the marks and the card docked in the corner and on a phone (`inspection-scroll.browser.test.tsx`, which needs the whole screen too), and the tests that play the registered incidents in jsdom (`incident-screen.test.tsx`, which also holds `with touch, a tap pins the box and a tap outside releases it`).
