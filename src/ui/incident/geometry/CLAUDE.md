# ui/incident/geometry

The browser tests of the incident screen that need a real layout and the real CSS (Tasks M21 and M23 of the 2D plan): what the cards, the inspection boxes, the labels and the packets of the map do. They moved here from `tests/e2e/` (`card-layout.spec.ts`, `chapter-1/chapter-1.spec.ts`, `incidents.spec.ts` and `roadmap.spec.ts`), keeping their titles, and run in `pnpm test:browser`, in headless Chromium, with the whole `IncidentScreen` over the real stylesheet and no network and no build. The folder exists because `src/ui/incident/` holds its 12 of 12 files, and its name says where it began: it also holds what the geometry e2e tests of the pin of the box and of the sends in flight became.

- `card-layout.browser.test.tsx` plays a scene for each kind of panel (Lucía, the question before and after answering, the result, a case before and after answering, the terminal, the card of a device, the network settings and the services of `laptop-lucia` (the sections of «Ajustes»), the tables (the routes of `router-oficina`, the translations and, since 02-11, the records of `servidor-autoritativo` with an alias refused and its reason under the table: `THE_RECORDS`, and, since 02-12, the cache of `servidor-dns`, read only, a minute after a `dig`: `THE_CACHE`), the routes of `router-proveedor`, the fix of the loop (`THE_PROVIDER_ROUTES`), the card of a case that has a fix, with its tasks and its question open, in `SCENES` only, the card that classifies as a ficha of «Preguntas», the clock of the game below the card of Lucía (`THE_CLOCK`, in `SCENES` only, like Lucía's own card), and a firewall with its two tabs of direction; since the first block of chapter 3 the browser (the «Navegador» tab of `tu-laptop`, with a page opened: `THE_BROWSER`), the sites of `servidor-internet` (`THE_SITES`), the terminal with the output of a `curl` sent twice (`THE_CURL_TERMINAL`) and the terminal of laptop-lucia with its url by parts and the order 7 picked (`THE_STRIP`), over `webSendIncident` and `addressByPartsIncident`, whose scenes say it in `incidents`) with the mouse of Playwright (`userEvent` of `vitest/browser`) and checks: no card clipped and none over another (`cardProblems`, at 390 px and 1440×900; a card inside a region that scrolls counts only where the region shows it, and so does a button of the body of the panel), the last element of each panel reached by the wheel alone (`reachWithWheel`, at 390×700 and 1280×720), the panel of every scene of `MAP_SCENES` inside its zone with its foot in view and its body scrolling (`the panel of every scene of the card scenes stays inside the column, its foot in view and its body scrolling, at 1280×600, 1280×720 and 1440×900`: `panelOutsideItsZone`), the body of the card that classifies scrolling to its last bin at 1280×600, the inspection box that does not fit scrolling to its last line (and, at 390×844, the choice bringing the panel into view; a hover never moves the page), the tab of a `Message`, the layer of the labels never moving when a piece is chosen on a narrow map, the panel covering no piece of the map, the inspection box covering no card of the zones of Lucía and of the panel, and, at 390 px, the panel of a server with its line of roles not scrolling sideways with every control within reach. A scene is `{ name, level, incidents?, reach, last }` (`incidents` is the list the screen plays, `INCIDENTS` unless the scene gives its own); `last` is the foot of the panel or, with none, its last control or table, and `inTheScene` draws the screen, reaches the scene and measures, in one of three modes (`live`, `right-after-the-click`, `at-rest`). The panel scrolls its own body only above 900 px and not under a window of about 600 px high (the supported low window is 1280×600), so the scenes that need the body to scroll run there.
- `map-boxes.browser.test.tsx` holds what the cards and the boxes cover: in 01-01, 01-02, 01-06, 01-07 and 01-10 at 1280×800 and 1440×900 (the printer, the terminal and the settings card against the pieces of the office, their labels and what the map draws of them), and in 04-01 the labels and stamps (never two overlap, but two stamps of one fan), the card against its Security Group, the inspection box of each of the nine things the player hovers (a describe per window, 1440×900 and 1920×1080: at 1280×800 the box of the map is smaller than the drawing of 04-01 and the preview covers the two labels that the scroll of the box brings under it, an interim until the camera) and the definition of a term; plus the 360 px case: in 01-14 case 4 the drawn shape that the 40 px model box overhangs by 4.0 px (the named exception of `map-sweep.test.ts`) lies inside the drawing.

The lock is 22 px and the alert 23 px, measured on 2026-10-20 (21.6 and 22.2 px with the fallback monospaced font of the browser of the tests). It lives here and not in `src/scene/layout/` because it reads the real pieces with `pieceViews` of `@/game`, which a test of `src/scene/` may not import.

- `pinned-box.browser.test.tsx` (it keeps the name of the pin, which is gone) holds the choice of a packet or a piece in the panel, in 01-08 (the lost ping to 10.0.0.10 of the terminal of `tu-laptop`, with the terminal closed again so the box has its usual place): `a packet chosen stays in the panel across a human-paced trip over another piece, and its box scrolls under the mouse` (one `describe` per window, 1440×900, 1280×800 and 1000×700: the click on the stamp chooses it, the mouse walks 30 steps of 30 ms to the nearest corner of the panel over the label of another piece, the straight path or, where it crosses none, a route through the label nearest to it, with the timers held and `passTime` after each `moveTheMouseTo`, so the 900 ms of the trip are the page's and not the machine's; the box is still in the panel and, when its body does not fit, the wheel brings its last line into view), `with the keyboard, Enter chooses the packet and PageDown scrolls its box in the panel; Escape gives the focus back and keeps the box` (at 1280×600, where the box does not fit the panel), `a click on a piece that opens a card opens it, shows the piece in the panel, and a click in the card leaves the panel as it is` (the piece and its terminal are now one panel: the click chooses it, and with the keyboard Enter moves the focus to its heading) and `a term definition can be reached with the mouse, and Escape closes it` (the mouse crosses the gap in 10 steps of 25 ms, shorter than the grace; it goes back from the definition to its term in one jump, because the definition is portaled and a path in steps would reopen it and hide a bug). At 1440×900 the hand crosses `internet`, so the test asserts that the trip passes over a label of another piece and not which. A last `describe`, `the «+N» of 02-03 at the desktop window` (Task M26 of the 2D plan), plays the real screen of 02-03: the terminal of `laptop-lucia` sends the four tests that end at her doorstep (the app and the closed port, to her own IP and to 127.0.0.1; the mouse goes away after each address is picked, because the card shrinks under it when the output of the last send goes and a stopped pointer over the `puerto` of a chip would open the tip of the term over the next chip), so the fan of that place draws the two newest stamps and a «+2» and nothing else is on the map. `in 02-03 a fourth stamp at laptop-lucia turns the last casilla into «+2», and hovering it lists the four` (the list is titled «4 sellos juntos» and has one field for each packet, the drawn ones too, the newest first, each with how it ended), `in 02-03 the list of «+2» opens in the panel with the keyboard focus and Escape gives the focus back` (Enter shows it in the panel and the focus goes to its heading; Escape gives it back to the «+2» and keeps the list) and `in 02-03 a tap on «+2» shows the list in the panel and a tap outside leaves it` (the tap is `tapAt`, a touch of the protocol of the browser, so the page sees a touch pointer). They are the three titles of the Task 28 quinquies that the sweep of Task M8 and the component tests did not play on the whole screen.
- `sends-in-flight.browser.test.tsx` holds `in 03 the same ping sent three times is one stamp and, on the 2D map, three packets in the air at once, and the first two go when their own trips end`: the real screen of 01-03, three sends of the ping to the printer a second apart on a held clock (`holdTheClock`: `setTimeout`, `clearTimeout` and `Date`, because `useSendsInFlight` arms its timer from `Date.now()`), the `g.map-flight` groups counted after each send and while the time passes: the trip lasts the `dur` of the `animateMotion` of a flight, none ends before it, the first and then the second go when their own trips end, and the last stays, finished and invisible, as does the one stamp. A second `describe`, `the send by name on the screen of 02-10` (Task 29 of the plan of chapter 2), holds `in 02-10 a ping by name draws the question first and the ping after it, each with its stamp`: the real screen of 02-10 on a held clock, the `ping` sent from the terminal (the name is the only address, so no address chip is picked), the two `g.map-flight` groups in the order question, ping, one stamp for each («Paquete: UDP 51514 → 53 de tu-laptop a servidor-dns…» and «Paquete: ping de tu-laptop a servidor-internet…»), and the timeline of the map paused and put at three instants with `pauseAnimations` and `setCurrentTime`: the question flies alone, the ping still waits while the answer is on its way back, and once it is back the question is over and the ping leaves.
- The helpers are in `src/testing/`: `browser-play.ts` (the mouse: `openCard(piece, tab?)` and `openTerminal(laptop)`, which click the label of the piece on the map and return its panel, on a tab when one is given, `sendTo`, `sendMessage`, `confirm`, `openByKeyboard`, `moveTheMouseTo`; the waits: `standStill`, which counts frames and calls `onFrame` after each; the grace of the inspection box: `holdTheGrace`, `passTheGrace`, `releaseTheGrace`, fake `setTimeout` and `clearTimeout` only, so frames and observers run as in the page, and `passTime(ms)` for a time of your own; `holdTheClock`, the same with `Date`; the wheel: `reachWithWheel` and `bringTheLastLineIntoView`), `card-boxes.ts` (`cardProblems`, `panelOutsideItsZone`, `isReachable` and `inspectionBoxOverCards`: they read the page they run in) and `map-boxes.ts` (the boxes of the labels, the stamps, the floor names and the drawings of the pieces).
- A locator by the name of a label of two lines (a Security Group, a firewall) cannot use the plain string: the line break that the label draws with CSS is a space for the engine of Playwright, which runs `userEvent.click`, and the characters `\a` for the engine of the page, which runs `.element()` and `expect.element`. `twoLineName('Security Group', 'servidor2')` is the pattern both match.
- What a test here asserts about the 2D map and the e2e did not: the inspection help is the note of the panel (`.incident__help-line`), at every width, which no card of the map reaches; the pieces a card must not cover are the drawings of the svg (`g.map-piece`), not a box inferred under each label; two stamps of one fan overlap on purpose (their centres one `FAN_PITCH` apart).
- A test sets its window with `page.viewport` (a `beforeEach` per window, so a title stays whole in a `describe` named after the window) and puts the initial one back in `afterEach`; a test that holds the grace gives it back in the same `afterEach`. No `setTimeout`, no retries, no skips: lint rejects them. While the timers are held, a `expect.element` that does not hold waits for the timeout of the test and not for its own (its retries use the timers the test holds), so the tests of the choice and of the sides give themselves 60 s and not the 300 s of the scene sweeps, and `expect.poll` (which gives up after the 10 s of the project) is what the test of the sends in flight uses.
- Not here: the pure tests of the layout (`map-sweep.test.ts`), the box of the inspection next to the marks, the terminal in the panel and the panel on a phone (`inspection-scroll.browser.test.tsx`, which needs the whole screen too), and the tests that play the registered incidents in jsdom (`incident-screen.test.tsx`, which also holds `with touch, a tap on a stamp shows its box in the panel, which a tap outside leaves where it is`).
