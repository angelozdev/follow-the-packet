# ui/incident/geometry

The browser tests of the geometry of the incident screen (Task M21 of the 2D plan): what a real layout and the real CSS decide about the cards, the boxes and the labels of the map. They moved here from `tests/e2e/` (`card-layout.spec.ts`, `chapter-1/chapter-1.spec.ts`, `incidents.spec.ts` and `roadmap.spec.ts`), keeping their titles, and run in `pnpm test:browser`, in headless Chromium, with the whole `IncidentScreen` over the real stylesheet and no network and no build. The folder exists because `src/ui/incident/` holds its 12 of 12 files.

- `card-layout.browser.test.tsx` plays a scene for each kind of card (Lucía, the question before and after answering, the result, a case before and after answering, the terminal, the card of a device, the terminal, the network settings, the services, the tables and the card that classifies as tabs of one card, and a firewall with its tabs) with the mouse of Playwright (`userEvent` of `vitest/browser`) and checks: no card clipped and none over another (`cardProblems`, at 390 px and 1440×900, with the inspection help re-pointed, see below), the last element of each card reached by the wheel alone (`reachWithWheel`, at 390×700 and 1280×720), a card beside a piece inside the map with its foot in reach (`MAP_SCENES` at 1280×360, 1000×700, 700×600 and 844×460, measured right after the click, with the grace of the inspection box held, and at rest, once it has ended), the body of the card that classifies scrolling to its last bin, the inspection box that does not fit scrolling to its last line (and, at 390×844, the pin bringing its holder into view, which is what the 2D map does: a hover never moves the page), the tab of a `Message`, and the layer of the labels never moving when a card opens on a narrow map. A scene is `{ name, level, reach, last }`; `inTheScene` draws the screen, reaches the scene and measures, in one of three modes (`live`, `right-after-the-click`, `at-rest`).
- `map-boxes.browser.test.tsx` holds what the cards and the boxes cover: in 01-01, 01-02, 01-06, 01-07 and 01-10 at 1280×800 and 1440×900 (the printer, the terminal and the settings card against the pieces of the office, their labels and what the map draws of them), and in 04-01 the labels and stamps (never two overlap, but two stamps of one fan), the card against its Security Group, the inspection box of each of the nine things the player hovers (a describe per window) and the definition of a term; plus the 360 px case: in 01-14 case 4 the drawn shape that the 40 px model box overhangs by 4.0 px (the named exception of `map-sweep.test.ts`) lies inside the drawing.
- `label-width.browser.test.tsx` compares the width of every label of every registered incident, in the state the player starts it in and with every combination of the lock and the alert, with the width the layout holds for it (`labels[id].width` of `layoutMap`): the drawn label is never more than 0.5 px over the model and at most 2 px under it, and the font of the labels advances 0.6 em. The lock is 22 px and the alert 23 px, measured on 2026-10-20 (21.6 and 22.2 px with the fallback monospaced font of the browser of the tests). It lives here and not in `src/scene/map2d/layout/` because it reads the real pieces with `pieceViews` of `@/game`, which a test of `src/scene/` may not import.
- The helpers are in `src/testing/`: `browser-play.ts` (the mouse: `openCard`, `openTerminal`, `sendTo`, `sendMessage`, `confirm`, `openByKeyboard`; the waits: `standStill`, which counts frames; the grace of the inspection box: `holdTheGrace`, `passTheGrace`, `releaseTheGrace`, fake `setTimeout` and `clearTimeout` only, so frames and observers run as in the page; the wheel: `reachWithWheel`), `card-boxes.ts` (`cardProblems`, `cardsOutsideTheMap`, `inspectionBoxOverCards`, `isReachable`: they read the page they run in, and `tests/e2e/card-boxes.ts` passes the last three to `page.evaluate`) and `map-boxes.ts` (the boxes of the labels, the stamps, the floor names and the drawings of the pieces).
- A locator by the name of a label of two lines (a Security Group, a firewall) cannot use the plain string: the line break that the label draws with CSS is a space for the engine of Playwright, which runs `userEvent.click`, and the characters `\a` for the engine of the page, which runs `.element()` and `expect.element`. `twoLineName('Security Group', 'servidor2')` is the pattern both match.
- What a test here asserts about the 2D map and the e2e did not: the inspection help is a note in the side panel above 900 px (`.incident__help-line`) and a paragraph under the map at 900 px and under (`.incident__inspection > p`), and `cardProblems` looks at both; the pieces a card must not cover are the drawings of the svg (`g.map-piece`), not a box inferred under each label; two stamps of one fan overlap on purpose (their centres one `FAN_PITCH` apart).
- A test sets its window with `page.viewport` (a `beforeEach` per window, so a title stays whole in a `describe` named after the window) and puts the initial one back in `afterEach`; a test that holds the grace gives it back in the same `afterEach`. No `setTimeout`, no retries, no skips: lint rejects them.
- Not here: the pure tests of the layout (`map-sweep.test.ts`), the box of the inspection beside a card and on a phone (`inspection-scroll.browser.test.tsx`, which needs the whole screen too), and the tests that play the registered incidents in jsdom (`incident-screen.test.tsx`).
- May import: the same as `src/ui/incident/`, and `@/testing/...` (tests only).
