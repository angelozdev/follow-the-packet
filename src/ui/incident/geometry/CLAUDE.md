# ui/incident/geometry

The browser tests of the incident screen that need a real layout and the real CSS (Tasks M21 and M23 of the 2D plan): what the cards, the inspection boxes, the labels and the packets of the map do. They moved here from `tests/e2e/` (`card-layout.spec.ts`, `chapter-1/chapter-1.spec.ts`, `incidents.spec.ts` and `roadmap.spec.ts`), keeping their titles, and run in `pnpm test:browser`, in headless Chromium, with the whole `IncidentScreen` over the real stylesheet and no network and no build. The folder exists because `src/ui/incident/` holds its 12 of 12 files, and its name says where it began: it also holds what the geometry e2e tests of the pin of the box and of the sends in flight became.

- The helpers are in `src/testing/`: `browser-play.ts` (the mouse: `openCard`, `openTerminal`, `sendTo`, `sendMessage`, `confirm`, `openByKeyboard`, `moveTheMouseTo`; the waits: `standStill`, which counts frames and calls `onFrame` after each; the grace of the inspection box: `holdTheGrace`, `passTheGrace`, `releaseTheGrace`, fake `setTimeout` and `clearTimeout` only, so frames and observers run as in the page, and `passTime(ms)` for a time of your own; `holdTheClock`, the same with `Date`; the wheel: `reachWithWheel` and `bringTheLastLineIntoView`), `card-boxes.ts` (`cardProblems`, `cardsOutsideTheMap`, `isReachable` and `inspectionBoxOverCards`: they read the page they run in) and `map-boxes.ts` (the boxes of the labels, the stamps, the floor names and the drawings of the pieces).
- A locator by the name of a label of two lines (a Security Group, a firewall) cannot use the plain string: the line break that the label draws with CSS is a space for the engine of Playwright, which runs `userEvent.click`, and the characters `\a` for the engine of the page, which runs `.element()` and `expect.element`. `twoLineName('Security Group', 'servidor2')` is the pattern both match.
- What a test here asserts about the 2D map and the e2e did not: the inspection help is a note in the side panel above 900 px (`.incident__help-line`) and a paragraph under the map at 900 px and under (`.incident__inspection > p`), and `cardProblems` looks at both; the pieces a card must not cover are the drawings of the svg (`g.map-piece`), not a box inferred under each label; two stamps of one fan overlap on purpose (their centres one `FAN_PITCH` apart).
- A test sets its window with `page.viewport` (a `beforeEach` per window, so a title stays whole in a `describe` named after the window) and puts the initial one back in `afterEach`; a test that holds the grace gives it back in the same `afterEach`. No `setTimeout`, no retries, no skips: lint rejects them. While the timers are held, a `expect.element` that does not hold waits for the timeout of the test and not for its own (its retries use the timers the test holds), so the tests of the pin and of the sides give themselves 60 s and not the 300 s of the scene sweeps, and `expect.poll` (which gives up after the 10 s of the project) is what the test of the sends in flight uses.
- Not here: the pure tests of the layout (`map-sweep.test.ts`), the box of the inspection next to the marks and the card docked in the corner and on a phone (`inspection-scroll.browser.test.tsx`, which needs the whole screen too), and the tests that play the registered incidents in jsdom (`incident-screen.test.tsx`, which also holds `with touch, a tap pins the box and a tap outside releases it`).
