# ui/incident/map-cards/identify

The card that classifies other devices, drawn in the panel of the piece of its owner, as the ficha «Clasificar» of its tab «Preguntas» (`classify-card.tsx`): the block with its mask, the 32 bits of the IP of the owner (four numbers of eight bits, the first ones the prefix fixes filled), the items still to classify and the two bins.

- It shows what `@/game` computed (`ClassifyCard`) and reports the placement of one item at a time (`onAnswer(deviceId, picks)`, which becomes `answerCard`): it never decides whether an item fits.
- **An item is placed when it is put down, not when "Listo" is pressed** (decided in Task 19). The bins, the line under each item and the outline of the block on the map are all drawn from `Session.answers`, so a draft until "Listo" would leave the model with nothing to draw. This differs from the card of a device (`../identify-card.tsx`), whose picks are a draft until "Listo". The player picks an item up (a `Choice`, pressed while it is held) and presses «Poner aquí» in a bin, which is also how an item moves to the other bin; nothing is dragged, so it works with the keyboard and needs no library. After placing, the focus goes to the heading of the panel (the button that had it is disabled). The frame has no way out of its own: no «Listo», and Escape closes nothing.
- The line of a placed item is a `Message` in a `status` region of its own (`success` when the bin is right, `error` when it is not). The row of bits is `role="img"` per number, named by its eight bits; the cells are hidden from the screen reader because the number beside them says the same thing.
- The item picked up lives above the tabs of the panel (`ClassifyDraft`), so it is kept when the player switches fichas, tabs and pieces.
- Not here: the card of a device with trays of chips (`../identify-card.tsx`), the frame every card of a piece returns (`../card-frame.tsx`), what the card shows (`src/game/view/cards/identify/`).
- May import: `@/game`, `@/copy`, `@/design`, `@/scene`, other files of `src/ui/` (`@/ui/<file>`, `@/ui/incident/map-cards/card-frame`), and only types from `@/engine`.
