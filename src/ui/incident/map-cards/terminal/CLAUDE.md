# ui/incident/map-cards/terminal

The terminal of an origin, drawn in the panel of its piece, in its tab «Terminal» (`terminal-card.tsx`): the trays of chips, the line they make, "Enviar" and, once something was sent with those chips, its output, the «Salida» (`output-card.tsx`): the numbered hops of a traceroute, or the lines an `nc`, a `dig` or a `curl` printed. The command is `command-line.tsx`.

- It shows what `@/game` computed (`TerminalCard`, `TracerouteView`, `ToolOutput`) and reports the player's picks; it never decides what a packet does.
- A terminal whose model carries a `strip` draws it (`web/url-strip.tsx`) between the tray of origins and the others, and its parts are picked in the draft (`TerminalDraft.parts`); the address is the chip those parts stand for.
- **The command and each block of the «Salida» are code on a dark panel (S1, decided 2026-10-25; `DESIGN.md`, «Code of a terminal»).** `CommandLine` draws the line the picks make in a `code` inside `p.command`, from the groups of `commandGroups` (`@/game`): the spaces inside a group are no-break spaces and the line breaks between groups and inside a url, so the text of the page is the line, as it was. The `$ ` is not text: the stylesheet draws it (`.command code::before`, `user-select: none`), so it is not selected or copied, and only before a command a shell runs: the screen says it with the `shell` handler (`isShellCommand`), and the line of a packet made by hand keeps the dark panel with `command--no-prompt`; a screen that gives no `shell` handler gets the prompt. A terminal with no command yet keeps the dashed `slot` with its hint, in the same `p` (`aria-live`). The panels are dark with paper text only; the key line of a `dig` is inverted (`--color-surface` with ink text), and the notes under a block, the gap lines and the count stay in the body font, outside the panel. The tests are `src/ui/catalog/code-style.browser.test.tsx`.
- The «Salida» generalizes the traceroute's: the numbered list (`rules`, label «Salida de traceroute», its notes) is unchanged, and the lines of an `nc` or a `dig` are written as the tool printed them, in `code`, one per line, tabs included (`.output__line`: `white-space: pre-wrap`, `tab-size: 4`, so a long line wraps like in a narrow terminal), under the label «Salida de nc» or «Salida de dig». The notes under it say which figures are examples. It adds no card of its own: it sits in the body of the panel, which caps its height inside its zone and scrolls its body while «Enviar» stays in view (`src/styles.css`, `.incident__panel .chip-card`), so an unfolded `dig` of twenty lines scrolls there and never grows the panel past the column (`an unfolded output in a card on the map stays inside the card, scrolls in its body and keeps the foot in view` in `src/ui/catalog/entities.browser.test.tsx`, which puts the specimen in a column).
- **The fold of the output of a `dig` (V11 = B) is a `Button`, not a `details`.** `DESIGN.md` makes every action a `Button` and the glossary of Lucía, the only `details` of the game, is a disclosure of a list the player reads, not an action on what a tool printed; and the panel has to keep what the player unfolded when it changes around it (the terminal is one tab of the panel of its piece, and Radix mounts only the selected tab), which the open attribute of a `details` loses with its element and a state held by `TerminalCard` does not. So the state is the terminal's draft (`useTerminalDraft`: the chips picked and `unfolded`, folded again when another chip is picked), which the panel keeps above its tabs and above the piece, and the output is controlled (`unfolded`, `onToggle`). `terminalFrame` is the terminal as a frame. The control is the `small` `Button`, with `aria-expanded`, named «Ver las N líneas restantes» or «Ocultar las N líneas restantes» (N, all the lines hidden); the lines it hides are replaced by a mark «⋯ N líneas plegadas» at each place (N, the lines hidden there). An output that has no folded lines has no control: the frame renders the control only when `ToolOutput.folded` is above 0.
- The output is `printed` (an `nc`, a `dig` or a `curl`) next to `route` (a traceroute): `route` keeps the signature it had and a card with no `printed` prints only traceroutes. `printed` gives one block or a list of them, and the card draws one `OutputCard` for each: the screen gives it `toolOutputs`, which for a `curl` is one block for each send of the same chips since the world last changed, each with its title («Envío 1», «Envío 2»), so the player can compare the first body with the second. A titled block shows its title as a caption above its lines (hidden from the screen reader, which hears it in the name of the group: «Salida de curl · Envío 1»); a block with no title, as an `nc` or a `dig` has, is named «Salida de nc» or «Salida de dig» as it always was. One send also has its title, «Envío 1». The fold of a `dig` is the draft's, so it unfolds every block at once.
- Not here: the frame every card returns and the other cards (`src/ui/incident/map-cards/`), the panel that draws them (`src/ui/shell/panel/`), what the terminal shows (`src/game/view/cards/terminal/`), the screen that chooses the piece (`src/ui/incident/incident-screen.tsx`).
