# ui/incident/map-cards/web

What the web adds to the cards of a piece: the tab of the browser of a device (`browser-card.tsx`).

- The browser is a card of the device it runs on, one more tab of the card of its piece (`pieceCard` lists it right after the terminal; it is not a piece of the map). Like every card it is a frame (`browserFrame`) and a card of its own (`BrowserCard`), and its draft, the address picked, is kept above the tabs by `PieceCard` (`useBrowserDraft`), so switching tabs loses neither the address nor what the browser showed for it (that is read from the session, `browserView`).
- It shows what `@/game` computed (`BrowserCard`, `BrowserView`) and reports the player's pick: a `ChipTray` «Dirección» with the whole url of each address, the primary button «Abrir» (disabled until an address is picked; it sends the message `browser` from the origin of the card) and, once that address was opened since the world last changed, «Lo que ve el navegador»: the address in `code` and either the lines of the page as plain text of the card or the error in plain words with a ✕ mark that CSS draws (`.browser__error`). The error is never a `Message`: it is what the network said, not a notice of the game. The group that holds it sits in a polite live region that is always mounted, so the screen reader hears the page or the error appear. The card holds no text of its own: the words come from `BROWSER_TEXT` and the lines of the page from the response. It has no table of what it remembers: that comes with the first cookie (chapter 3, subchapter 06).
- The «Sitios» card of a web server needs no file here: it is the `TableCard` of `table-card.tsx` (a table of kind `sites`), with its two trays and its refusal under the table. Its tests are here with the other tests of the web (`web-cards.test.tsx`: the browser tab, the sites card and the output of a `curl`, each in a `describe`, over one `Hosted` piece card of `webSendIncident`): a site needs both trays to be placed, a refused site is an error `Message` under the table in a `role="status"` region that was already there, and the focus goes back to the title after adding, as in the records of chapter 2. A piece with a single card draws no tab bar.
- The repeated blocks of the output of a `curl` are tested there too, though the card is the terminal's (`terminal/`); `send-rules.browser.test.tsx` holds the test of the rules card of a send incident, which needs the real map.
- Not here: the chip card and the other cards (`src/ui/incident/map-cards/`), the terminal (`src/ui/incident/map-cards/terminal/`), what each card shows (`src/game/view/cards/web/`), the Spanish text (`src/copy/web/`).
