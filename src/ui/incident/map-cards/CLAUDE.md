# ui/incident/map-cards

The cards that open beside a piece of the map, all built from `chip-card.tsx` (`ChipCard`: the dialog with its title on the stripe, with the icon its caller gives it, what the caller puts in it, and, in a foot of their own, the caller's `actions` (the terminal's «Enviar») and Escape's twin «Listo», which closes the card unless the caller gives it its own way out with `onDone`; a docked card that outgrows its free height scrolls only what is above the foot, so «Enviar» and «Listo» stay in view; `trayChips`: the card's chips, with their terms, for the `ChipTray` of `@/design`): the task card of a firewall (`task-card.tsx`, rules from Type and Source chips, or Type and Destination chips for the outbound ones), the terminal of an origin (`terminal/terminal-card.tsx`, a packet from origin, address and message chips, its line and "Enviar") the card of a device in an identify incident (`identify-card.tsx`, a tray of chips for each thing to say about it) and the «Ajustes de red» card of a device (`settings-card.tsx`, a tray of chips for each setting saved on it).

- A card shows what `@/game` computed (`FirewallCard`, `TerminalCard`, `TracerouteView`) and reports the player's picks; it never decides what a rule or a packet does.
- The task card of a firewall shows its inbound list as it always did. When `FirewallCard` carries an `outbound` list (and the incident gives the Destination chips and the outbound handlers), it shows two `Tabs` of `@/design`, the inbound one first and selected, each with its list, its slot and its Type tray plus Source or Destination tray, built by one panel for both directions. `Tabs` mounts only the selected panel, so the card keeps what was picked above the tabs, per direction: switching tabs loses nothing and a Type picked in one tab never completes a rule in the other. The inbound handlers (`onPlace`, `onRemove`) are called as before; the outbound ones come in the `outbound` prop.
- The terminal prints a traceroute under its command, as a numbered list in the list of the task card (the class `rules`, one line per hop with the time to the right) and, under it, the notes in `muted` text. It reuses what the task card already has and adds no element.
- Both are a `Card` of `@/design` whose stripe holds the card's `h2` (the focus lands on it) and the icon of what it is about (the firewall's own icon, from the `kind` of the card, or `CardIcon` for the terminal), with the grid `chip-card` inside, and use the class `slot` and the `Button`, `ChipTray` and `Choice` of `@/design` («×», «Listo» and «Enviar», which stays disabled until the line is complete; the trays; the chip given back from the slot). A new card that picks values with chips uses the same tray, and reuses them before adding any.
- The identify card keeps the player's picks as a draft in its own state, starting from the answer saved: picking a chip only changes the draft and hides the lines. «Listo» is its own way out (`onDone`): it reports the picks of every tray (`onAnswer`, which becomes `answerCard` in the session) and shows, under each tray, the `lines` of the chip picked (`role="status"`, class `feedback`, or `feedback feedback--wrong` for a chip that is not `correct`); with no line to show it closes, and with the lines showing it closes. Escape closes it at any time and reports nothing, so reopening the card shows the answer saved. A yes or no (`segmented`) is the `segmented` variant of `ChipTray`. The card holds no text of its own: the title, the question, the tray names and the lines come from `@/game`.
- The settings card has no draft: picking a chip is a change of the device, reported at once (`onPick`, which becomes `setSetting` in the session), and the tray shows the chip `@/game` says matches the world. It is a `ChipCard` with the `settings` icon of `CardIcon` and one `ChipTray` per setting, named by its term; «Listo» and Escape only close it.
- Not here: the screen that opens a card (`src/ui/incident/incident-screen.tsx`), the anchor that keeps it beside its piece (`src/scene/overlay/`).
- May import: `@/game`, `@/levels`, `@/copy`, `@/design`, other files of `src/ui/` (`@/ui/<file>`, `@/ui/incident/use-focus-on-mount`), and only types from `@/engine`.
