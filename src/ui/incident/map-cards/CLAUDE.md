# ui/incident/map-cards

The cards that open beside a piece of the map, all built from `chip-card.tsx` (`ChipCard`: the dialog with its title on the stripe, with the icon its caller gives it, what the caller puts in it, and, in a foot of their own, the caller's `actions` (the terminal's «Enviar») and Escape's twin «Listo», which closes the card unless the caller gives it its own way out with `onDone`; a docked card that outgrows its free height scrolls only what is above the foot, so «Enviar» and «Listo» stay in view; `trayChips`: the card's chips, with their terms, for the `ChipTray` of `@/design`): the task card of a firewall (`task-card.tsx`, rules from Type and Source chips, or Type and Destination chips for the outbound ones), the terminal of an origin (`terminal/terminal-card.tsx`, a packet from origin, address and message chips, its line and "Enviar") the card of a device in an identify incident (`identify-card.tsx`, a tray of chips for each thing to say about it) the «Ajustes de red» and «Servicios» cards of a device (`settings-card.tsx`, a tray of chips for each setting saved on it), the tables of a piece (`table-card.tsx`), the card that classifies (`identify/classify-card.tsx`, with its own guide), the tab of the browser (`web/browser-card.tsx`, with its own guide) and the card a piece opens from its one button (`piece-card.tsx`).

- A card shows what `@/game` computed (`FirewallCard`, `TerminalCard`, `TracerouteView`) and reports the player's picks; it never decides what a rule or a packet does.
- The task card of a firewall shows its inbound list as it always did, and the screen opens it for any firewall whose incident offers rule chips for it (`ruleChipsOf`): every firewall of a filter incident, and in a send incident only the one its `send.rules` names, with the Type and Source chips of that card and no outbound list. When `FirewallCard` carries an `outbound` list (and the incident gives the Destination chips and the outbound handlers), it shows two `Tabs` of `@/design`, the inbound one first and selected, each with its list, its slot and its Type tray plus Source or Destination tray, built by one panel for both directions. `Tabs` mounts only the selected panel, so the card keeps what was picked above the tabs, per direction: switching tabs loses nothing and a Type picked in one tab never completes a rule in the other. The inbound handlers (`onPlace`, `onRemove`) are called as before; the outbound ones come in the `outbound` prop.
- The terminal prints what was sent under its command, in its «Salida» (`terminal/output-card.tsx`, see its guide): a traceroute as a numbered list in the list of the task card (the class `rules`, one line per hop with the time to the right), above it the reason the sender could not send it, when that is the case, as a `warning` `Message`, and, under it, the notes in `muted` text; an `nc`, a `dig` or a `curl` as the lines the tool printed, in `code` (the class `output`), with the output of a `dig` folded behind a `small` `Button` and, for a `curl`, one block for each send of the same chips, each under its title («Envío 1», «Envío 2»). It adds no card: the body of the terminal card scrolls it.
- Both are a `Card` of `@/design` whose stripe holds the card's `h2` (the focus lands on it) and the icon of what it is about (the firewall's own icon, from the `kind` of the card, or `CardIcon` for the terminal), with the grid `chip-card` inside, and use the class `slot` and the `Button`, `ChipTray` and `Choice` of `@/design` («×», «Listo» and «Enviar», which stays disabled until the line is complete; the trays; the chip given back from the slot). A new card that picks values with chips uses the same tray, and reuses them before adding any.
- **Every card is a frame and a card of its own.** A card is written as a function that returns its frame (`CardFrame`: title, icon, body, foot actions and, when it has its own way out, what "Listo" does) from its model and its draft, plus a component that draws that frame in a `ChipCard` (`FramedCard`). `TerminalCard`, `IdentifyCard`, `SettingsCard`, `ServicesCard`, `TableCard` and `ClassifyCard` are those components, and are what a piece with a single card opens. The same frames are what the tabs of a piece show.
- **The card of a piece (`piece-card.tsx`).** A piece with several cards opens one `ChipCard` whose body is the `Tabs` of `@/design`, one tab per card of the model (`pieceCard`), opening on the tab the model says; the title, the icon and the foot ("Enviar", "Agregar", "Listo") are those of the tab chosen, so the name of the dialog follows the tab («Terminal de tu-laptop», «Ajustes de red de tu-laptop»). `Tabs` mounts only the panel chosen, so what the player has half done lives in the `PieceCard`, above the tabs, as the draft of each kind of card (`useTerminalDraft`, `useBrowserDraft`, `useIdentifyDraft`, `useClassifyDraft`, `useEntryDrafts`) and is not lost when the tab changes. Moving the card to the piece of another origin keeps the drafts and returns to the first tab. A piece with one card shows no tabs. `pieceHandlers` wires what the cards ask to the reducer of the session.
- The sites of a web server (`TableCard` of kind `sites`, tab «Sitios» of its piece) are drawn by the same table card: two trays (name, what it serves), «Agregar sitio» and the refusal of the engine under the table. The tab of the browser of a device (`web/browser-card.tsx`, from `browserCard` of `@/game`, see its guide) is a card of the same frame; its draft, the address picked, lives above the tabs too (`useBrowserDraft`).
- The table card (`table-card.tsx`) draws the `Table` of `@/design` from a `TableCard`: its rows with the one in use marked, a muted line when there is one, and, if the player edits it, a small button on each row that can be taken away, the error `Message` of a refused entry under the table inside a `role="status"` region that is mounted before the message (so the screen reader hears it appear), the trays of the entry and a button («Agregar ruta», «Agregar registro») that is enabled once every tray has a pick and places the entry. After adding or removing, the focus goes to the title, because the button that had it is gone or disabled. A table the player does not edit has no trays, no buttons and no status region.
- The identify card keeps the player's picks as a draft in its own state, starting from the answer saved: picking a chip only changes the draft and hides the lines. «Listo» is its own way out (`onDone`): it reports the picks of every tray (`onAnswer`, which becomes `answerCard` in the session) and shows, under each tray, the `lines` of the chip picked (a `Message` of `@/design` with `role="status"`: `success` for a chip that is `correct` and `error` for one that is not); with no line to show it closes, and with the lines showing it closes. Escape closes it at any time and reports nothing, so reopening the card shows the answer saved. A yes or no (`segmented`) is the `segmented` variant of `ChipTray`. The card holds no text of its own: the title, the question, the tray names and the lines come from `@/game`.
- The settings card has no draft: picking a chip is a change of the device, reported at once (`onPick`, which becomes `setSetting` in the session), and the tray shows the chip `@/game` says matches the world. It is a `ChipCard` with the `settings` icon of `CardIcon` and one `ChipTray` per setting, named by its term; «Listo» and Escape only close it.
- Every card here is a card on the map: the anchor caps its height with the map and its body scrolls, with the foot in view (`src/styles.css`, `.map-card`), whatever it holds (a long table, a classify card, the tabs of a piece).
- Not here: the screen that opens a card (`src/ui/incident/incident-screen.tsx`), the anchor that keeps it beside its piece (`src/scene/overlay/`).
- May import: `@/game`, `@/levels`, `@/copy`, `@/design`, other files of `src/ui/` (`@/ui/<file>`, `@/ui/incident/use-focus-on-mount`), and only types from `@/engine`.
