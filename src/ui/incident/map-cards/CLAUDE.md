# ui/incident/map-cards

The cards of a piece, drawn in the panel (`src/ui/shell/panel/`): the rules of a firewall (`task-card.tsx`, rules from Type and Source chips, or Type and Destination chips for the outbound ones), the terminal of an origin (`terminal/terminal-card.tsx`, a packet from origin, address and message chips, its line and "Enviar"), the questions of a device in an identify incident (`identify-card.tsx`, a tray of chips for each thing to say about it), the «Ajustes de red» and «Servicios» of a device (`settings-card.tsx`, a tray of chips for each setting saved on it), the tables of a piece (`table-card.tsx`), what classifies (`identify/classify-card.tsx`, with its own guide) and the browser (`web/browser-card.tsx`, with its own guide). They no longer open beside a piece on the map: each is a function that returns its frame, and the panel decides in which tab and under which heading it goes. `card-frame.tsx` has the type of a frame (`CardFrame`: body, foot actions and, when it has its own way out, what "Listo" does) and `trayChips` (the card's chips, with their terms, for the `ChipTray` of `@/design`), and `use-drafts-by-key.ts` keeps one draft for each key.

- A card shows what `@/game` computed (`FirewallCard`, `TerminalCard`, `TracerouteView`) and reports the player's picks; it never decides what a rule or a packet does.
- **Every card is a frame.** A card is written as a function that returns its frame from its model and its draft (`terminalFrame`, `browserFrame`, `identifyFrame`, `classifyFrame`, `settingsFrame`, `servicesFrame`, `tableFrame`, `rulesFrame`); the panel draws the body in the tab or ficha the card belongs to and the foot of the one that is chosen. A card has no component of its own and no dialog. What the player has half done is the draft the caller gives (`useTerminalDraft`, `useBrowserDraft`, `useIdentifyDrafts`, `useClassifyDraft`, `useEntryDrafts`, `useRuleDrafts`), kept above the tabs and above the piece, so none of it is lost when the tab, the ficha or the piece changes.
- The rules of a firewall are the frame `rulesFrame`: the inbound list as it always was, with the Type and Source chips of its card and no outbound list unless `FirewallCard` carries an `outbound` list (the ladder has opened the outbound rules of its kind and the incident gives the Destination chips); then the frame shows two `Tabs` of `@/design`, of the `underline` variant because they sit inside the tab «Tablas», named `TASK_CARD_TEXT.tabsName`, the inbound one first, each with its list, its slot and its Type tray plus Source or Destination tray, built by one panel for both directions. The slots of each direction and the direction chosen are the draft of the firewall (`RuleDraft`), so switching direction loses nothing, a Type picked in one never completes a rule in the other and a chip left in a slot is still there when the player comes back to the piece. The handlers (`RuleHandlers`) place and take away a rule, inbound and outbound.
- The terminal prints what was sent under its command, in its «Salida» (`terminal/output-card.tsx`, see its guide): a traceroute as a numbered list in the list of the rules (the class `rules`, one line per hop with the time to the right), above it the reason the sender could not send it, when that is the case, as a `warning` `Message`, and, under it, the notes in `muted` text; an `nc`, a `dig` or a `curl` as the lines the tool printed, in `code` (the class `output`), with the output of a `dig` folded behind a `small` `Button` and, for a `curl`, one block for each send of the same chips, each under its title («Envío 1», «Envío 2»). It adds no card: the body of the panel scrolls it.
- The terminal and the rules use the class `slot` (the terminal, for its command, the class `command`: a dark panel with a `$ `) and the `Button`, `ChipTray` and `Choice` of `@/design` («×», «Listo» and «Enviar», which stays disabled until the line is complete; the trays; the chip given back from the slot). A new card that picks values with chips uses the same tray, and reuses them before adding any.
- The sites of a web server (`TableCard` of kind `sites`, ficha «Sitios» of its piece) are drawn by the same table frame: two trays (name, what it serves), «Agregar sitio» and the refusal of the engine under the table. The browser of a device (`web/browser-card.tsx`, from `browserCard` of `@/game`, see its guide) is a frame of the same kind; its draft, the address picked, lives above the tabs too (`useBrowserDraft`).
- The table frame (`table-card.tsx`) draws the `Table` of `@/design` from a `TableCard`: its rows with the one in use marked, a muted line when there is one, and, if the player edits it, a small button on each row that can be taken away, the error `Message` of a refused entry under the table inside a `role="status"` region that is mounted before the message (so the screen reader hears it appear), the trays of the entry and a button («Agregar ruta», «Agregar registro») that is enabled once every tray has a pick and places the entry. After adding or removing, the focus goes to the heading of the panel (`refocus`), because the button that had it is gone or disabled. A table the player does not edit has no trays, no buttons and no status region.
- The questions of a device keep the player's picks as a draft of the device (`useIdentifyDrafts`), starting from the answer saved: picking a chip only changes the draft and hides the lines. «Listo» is its own way out (`onDone`): it reports the picks of every tray (`onAnswer`, which becomes `answerCard` in the session) and shows, under each tray, the `lines` of the chip picked (a `Message` of `@/design` with `role="status"`: `success` for a chip that is `correct` and `error` for one that is not); with no line to show, or with the lines showing, it hides them and calls `onClose`, which the screen makes the release of the piece chosen. A yes or no (`segmented`) is the `segmented` variant of `ChipTray`. The frame holds no text of its own: the question, the tray names and the lines come from `@/game`.
- The settings frame has no draft: picking a chip is a change of the device, reported at once (`onPick`, which becomes `setSetting` in the session), and the tray shows the chip `@/game` says matches the world; it has no way out of its own.
- A piece that is a server says its roles in a line at the top of its panel, `p.chip-card__roles` (`Papel: …` or `Papeles: …`, from `Panel.roles`), drawn once by the panel whatever its tabs.
- Not here: the panel that draws the frames (`src/ui/shell/panel/`), the screen that chooses a piece (`src/ui/incident/incident-screen.tsx`).
- May import: `@/game`, `@/levels`, `@/copy`, `@/design`, other files of `src/ui/` (`@/ui/<file>`), and only types from `@/engine`.
