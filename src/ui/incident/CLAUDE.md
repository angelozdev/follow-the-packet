# ui/incident

The incident screen: the map with the cards that open beside a piece of it (`map-cards/`: the task card of a firewall, the terminal of an origin (`map-cards/terminal/`) and the card of a device in an identify incident), the inspection box, and the side panel (`side-cards/`: Lucía's card with her question, the card of the case a diagnose is on and the result). The screen joins them, decides which card opens (a firewall its task card, every other piece its `PieceCard`, which draws one card with a tab for each card the piece has) and keeps the hooks they share (`use-focus-on-mount.ts`, `use-inspection.ts`).

- The screen decides which card opens: a firewall opens its task card; every other piece opens its `PieceCard` (`map-cards/piece-card.tsx`), whose model `pieceCard` lists the cards it has: an origin its terminal, a device with a card in an identify incident that card (keyed by its device, so the picks of one never pass to another), the owner of a card that classifies that card, a device its «Ajustes de red» and «Servicios», a router its tables, a server of names its records and its cache. Where the card sits is the scene's rule (`cardPlacement` and `terminalSide` in `src/scene/layout.ts`): the terminal opens on the free side of its origin and, when neither side is free (an origin drawn outside the office, to the left of the row), docks in the bottom left corner of the map, so it covers no other piece's label or stamp: when what it shows outgrows the free height under the pieces (a traceroute listed, a table), its body scrolls. Every card that opens beside a piece is a `ChipCard` inside the anchor that caps its height with the map and scrolls its body, so the tables, the card with tabs and the one that classifies follow the same rule as the terminal; the clock card is not one of them, it sits in the side panel.
- Until a piece or a stamp is inspected, the inspection area shows the inspect help and a legend of the stamps (✓ arrived, ✕ stopped, ? lost, ! a warning appeared) and the lock, in words that need no term of any chapter. The double ring of an intruder's stamp is not in it: the legend names the stamps that have a mark of their own.
- Every card wears the stripe of style B (`DESIGN.md`, "Style B"): Lucía's card has its title on the stripe with her role at its right; the question, the result and the inspection box have their type on the stripe and their heading under it.
- The glossary shows the icon of each term that names an entity, and the inspection box shows the icon of what it inspects in its heading (`EntityIcon` from `@/scene`). A field of the inspection box whose value is one short line sits beside its label (a wrapping flex row; a value that does not fit, or a field with several lines, goes under the label), so the box stays low (a field's `notes`, such as the one about the header outside the office, are drawn as muted lines under its values): it is anchored at the bottom right of the map (at 900 px and under it is not drawn over the map: it is a block of its own between the map and the side panel, so a card docked in the map's corner never covers it), and the row of the public IP (D-5) made the box of a VPC server cover the label «Security Group servidor2» in 04-01 at 1440×900. The box never grows over a label, a stamp or a floor name of the map: `use-inspection.ts` caps its height, each time it inspects another piece and before it is drawn, with the room from its bottom edge up to the lowest of those marks that lies over it (`inspection-room.ts`, a gap of 8 px, a floor of 120 px; `MAP_MARKS` in `@/scene` names what counts), written as `--inspection-room` on the holder; the CSS takes the smaller of that and 45 % of the map. Nothing in `layout.ts` reserves room for the box, so this cap is what keeps it clear: a box that lists one more field (04-01 lists «Servicios» once `service` is known) scrolls instead of covering the label «Security Group servidor2». The map is measured when the box opens, not on every frame: a player who pans or zooms the map moves the labels under the box as they choose. When the text of the box does not fit its height (the smaller of that cap and 45 % of the map, 45vh under 900 px), its body scrolls inside the box and its band stays: the box is hover-driven, so `use-inspection.ts` keeps it for 400 ms after the pointer leaves the piece (time to cross to it) and for as long as the pointer is over it, and the box takes the mouse only while it scrolls (`data-scrolls` on its holder, set when the body has more text than it shows), so a box that fits never stands between the pointer and the label under it. Its scrollbar is always drawn, so a text cut at the bottom shows there is more.
- State lives in the session reducer of `@/game`, including the game clock (`Session.clock`, which only the player's `wait` moves; the clock card of `side-cards/` shows it and offers the steps). What the player has half done in a card (the chips of a terminal, the trays of an entry, the item picked up to classify) is the card's own state, kept above its tabs when the piece has several cards.
- Effects live in `use-*.ts` hooks next to their first consumer.
- Not here: session state and what the panels show (`src/game/`), the map and its labels (`src/scene/`), visible Spanish text (`src/copy/`).
- May import: `@/game`, `@/scene`, `@/storage`, `@/levels`, `@/copy`, `@/design`, other files of `src/ui/` (`@/ui/<file>`), and only types from `@/engine`.
