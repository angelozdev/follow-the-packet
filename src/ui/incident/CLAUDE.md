# ui/incident

The incident screen: the strips across the top and the bottom (`src/ui/shell/strip/`), the zone of Lucía at the left (`src/ui/shell/lucia/`) with her cards (`side-cards/`: Lucía's card with her question, the card of the case a diagnose is on and the result), the map in its box with the inspection box that previews over it, the panel of what the player chose at the right (`src/ui/shell/panel/`, which draws the cards of `map-cards/`: the rules of a firewall, the terminal of an origin (`map-cards/terminal/`), the questions of a device in an identify incident and the rest, each as a part of a tab of the panel of its piece). The screen joins them, gives the panel the choice (`useSelection`) and the choice of tab and ficha (`usePanelChoice`, which «Ver fila en la tabla de rutas» of the newest line sets from the screen), gives the bottom strip fixed at the bottom of the window (`src/ui/shell/strip/`, with the newest line and the registry of `src/ui/shell/status/` the bar of systems of `src/ui/shell/systems/`, whose buttons `useSystems` builds from the session, the choice, the choice of tab and the state of the window, and the system window of `src/ui/shell/windows/`, whose state `useSystemWindow` the screen holds and which it marks the zones `inert` for) the lines of the registry (`registryLines`, newest first) and keeps the hooks they share (`use-focus-on-mount.ts`, `use-inspection.ts`).

- No card opens beside a piece: choosing one (a click, a tap, Enter or Space) draws its panel in its zone, whatever the piece, and `ShellPanel` (`src/ui/shell/panel/`) says what the piece offers: the tabs `panelOf` of `@/game` lists in the fixed order, with the drafts of the terminal, the browser, the tables, the rules and the questions above them, so the screen keeps no card state. A firewall's rules are offered when its incident gives rule chips for it (`ruleChipsOf`: a filter incident for every firewall, a send incident for the firewall of its `send.rules`) and the ladder lets the player set it; a locked piece, and every piece once the incident is over, has only «Estado». The terminal moves the panel to the piece of another origin (`selection.choosePiece`) and keeps the terminal in front. The clock card is not in the panel, it sits in the zone of Lucía.
- Until a piece or a stamp is chosen, the panel (`src/ui/shell/`) is the inspect help (`InspectHelp`, a `Message` of variant `note` in two lines: what hovering or focusing a piece or a stamp does with what a click, a tap, Enter or Space does (`SCREEN_TEXT.inspectClickHelp`), and the legend of the stamps (✓ arrived, ✕ stopped, ? lost, ! a warning appeared) and the lock, in words that need no term of any chapter). The double ring of an intruder's stamp is not in it: the legend names the stamps that have a mark of their own. The help is at every width: Decision D of the student (2026-10-19) took it out from over the map above 900 px, because it covered labels, pieces and stamps there, and the panel made it the note at 900 px and under too, so the holder of the box over the map stays empty until a piece or a stamp is hovered or focused, because `use-inspection.ts` and the box live in it. The note is the panel, a direct child of its zone, and it stays while a box previews. The browser tests of `inspection-scroll.browser.test.tsx` hold the 2D layout: in every incident at 1280×800, 1440×900 and 1280×720 the note meets no mark and leaves the map, at 900 and 901 px it is in the panel and not in the holder (after the box of the map at 900 px, beside it at 901 px).
- Every card wears the stripe of style B (`DESIGN.md`, "Style B"): Lucía's card has its title on the stripe with her role at its right; the question, the result and the inspection box have their type on the stripe and their heading under it (the box of a packet in the panel has a small «Cerrar» button at the right of its stripe; the panel of a piece has its icon and its name on the stripe and a small X at its right).
- The glossary shows the icon of each term that names an entity, and the inspection box shows the icon of what it inspects in its heading (`EntityIcon` from `@/scene`), except for the list of the stamps of a place, which is no entity (`Inspection.entity` is absent) and has no icon; its fields are keyed by position, because two packets of a list can share a label. A field of the box that is an HTTP message (`InspectionField.message`: the request and the response) is one block of code on a light panel (`InspectionFields`, `.output--message`: `--color-shade` fill, a `--stroke-thin` border in `--color-ink-soft`, mono lines with the hanging indent of the terminal; its notes stay under it in the body font) whatever its length, and its lines pass `ips={false}`. A field of the inspection box whose value is one short line sits beside its label (a wrapping flex row; a value that does not fit, or a field with several lines, goes under the label), so the box stays low (a field's `notes`, such as the one about the header outside the office, are drawn as muted lines under its values): it opens at the bottom right of the map and steps aside to the bottom left when a mark of the map stands in that place (the next bullet; at 900 px and under it is not drawn over the map: it is a block of its own between the map and the panel), and the row of the public IP (D-5) made the box of a VPC server cover the label «Security Group servidor2» in 04-01 at 1440×900. The box never grows over a label, a stamp or a floor name of the map: `use-inspection.ts` caps its height, each time it inspects another piece and before it is drawn, with the room from its bottom edge up to the lowest of those marks that lies over it (`inspection-room.ts`, a gap of 8 px, a floor of 120 px; `MAP_MARKS` in `@/scene` names what counts), written as `--inspection-room` on the holder; the CSS takes the smaller of that and 45 % of the map. Nothing in the layout of the map reserves room for the box, so this cap is what keeps it clear: a box that lists one more field (04-01 lists «Servicios» once `service` is known) scrolls instead of covering the label «Security Group servidor2». The marks of the map are measured when the box opens or changes place, not on every frame (the next bullet). When the text of the box does not fit its height (the smaller of that cap and 45 % of the map, 45vh under 900 px), its body scrolls inside the box and its band stays: the box is a preview while the pointer or the focus is on a piece or a stamp, and it never pins: a click, a tap, Enter or Space chooses the piece or the stamp, and the panel shows it (`src/ui/shell/`; a preview of the key that is chosen stays drawn, hidden from assistive technology with `aria-hidden` on its holder, because the panel says the same). `use-inspection.ts` is the box of `useFloatingBox` (`src/ui/floating-box/`) over the inspections of the session: a preview stays 400 ms after the pointer leaves the piece (time to cross to it) and for as long as the pointer is over it, and a touch never previews. A preview goes when its piece no longer has an inspection. The choice is the screen's, not the box's: a choice made with the keyboard moves the focus to the heading of the panel (`tabIndex={-1}`, from where the arrow keys scroll its body; Shift+Tab stops first on the scrolling body, which Chrome makes a Tab stop, and then reaches «Cerrar» in the box of a packet or the X of a piece). That decision is made once, when the click happens, and used once, so a later change of phase never moves the focus. The box takes the mouse only while it scrolls (`data-scrolls` on its holder, set when the body has more text than it shows), so a box that fits never stands between the pointer and the label under it. Its scrollbar is always drawn, so a text cut at the bottom shows there is more.
- **The inspection box previews and covers no mark of the map.** Above 900 px it has two places, the bottom right corner of the map, where it opens, and the bottom left one (`data-place` on the holder; the CSS of the left place is inside `@media (min-width: 901px)`, because a rule with an attribute outweighs the plain class of the 900 px block and would win there). `inspectionPlace` (`inspection-place.ts`, pure) picks it from the marks of the map (the labels, stamps and floor names that lie inside the map, `MAP_MARKS`): the place with no mark in the bottom `MIN_ROOM` (120 px) of the box wins, because the box is never shorter than that and covers such a mark whatever it shows; when the marks cover both places, or none, the place the box already has wins, and a box that opens takes the right one. A mark higher than that is left to the height of the box (`inspectionRoom`, measured at the place chosen), which is capped by the marks above it. `use-inspection.ts` reads the marks from the DOM, in a layout effect each time the box shows another piece and before the browser draws, and writes `data-place` and `--inspection-room` on the holder; it holds no observer of the map, because nothing but the preview floats over it. Under 901 px the holder is not over the map, which the hook sees by comparing rectangles, and none of this applies. The panel lies under the map there, on a phone usually below the fold (the panel of 04-01 at 390×844 starts under the window), so `use-selection.ts` brings it into view with `scrollIntoView({ block: 'nearest' })`, the least the player has to scroll, when the player chooses a piece or a stamp (a click, a tap, Enter or Space; never a hover, so a hover scrolls nothing). It does not put the page back when the choice changes: the player may have scrolled since.
- State lives in the session reducer of `@/game`, including the game clock (`Session.clock`, which only the player's `wait` moves; the clock card of `side-cards/` shows it and offers the steps). What the player has half done in the panel (the chips of a terminal, the address of the browser, the trays of an entry, the slots of the rules of a firewall, the picks of the questions of a device, the item picked up to classify) and the tab and ficha chosen on each piece are the state of the shell (`src/ui/shell/panel/`), kept above the tabs and above the piece, so none is lost when the player changes tab, ficha or piece. A diagnose starts them again with each case (the panel is keyed by the case).
- The screen gives the map `drawnSends(session.sent)` (a `useMemo` of `session.sent`): the last send of each combination, and before each send by name its question as a run of its own, with the packet that asked marked to wait for it (`after`), so the question has its stamp, its box and its place on the map. `map-sweep.test.ts` replays the sends by name of `namesSendIncident` in six worlds (the resolver answers, an authoritative server fails, an address with no resolver, an address nobody has, a firewall that stops the question, a hosts file) at seven widths; the registered incidents 02-10 to 02-14 send by name too, and the first `describe` walks them like every other incident. At 900 px and under `main.incident` is a flex column (the strip, Lucía, the map, the box that previews, the panel and the bottom strip last, sticky at the bottom of the window), so each takes the height of what it holds; above 900 px it is a grid of three rows (the strip, the zones and the bottom strip) and three columns (Lucía, the box of the map and the panel). The screen passes the scene the terms the player knows (`known`, from the session), which only the 2D map reads: a group names its block only once `cidr` is known, and what inspects the list of a «+N» (`inspectStampsTrigger`, `packetIds => trigger(PIECE_ID.stamps(packetIds))`), because the 2D scene draws the «+N» and cannot import `@/game`.
- The idle help of the box, left in the holder over the bottom right of the map, covered labels and pieces of the 2D map in some incidents (measured on 2026-10-19 at scroll top: 5 of the 25 incidents at 1280×800, 3 at 1440×900, 7 at 1060×800 and 4 at 1280×720, whatever the scroll), so it is the note of the panel (the first bullet above). The cost it had, that the note started lower than the window reaches when Lucía's card was long (at 1280×800 it measured 1013 px from the top in 02-03), is gone: the panel is a zone of its own beside the map, so the note is always in the window.
- `inspection-scroll.browser.test.tsx` holds the browser tests of the 2D screen that need the whole `IncidentScreen`: the inspection box next to the marks of the map, the panel on a phone (390×844 in 04-01: a hover leaves the page where it is and a click that chooses a piece brings the panel into view), the terminal of `tu-laptop` in the panel (01-07 at 1440×900: its body has nothing to scroll, before and after a ping; Task M17, fix round 3) and the inspect help at every width, because `src/scene/` cannot import this folder and this one is at its cap of files.
- `geometry/` holds the browser tests that need the whole `IncidentScreen` and a real layout, moved from the e2e in Tasks M21 and M23 of the 2D plan: the panel in every scene (inside its zone, its foot in view, its body scrolling), what the panel and the inspection boxes cover, the width of every label, the choice of a packet or a piece in the panel (the trip of a hand across another piece, the keyboard, the definition of a term), and the sends of one combination that fly at once (its guide says how they work). `src/ui/incident/` is at its cap of files, so they live there.
- `map-sweep.test.ts` lays out the 2D map in every state of every registered incident, at seven widths, without a browser: no overlap, nothing outside the drawing, every stamp drawn or counted by a «+N», every label of a packet placed or left out for a reason, the order of reading. Its second `describe` replays the states the geometry e2e tests of chapters 1 and 2 played (the sends and the edits of the player, at the 900 px and 1060 px areas of their two windows, and, for the states of 02-10, 02-14 case 1 and `in 02-07 the map and its labels fit the area at full size`, also at the 1280 px and 1440 px of the map drawn in a row), so a change of the layout shows there before the player sees it. The states of the first `describe` include, since Task 34, the case of a diagnose that has a fix, repaired (`02-14: case 5, repaired, every combination`).
- `incident-screen.test.tsx` draws the screen in jsdom over a double of `GameScene` (each piece as its label and each packet as a button), which `scene.drawsTheRealMap` switches off: its last describe, `the incidents of the chapters on the 2D map`, plays registered incidents (01-05, 01-07, 01-11, 01-13, 02-01, 02-02, 02-03, 02-08, 02-12, 02-13, 02-14, 04-01 and 04-02; Task 34 added `in 02-12 waiting from the clock card changes the answer of dig`, `in 02-03 the services card opens from the label of laptop-lucia and switches the address` and `in 02-14 case 5 can be repaired from the routes card`; the one of 02-13 is `02-13: dig of tienda.lucia.example draws a question to the resolver and ping of the same name draws none`, the screen half of the test of the same title in `src/game/chapters/chapter-2.test.ts`) on the real 2D map, with the player's clicks and keys, for what a screen reader hears (the stamps), the labels of the packets in a still map, the focus of the cards and the preference for less motion. The sheet of the map has no width in jsdom, so the file answers `useElementSize` of `@mantine/hooks` with 1060 px; a geometry that needs a real layout is a browser test (`inspection-scroll.browser.test.tsx`).
- Effects live in `use-*.ts` hooks next to their first consumer.
