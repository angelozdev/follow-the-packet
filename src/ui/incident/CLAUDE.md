# ui/incident

The incident screen: Lucía's card (tasks, the "Enviar solución" button and her reply, the folded glossary with this incident's new terms first, and hints; the report and the tasks mark each new term with «nuevo» once, at its first appearance on the card, however many times they use it; a diagnose has no tasks or button, and its hints are those of the case), the cards that open beside a piece of the map (`map-cards/`: the task card of a firewall, the terminal of an origin (`map-cards/terminal/`) and the card of a device in an identify incident), the inspection box, Lucía's question (the incident's own, or «¿Qué pasó?»), the card of the case a diagnose is on (`case-card.tsx`) and the result.

- The screen decides which card opens: a firewall opens its task card, an origin its terminal, a device with a card in an identify incident that card (keyed by its device, so the picks of one never pass to another). Where the card sits is the scene's rule (`cardPlacement` and `terminalSide` in `src/scene/layout.ts`): the terminal opens on the free side of its origin and, when neither side is free (an origin drawn outside the office, to the left of the row), docks in the bottom left corner of the map, so it covers no other piece's label or stamp: when what it shows outgrows the free height under the pieces (a traceroute listed), its body scrolls.
- Until a piece or a stamp is inspected, the inspection area shows the inspect help and a legend of the stamps (✓ arrived, ✕ stopped, ? lost, ! a warning appeared) and the lock, in words that need no term of any chapter. The double ring of an intruder's stamp is not in it: the legend names the stamps that have a mark of their own.
- The case card sits in the side panel, below Lucía's card, in a diagnose incident until the result: it shows the case's report and the tests the origin can send (the list of the tasks, ticked once sent), and the primary button «Ya sé la causa» opens the one question of the case, with its options as the lettered `Choice` options of `OptionList` (`explanation-card.tsx`, which Lucía's explanation uses too). A wrong option shows its feedback and is locked; the right one shows its feedback, locks them all and offers «Siguiente caso» (or «Ver resultado» after the last, which saves the stars as the explanation's «Ver resultado» does). The screen keys it by the case, so each case starts with its question closed, and the next case closes any card open on the map. The result card of a diagnose words its three stars for the cases and lists the cases that were not right at the first try (`cases`: `caseSummary`).
- Every message to the player is a `Message` of `@/design`: Lucía's alert (`error`, `role="alert"`, mounted again with each episode), her reply to a rejected solution (an `error` per failing task and one for an intruder, inside the `status` region that is always mounted) and the feedback of an option (`success` under «Correcto.», `error` under «No es eso.»). The guard is a rendered-DOM test, `every warning and error an incident can trigger is drawn by Message and no alert is drawn outside it` in `incident-screen.test.tsx`.
- Every card wears the stripe of style B (`DESIGN.md`, "Style B"): Lucía's card has its title on the stripe with her role at its right; the question, the result and the inspection box have their type on the stripe and their heading under it.
- The glossary shows the icon of each term that names an entity, and the inspection box shows the icon of what it inspects in its heading (`EntityIcon` from `@/scene`). A field of the inspection box whose value is one short line sits beside its label (a wrapping flex row; a value that does not fit, or a field with several lines, goes under the label), so the box stays low (a field's `notes`, such as the one about the header outside the office, are drawn as muted lines under its values): it is anchored at the bottom right of the map (at 900 px and under it is not drawn over the map: it is a block of its own between the map and the side panel, so a card docked in the map's corner never covers it), and the row of the public IP (D-5) made the box of a VPC server cover the label «Security Group servidor2» in 04-01 at 1440×900. When the text of the box does not fit its height (45 % of the map, 45vh under 900 px), its body scrolls inside the box and its band stays: the box is hover-driven, so `use-inspection.ts` keeps it for 400 ms after the pointer leaves the piece (time to cross to it) and for as long as the pointer is over it, and the box takes the mouse only while it scrolls (`data-scrolls` on its holder, set when the body has more text than it shows), so a box that fits never stands between the pointer and the label under it. Its scrollbar is always drawn, so a text cut at the bottom shows there is more.
- State lives in the session reducer of `@/game`; there is no game clock.
- Effects live in `use-*.ts` hooks next to their first consumer.
- Not here: session state and what the panels show (`src/game/`), the map and its labels (`src/scene/`), visible Spanish text (`src/copy/`).
- May import: `@/game`, `@/scene`, `@/storage`, `@/levels`, `@/copy`, `@/design`, other files of `src/ui/` (`@/ui/<file>`), and only types from `@/engine`.
