# ui/incident

The incident screen: the map with the cards that open beside a piece of it (`map-cards/`: the task card of a firewall, the terminal of an origin (`map-cards/terminal/`) and the card of a device in an identify incident), the inspection box, and the side panel (`side-cards/`: Lucía's card with her question, the card of the case a diagnose is on and the result). The screen joins them, decides which card opens (a firewall its task card, every other piece its `PieceCard`, which draws one card with a tab for each card the piece has) and keeps the hooks they share (`use-focus-on-mount.ts`, `use-inspection.ts`).

- The screen decides which card opens: a firewall opens its task card; every other piece opens its `PieceCard` (`map-cards/piece-card.tsx`), whose model `pieceCard` lists the cards it has: an origin its terminal, a device with a card in an identify incident that card (keyed by its device, so the picks of one never pass to another), the owner of a card that classifies that card, a device its «Ajustes de red» and «Servicios», a router its tables, a server of names its records and its cache. Where the card sits is the scene's rule (`cardPlacement` and `terminalSide` in `src/scene/cards/`): the terminal opens on the free side of its origin and, when neither side is free (an origin drawn outside the office, to the left of the row), docks in the bottom left corner of the map, so it covers no other piece's label or stamp: when what it shows outgrows the free height under the pieces (a traceroute listed, a table), its body scrolls. Every card that opens beside a piece is a `ChipCard` inside the anchor that caps its height with the map and scrolls its body, so the tables, the card with tabs and the one that classifies follow the same rule as the terminal; the clock card is not one of them, it sits in the side panel.
- Until a piece or a stamp is inspected, the inspection area shows the inspect help (with an open card it loses its last sentence, about pinning the box with a click or Enter, `SCREEN_TEXT.inspectPinHelp`, drawn in a span of its own: while a card is open the box may have no place, and the promise would be false) and a legend of the stamps (✓ arrived, ✕ stopped, ? lost, ! a warning appeared) and the lock, in words that need no term of any chapter. The double ring of an intruder's stamp is not in it: the legend names the stamps that have a mark of their own. On the 2D map above 900 px that help is not over the map: the screen draws the same two lines (`InspectHelp`, with the same pinning sentence rule) as a `Message` of variant `note` in the side panel (Decision D of the student, 2026-10-19), so it never covers a label, a piece or a stamp; the holder of the box stays over the map, empty until a piece or a stamp is inspected, because `use-inspection.ts` and the box live in it. The screen reads the breakpoint with `useMediaQuery('(min-width: 901px)')` of `@mantine/hooks`, read at the first render so the help never shows over the map for a frame; 900 px and under keep the two paragraphs in the holder, as the CSS block of 900 px lays them. The note sits right under Lucía's card, after the note of the box with no room when that one shows (`side-cards/CLAUDE.md`), and it stays while a box is on show, unlike the help over the map, which went: a note that came and went moved the cards below it (the clock, the case, the explanation) by its height, and a click on one of them could lose its target (with that version, the 01-14 test of `chapter-1.spec.ts` at 1440×900 timed out at «Ya sé la causa»; a pointerdown outside a pinned box releases it, which brought the note back under the pointer). A jsdom test has no `matchMedia`, so it sees the help in the holder; the browser tests of `inspection-scroll.browser.test.tsx` hold the 2D layout: in every incident at 1280×800, 1440×900 and 1280×720 the note meets no mark and leaves the map, at 900 and 901 px it is in the holder and in the panel, what is under the note stays where it is while a box shows, is pinned and goes, and a card open takes its pinning sentence.
- Every card wears the stripe of style B (`DESIGN.md`, "Style B"): Lucía's card has its title on the stripe with her role at its right; the question, the result and the inspection box have their type on the stripe and their heading under it (a pinned inspection box also has «Fijada» and a small «Cerrar» button at the right of its stripe).
- The glossary shows the icon of each term that names an entity, and the inspection box shows the icon of what it inspects in its heading (`EntityIcon` from `@/scene`), except for the list of the stamps of a place, which is no entity (`Inspection.entity` is absent) and has no icon; its fields are keyed by position, because two packets of a list can share a label. A field of the inspection box whose value is one short line sits beside its label (a wrapping flex row; a value that does not fit, or a field with several lines, goes under the label), so the box stays low (a field's `notes`, such as the one about the header outside the office, are drawn as muted lines under its values): it opens at the bottom right of the map and steps aside to the bottom left when an open card touches that place (the next bullet; at 900 px and under it is not drawn over the map: it is a block of its own between the map and the side panel, so a card docked in the map's corner never covers it), and the row of the public IP (D-5) made the box of a VPC server cover the label «Security Group servidor2» in 04-01 at 1440×900. The box never grows over a label, a stamp or a floor name of the map: `use-inspection.ts` caps its height, each time it inspects another piece and before it is drawn, with the room from its bottom edge up to the lowest of those marks that lies over it (`inspection-room.ts`, a gap of 8 px, a floor of 120 px; `MAP_MARKS` in `@/scene` names what counts), written as `--inspection-room` on the holder; the CSS takes the smaller of that and 45 % of the map. Nothing in the layout of the map reserves room for the box, so this cap is what keeps it clear: a box that lists one more field (04-01 lists «Servicios» once `service` is known) scrolls instead of covering the label «Security Group servidor2». The marks of the map are measured when the box opens or changes place, not on every frame: only the open card is watched all the time (the next bullet). When the text of the box does not fit its height (the smaller of that cap and 45 % of the map, 45vh under 900 px), its body scrolls inside the box and its band stays: the box is a preview while the pointer or the focus is on a piece or a stamp, and a click, a tap, Enter or Space pins it. `use-inspection.ts` is the box of `useFloatingBox` (`src/ui/floating-box/`) over the inspections of the session: a preview stays 400 ms after the pointer leaves the piece (time to cross to it) and for as long as the pointer is over it, and a touch never previews. A pinned box ignores the hover and the focus of other pieces and goes with «Cerrar», Escape, a pointerdown outside it, or when its piece no longer has an inspection. A pin from the keyboard moves the focus to the heading of the box (`tabIndex={-1}`, from where the arrow keys scroll its body; Shift+Tab stops first on the scrolling body, which Chrome makes a Tab stop, and then reaches «Cerrar»), except when the same click opens a card (`opensCard`, which the screen also uses to open it): the card keeps the focus, and Escape inside it closes the card and also releases the box. That choice is made once, when the pin happens, and used once, so a later change of phase never moves the focus. The box takes the mouse only while it scrolls (`data-scrolls` on its holder, set when the body has more text than it shows), so a box that fits, pinned or not, never stands between the pointer and the label under it; «Cerrar» always takes it. Its scrollbar is always drawn, so a text cut at the bottom shows there is more.
- **The inspection box never covers an open card (task 28 bis, rule D).** Above 900 px it has two places, the bottom right corner of the map, where it opens, and the bottom left one (`data-place` on the holder; the CSS of the left place is inside `@media (min-width: 901px)`, because a rule with an attribute outweighs the plain class of the 900 px block and would win there). `inspectionPlace` (`inspection-place.ts`, pure) picks it: a place is free when the box in it, at the tallest it may be (45 % of the map, whatever it shows, so the place does not change from one piece to the next), stays 8 px or more from the open card; a card with no size touches nothing; between two free places the one the box has wins, so it does not jump while the card leaves its place alone, and a box that opens takes the right one. The marks of the map (the labels, stamps and floor names that lie inside the map, `MAP_MARKS`) also pick it, as a preference (Task M17, fix round 1: in 04-01 at 1280×800 the box in the right corner covered «internet», «Internet gateway» and «Atacante»): between two places the card leaves free, the one with no mark in the bottom `MIN_ROOM` (120 px) of the box wins, because the box is never shorter than that and covers such a mark whatever it shows; when the marks cover both places, or none, the order above stands, and a place that is free of marks but touched by the card is never taken. A mark higher than that is left to the height of the box (`inspectionRoom`, measured at the place chosen), which is capped by the marks above it. `use-inspection.ts` reads the card from the DOM (`MAP_CARD` in `@/scene`) and is not told where it is: the card's real height, its `--card-room` cap and the `clamp` under the title bar exist only in the DOM. A mutation observer on `.incident__map` (`useMutationObserver` of `@mantine/hooks`, which the screen attaches to the map as the `watchTheMap` ref the hook returns; `childList`, `subtree`, `style` and `class`) fires when the card appears, changes tab, height or side; the stage of the 2D map scrolls under a card without changing the DOM, so the hook also listens to `scroll` on the map in the capture phase (`useEventListener`, because a scroll does not bubble; the screen's `watchTheMap` is the two merged, `useMergedRef`) and calls the same function; its callback measures and writes `data-place` and `--inspection-room` on the holder in the same task, before the browser draws: a state would lag a frame. It acts only while a box is on show or a card is asked for. A box that a card comes to touch (it changes tab, height or side, or the stage scrolls under it) changes place the moment it would touch (decided 44 of the plan). When neither place is free a preview is not drawn (the help shows instead) and a pinned box is released with `release()`: the pin and the ring of its piece go, and if the focus was inside the box it returns to the piece; the box does not come back by itself when the card closes, the player pins it again. The reason is written (decided 42 and 43 of the plan): the hook returns `noRoom`, true from the first attempt of a box that finds no place, and it stays, though the pointer leaves, for as long as the card is asked for (so the reason does not blink); it ends when the card closes or when a box asked for finds a place. The screen draws a `Message` of variant `note` (`role="status"`, `SCREEN_TEXT.noRoomForTheBox`) in the side panel, right under Lucía's card, the one place no card of the map can cover, and the grey help of the corner is no host for it: a card sometimes covers that help (backlog J2 and J8). The box of the click that opens a card waits, hidden (`data-hold`, `visibility: hidden`), until the card is in the DOM and measured: the preview that was up before the click would otherwise show over a card that is not drawn yet. The screen tells the hook that a card is asked for (`card !== null || piece !== null`). Under 901 px the holder is not over the map, which the hook sees by comparing rectangles, and none of this applies, except one thing (Task M17, fix round 3): when a box is pinned (a click, a tap, Enter or Space; never a preview, so a hover scrolls nothing) and the holder lies under the map, which on a phone is usually below the fold (y 875 in a window of 844 in 01-05, and the list of a «+N» at y 759 to 1092), the hook brings the holder into view with `scrollIntoView({ block: 'nearest' })`, the least the player has to scroll, as the card of the 2D map is brought into view (`useRevealCard`). It does it once, when the pin happens or the pinned box shows another piece, and not when the same click opens a card (`opensCard`, decided at the pin like the focus): the card's own reveal is the one the player needs then, and revealing the box too moved the page 100 px at 390×844 (`geometry/card-layout.browser.test.tsx`, «a card opened on a narrow map never scrolls the layer of the labels…»; until Task M21 of the 2D plan, `tests/e2e/card-layout.spec.ts`). It does not put the page back when the box is released: the player may have scrolled since, and «Cerrar» must not move the page under their finger. If a frame ever showed the box over the card, plan B would be `useInterval` of `@mantine/hooks` at 32 ms or less; it was not needed (`the inspection box covers no card of the map, right after the click and at rest, in a low or narrow window` in `geometry/card-layout.browser.test.tsx` samples the DOM after every frame the page draws, from the click on; until Task M23 of the 2D plan the oracle of `tests/e2e/card-layout.spec.ts` stepped the page frame by frame).
- State lives in the session reducer of `@/game`, including the game clock (`Session.clock`, which only the player's `wait` moves; the clock card of `side-cards/` shows it and offers the steps). What the player has half done in a card (the chips of a terminal, the trays of an entry, the item picked up to classify) is the card's own state, kept above its tabs when the piece has several cards.
- The screen gives the map `drawnSends(session.sent)` (a `useMemo` of `session.sent`): the last send of each combination, and before each send by name its question as a run of its own, with the packet that asked marked to wait for it (`after`), so the question has its stamp, its box and its place on the map. `map-sweep.test.ts` replays the sends by name of `namesSendIncident` in six worlds (the resolver answers, an authoritative server fails, an address with no resolver, an address nobody has, a firewall that stops the question, a hosts file) at seven widths; the registered incidents 02-10 to 02-14 send by name too, and the first `describe` walks them like every other incident. At 900 px and under the grid of its `main` (`.incident`) sets the row of the map to `auto`. The screen passes the scene the terms the player knows (`known`, from the session), which only the 2D map reads: a group names its block only once `cidr` is known, and what inspects the list of a «+N» (`inspectStampsTrigger`, `packetIds => trigger(PIECE_ID.stamps(packetIds))`), because the 2D scene draws the «+N» and cannot import `@/game`.
- In the 2D map the screen's `overlay` (the card it opens) is drawn by `GameScene` inside the stage, over the drawing, and `MAP_CARD` is the same `.map-card` the inspection box watches. The idle help of the box, left in the holder over the bottom right of the map, covered labels and pieces of the 2D map in some incidents (measured on 2026-10-19 at scroll top: 5 of the 25 incidents at 1280×800, 3 at 1440×900, 7 at 1060×800 and 4 at 1280×720, whatever the scroll), so above 900 px it is a note of the side panel (the first bullet above). The cost of the student's choice: the note starts lower than the window reaches when Lucía's card is long (at 1280×800 it measured 1013 px from the top in 02-03, with the panel unscrolled, and about 152 px high), so the panel has to be scrolled to read it.
- `inspection-scroll.browser.test.tsx` holds the browser tests of the 2D screen that need the whole `IncidentScreen`: the inspection box next to a card and next to the marks of the map, the same box pinned on a phone (390×844 in 04-01: a hover leaves the page where it is, a click brings the holder into view, but not when the same click opens a card), and the card docked in the corner (01-07 at 1440×900: the body of the terminal of `tu-laptop` has nothing to scroll, before and after a ping; Task M17, fix round 3), because `src/scene/` cannot import this folder and this one is at its cap of files.
- `geometry/` holds the browser tests that need the whole `IncidentScreen` and a real layout, moved from the e2e in Tasks M21 and M23 of the 2D plan: the cards in every scene, what the cards and the inspection boxes cover, the width of every label, the pin of the inspection box (the trip of a hand across another piece, the keyboard, a click that opens a card, the definition of a term), the side the box takes beside an open card, and the sends of one combination that fly at once (its guide says how they work). `src/ui/incident/` is at its cap of files, so they live there.
- `map-sweep.test.ts` lays out the 2D map in every state of every registered incident, at seven widths, without a browser: no overlap, nothing outside the drawing, every stamp drawn or counted by a «+N», every label of a packet placed or left out for a reason, the order of reading. Its second `describe` replays the states the geometry e2e tests of chapters 1 and 2 played (the sends and the edits of the player, at the 900 px and 1060 px areas of their two windows, and, for the states of 02-10, 02-14 case 1 and `in 02-07 the map and its labels fit the area at full size`, also at the 1280 px and 1440 px of the map drawn in a row), so a change of the layout shows there before the player sees it. The states of the first `describe` include, since Task 34, the case of a diagnose that has a fix, repaired (`02-14: case 5, repaired, every combination`).
- `incident-screen.test.tsx` draws the screen in jsdom over a double of `GameScene` (each piece as its label and each packet as a button), which `scene.drawsTheRealMap` switches off: its last describe, `the incidents of the chapters on the 2D map`, plays registered incidents (01-05, 01-07, 01-11, 01-13, 02-01, 02-02, 02-03, 02-08, 02-12, 02-13, 02-14, 04-01 and 04-02; Task 34 added `in 02-12 waiting from the clock card changes the answer of dig`, `in 02-03 the services card opens from the label of laptop-lucia and switches the address` and `in 02-14 case 5 can be repaired from the routes card`; the one of 02-13 is `02-13: dig of tienda.lucia.example draws a question to the resolver and ping of the same name draws none`, the screen half of the test of the same title in `src/game/chapter-2.test.ts`) on the real 2D map, with the player's clicks and keys, for what a screen reader hears (the stamps), the labels of the packets in a still map, the focus of the cards and the preference for less motion. The sheet of the map has no width in jsdom, so the file answers `useElementSize` of `@mantine/hooks` with 1060 px; a geometry that needs a real layout is a browser test (`inspection-scroll.browser.test.tsx`).
