# ui/floating-box

The one behaviour of the boxes that float over the game and open from a trigger: `use-floating-box.ts`, and the definition of a term (`term-tip.tsx`), which is built on it. The inspection box (`src/ui/incident/use-inspection.ts`) is built on it too. The hook is generic by key, so many triggers share one box, and it keeps the state of the box and the rules below in one place; a new floating box uses it and writes none of them again.

- Preview: a mouse or a pen hovering a trigger, or the focus on it, shows the box of its key. When the pointer or the focus leaves, the box waits `GRACE_MS` (400 ms, `useTimeout`) so the pointer can cross to it, stays while the pointer is over it (the props in `box`), and goes as soon as the pointer leaves it, unless it leaves straight onto the trigger that opened it (a definition is portaled, so React sees it inside its term and gives the term no new `pointerenter`). Another trigger of the same scope takes it at once: the scope is the argument of the hook, and the boxes of one scope hand a preview to each other through a window event; a pinned box is not handed. A touch pointer never previews.
- Pin: a click, a tap, Enter or Space (a click with `detail === 0` is from the keyboard) pins the box, and the optional `onPin(key, fromKeyboard)` of the hook runs once at that moment, so a caller can decide something that must not be recomputed later, such as where the focus goes. The trigger carries `data-pinned`. A pinned box ignores hover and focus on other triggers of its hook, and a click on another trigger pins that one. The inspection box does not pin: its triggers replace the click of the hook with the choice of the screen (`src/ui/shell/use-selection.ts`), so its box only previews.
- Release: Escape anywhere (`useWindowEvent`), a pointerdown outside the box and the trigger that opened it (`useClickOutside`, on while a box is shown), and `release()`, which a close button calls. The listeners of Escape and of the hand-over between hooks of a scope do nothing in a hook that shows no box. Escape and `release()` give the focus back to that trigger when it was inside the box, and the focus that comes back does not reopen the box: the key that was shown is dismissed until the pointer or the focus leaves its trigger and comes back, whichever order the closing and the focus arrive in. The dismissal holds for a pinned box and for a preview whose trigger still has the focus or the pointer (`:hover`, `:focus`); a preview that only waited out its grace after the focus left (the camera of the map releases it when a Tab to the next piece moves the map) is not dismissed, so the focus that comes back to its trigger previews it. The caller releases a box whose subject no longer exists (the inspection box, when its piece or its packet has no inspection any more); the hook does not know it.
- State is held in a ref as well as in React state, so a handler that a trigger gave to a label or a stamp never reads a stale pin.
- `TermTip` is the definition of a term: mouse hover previews it, the pointer can move onto it (6 px away, within the grace), a click or a tap on the term pins it, and Escape or a pointerdown outside the term and the definition releases it. A term inside a button or a link neither pins nor lets the clicks of its definition reach that button: the click is the button's. Terms are not Tab stops, so with the keyboard the definition is in the glossary of the Almanaque. Each term has its own hook, in the scope `term`: the previews of two terms hand over at once, and a pinned definition stays while another term's is previewed.
- Not here: what a box shows (`src/ui/incident/` for the inspection box), the look of a box (`src/styles.css` and `src/design/`), where a box sits (the scene, for the inspection triggers; `@floating-ui/react-dom`, for a definition).
- May import: `@/copy`, and `@mantine/hooks` and `@floating-ui/react-dom`.
