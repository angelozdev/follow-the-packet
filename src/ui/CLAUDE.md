# ui

Screens and panels: the roadmap (the game chapters grouped by level: a caption `h2` per level and a `Card` per chapter, with «Capítulo N» and its count on the stripe and the chapter's title under it), the notice page (unknown or locked incident), the incident page and the incident screen (`incident/`), the entity catalog page (`catalog/`, at `#/entidades`, not linked from the roadmap), plus shared pieces (`stars.tsx`, `tagged-text.tsx`, `term-tip.tsx`, `card-icon.tsx` (the icon on the stripe of a card whose kind is not an entity of the map: Lucía, a question, a result, an inspection, a terminal, the network settings of a device and a chapter), and the "Sin animaciones" checkbox: `motion-toggle.tsx` with `use-still-mode.ts`).

- A term in a text shows its definition on hover, in a tooltip portaled to `document.body` and positioned with `@floating-ui/react-dom` (`term-tip.tsx`). Terms are not Tab stops. The inspection box renders its terms with `tips={false}`: a tooltip over a box that scrolls would cover the text the player is reading.
- Every button and link is a `Button` of `@/design`, every chip and option a `Choice`, every panel a `Card`, every name a `NameTag` and every warning or error a `Message` (`note`, `success`, `warning` or `error`, with the words of `MESSAGE_TEXT` as its `words`; the screen that owns the message gives it `role="status"` or `role="alert"` when it must be announced); this folder writes no `<button>`, `<a>` or alert paragraph of its own and adds no look of its own to them. The notice page takes the variant of its message from its caller: `error` for an incident that does not exist and `warning` for one that is locked.
- Renders results computed elsewhere. Effects live in `use-*.ts` hooks next to their first consumer; prefer `@mantine/hooks`.
- May import: `@/game`, `@/scene`, `@/storage`, `@/levels`, `@/copy`, `@/design`, and only types from `@/engine`.
