# ui

Screens and panels: the roadmap (the game chapters grouped by level: a caption `h2` per level and a `Card` per chapter, with «Capítulo N» and its count on the stripe and the chapter's title under it), the notice page (unknown or locked incident), the incident page and the incident screen (`incident/`), the entity catalog page (`catalog/`, at `#/entidades`, not linked from the roadmap), plus shared pieces (`stars.tsx`, `tagged-text.tsx`, `floating-box/` (the one behaviour of the boxes that float from a trigger, and the definition of a term built on it), `card-icon.tsx` (the icon on the stripe of a card whose kind is not an entity of the map: Lucía, a question, a result, an inspection, a terminal, the network settings of a device and a chapter), and the "Sin animaciones" checkbox: `motion-toggle.tsx` with `use-still-mode.ts`).

- A term in a text shows its definition in a tooltip portaled to `document.body` and positioned with `@floating-ui/react-dom` (`floating-box/term-tip.tsx`): on hover as a preview the pointer can move onto, and pinned by a click or a tap on the term until Escape or a pointerdown outside it. Terms are not Tab stops. The inspection box renders its terms with `tips={false}`: a tooltip over a box that scrolls would cover the text the player is reading.
- The boxes that float from a trigger (the inspection box and the definition of a term) share one behaviour, `useFloatingBox` in `floating-box/`: a preview on hover and focus, a pin on a click or Enter, and the ways out. Nothing else in this folder writes timers, Escape or outside-click handling for a box of this kind.
- Every button and link is a `Button` of `@/design`, every chip and option a `Choice`, every panel a `Card`, and every name a `NameTag`; this folder writes no `<button>` or `<a>` of its own and adds no look of its own to them.
- Renders results computed elsewhere. Effects live in `use-*.ts` hooks next to their first consumer; prefer `@mantine/hooks`.
- May import: `@/game`, `@/scene`, `@/storage`, `@/levels`, `@/copy`, `@/design`, and only types from `@/engine`.
