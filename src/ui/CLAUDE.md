# ui

Screens and panels: the roadmap (the game chapters grouped by level: a caption `h2` per level and a `Card` per chapter, with «Capítulo N» and its count on the stripe and the chapter's title under it), the notice page (unknown or locked incident), the incident page and the incident screen (`incident/`, with the cards of a piece in `map-cards/` and the cards of Lucía's region in `side-cards/`), the column at the right of its map and the panel of what the player chose in it (`shell/`, with the tabs of a piece in `shell/panel/`), the entity catalog page (`catalog/`, at `#/entidades`, not linked from the roadmap), plus shared pieces (`stars.tsx`, `tagged-text.tsx`, `floating-box/` (the one behaviour of the boxes that float from a trigger, and the definition of a term built on it), `card-icon.tsx` (the icon on the stripe of a card whose kind is not an entity of the map: Lucía, a question, a result, an inspection, a terminal, the network settings of a device, a table, the clock of the game, a case of a diagnose and a chapter), and the "Sin animaciones" checkbox: `motion-toggle.tsx` with `use-still-mode.ts`).

- A term in a text shows its definition in a tooltip portaled to `document.body` and positioned with `@floating-ui/react-dom` (`floating-box/term-tip.tsx`): on hover as a preview the pointer can move onto, and pinned by a click or a tap on the term until Escape or a pointerdown outside it. Terms are not Tab stops. The inspection box renders its terms with `tips={false}`: a tooltip over a box that scrolls would cover the text the player is reading. An IP in the plain text of a `TaggedText` (with its port or its prefix) is drawn in the mono face with no box (`.ip-text`), and reads the same; a text that is code already, or a chip (a control, not prose), passes `ips={false}` and keeps its face and its text nodes.
- The boxes that float from a trigger (the inspection box and the definition of a term) share one behaviour, `useFloatingBox` in `floating-box/`: a preview on hover and focus, a pin on a click or Enter, and the ways out (the inspection box never pins: its triggers carry the choice of the screen instead of the click of the hook, `shell/use-selection.ts`). Nothing else in this folder writes timers, Escape or outside-click handling for a box of this kind.
- Every button and link is a `Button` of `@/design`, every chip and option a `Choice`, every panel a `Card`, every name a `NameTag` and every warning or error a `Message` (`note`, `success`, `warning` or `error`, with the words of `MESSAGE_TEXT` as its `words`; the screen that owns the message gives it `role="status"` or `role="alert"` when it must be announced); this folder writes no `<button>`, `<a>` or alert paragraph of its own and adds no look of its own to them. The notice page takes the variant of its message from its caller: `error` for an incident that does not exist and `warning` for one that is locked.
- Renders results computed elsewhere. Effects live in `use-*.ts` hooks next to their first consumer; prefer `@mantine/hooks`.
- May import: `@/game`, `@/scene`, `@/storage`, `@/levels`, `@/copy`, `@/design`, and only types from `@/engine`.
