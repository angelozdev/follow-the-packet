# engine/exposure

The fixed probe battery behind "only what is needed": which probes a firewall lets in without being an expected flow. A device without firewalls opens nothing. The battery sends from the actors that send packets, the internet probe and every device; a server of the internet only answers, so it is no source. `reachesHost` says whether a packet got to its device, past its firewalls or with none.
