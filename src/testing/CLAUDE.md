# testing

Helpers for tests only: network fixtures (`fixtures.ts`: the lab 03 VPC and the chapter 1 office), incidents (`incidents.ts`: a registered filter incident by its id, a send incident on the office, one on the office with routers whose chips ping, ping with a TTL and traceroute, an identify incident as 01-01 plays it: the cards of laptop-visita and the printer, whose tray starts with the IP of Lucía's note picked, a diagnose incident (seven cases on the office of the map that grows, each from the world before the incident plus its own changes, each with its chips, and the right cause in a different place), and a filter incident with a host firewall on your laptop with both lists empty, Source and Destination chips, and an outbound rule as its solution (`outboundFilterIncident`), a filter incident with only the inbound rules of a host firewall open: laptop-lucia has a firewall with no inbound rules that lets everything out, Source chips for your laptop and the printer, and an inbound rule as its solution (`hostFirewallIncident`, built from `outboundFilterIncident`), a send incident with a setting: a reinstalled laptop with no default gateway, whose card offers none, the router, the printer and an IP nobody has, and whose terminal offers only the address and the ping), the VPC worlds of chapter 4 with the packets of their flows (`chapter4Worlds`: 04-01 and 04-02 each at its start, once solved and with each one-chip rule placed, which the engine tests need and cannot import from `@/levels`), the office worlds of chapter 1 with the packets that can be sent in each (`chapter1Worlds`: every incident at its start, once solved and with each choice of each of its settings, and every case of a diagnose incident in its own world; the packets are those of the flows and of the send chips, a traceroute through its probe), readers for the repository's `syllabus.md` and for the chapter map and the levels of the chapters doc (`repo.ts`) and the Vitest setup (`setup.ts`).

- `tcpService(port, listen)` builds a TCP `Service` (on all addresses unless told otherwise) for the tests that give a device or a server a program.
- `EVERY_TERM` is every registered term, for the tests that do not care what the player knows yet; a test about what is hidden builds its own set or its own incident.
- Production code never imports `@/testing`.
- May import: `@/engine`, `@/copy`, `@/levels`.
