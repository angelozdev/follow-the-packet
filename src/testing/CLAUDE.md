# testing

Helpers for tests only: network fixtures (`fixtures.ts`: the lab 03 VPC and the chapter 1 office), incidents (`incidents.ts`: a registered filter incident by its id, a send incident on the office, one on the office with routers whose chips ping, ping with a TTL and traceroute, an identify incident as 01-01 plays it: the cards of laptop-visita and the printer, whose tray starts with the IP of Lucía's note picked, a diagnose incident (seven cases on the office of the map that grows, each from the world before the incident plus its own changes, each with its chips, and the right cause in a different place), and a filter incident with a host firewall on your laptop with both lists empty, Source and Destination chips, and an outbound rule as its solution (`outboundFilterIncident`), a filter incident with only the inbound rules of a host firewall open: laptop-lucia has a firewall with no inbound rules that lets everything out, Source chips for your laptop and the printer, and an inbound rule as its solution (`hostFirewallIncident`, built from `outboundFilterIncident`), a send incident with a setting: a reinstalled laptop with no default gateway, whose card offers none, the router, the printer and an IP nobody has, and whose terminal offers only the address and the ping; `namesSendIncident`, a send incident on `officeWithNames()` that offers what chapter 2 adds to the contract: an `nc` and a `dig`, a name as an address, the DNS server and the listen address as settings, a table of routes and one of records, a clock, the card a piece opens first and tasks that expect an answer and a status; `classifyIncident`, an identify incident whose card of your laptop classifies the printer and laptop-andres around 192.168.1.0/24; and `repairableDiagnoseIncident`, the diagnose whose fifth case is the loop of the final watch of chapter 1 (the router of the provider sends everything back to the office router, so the ping of laptop-marta to the server ends in Time Exceeded) and has a fix: the table of routes of the provider, with the default route and the range of the server as destinations and the office router and the backbone as next hops, the task that the ping comes back, and a reference solution that takes the default route away, places the range of the server through the backbone and pings again), the VPC worlds of chapter 4 with the packets of their flows (`chapter4Worlds`: 04-01 and 04-02 each at its start, once solved and with each one-chip rule placed, which the engine tests need and cannot import from `@/levels`), the office worlds of chapter 1 with the packets that can be sent in each (`chapter1Worlds`: every incident at its start, once solved and with each choice of each of its settings, and every case of a diagnose incident in its own world; the packets are those of the flows and of the send chips, a traceroute through its probe), readers for the repository's `syllabus.md` and for the chapter map and the levels of the chapters doc (`repo.ts`) and the Vitest setup (`setup.ts`).

- `officeWithNames()` is the office of the map that grows with the DNS of chapter 2: `tu-laptop` uses the resolver `servidor-dns`, which asks `servidor-autoritativo` (zone `lucia.example`, with `www` and an alias `blog`) and `servidor-autoritativo-noticias` (zone `noticias.example`, down); `NAME_SERVERS` holds their ids, IPs and the zones. They sit behind `router-backbone`, which gets one interface for each.
- `dig(from, name)` builds the `dig` of a name (a UDP datagram to port 53 with `lookup: 'dig'` and the name as destination) and `withResolverCache(net, entries)` gives the resolver of `officeWithNames()` an initial `dns.cache`, measured against the clock of the game.
- `tcpService(port, listen)` builds a TCP `Service` (on all addresses unless told otherwise) for the tests that give a device or a server a program.
- `EVERY_TERM` is every registered term, for the tests that do not care what the player knows yet; a test about what is hidden builds its own set or its own incident.
- `CHAPTER_1_TERMS` is the terms the incidents of chapter 1 introduce: for a test of chapter 1 that sends packets and must not see a term of a later chapter (`EVERY_TERM` includes them, so the box of a trip would show a route or a translation). A test that needs a session with it builds the incident with `introduces: [...CHAPTER_1_TERMS]`.
- Production code never imports `@/testing`.
- May import: `@/engine`, `@/copy`, `@/levels`.
