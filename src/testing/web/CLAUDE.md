# testing/web

Helpers for the tests of the web in chapter 3, apart from `src/testing/` because that folder is full. `web-incident.ts` has `webSendIncident`, a send incident on the office with names that offers what chapter 3 adds to the contract: URL chips (by parts and whole), the messages `curl` (alone, with `-i`, with `-d`, with `-X PUT -d` and with `--connect-timeout 3`) and the browser, a rules card for the host firewall of laptop-lucia, a table of sites on the server of the internet, a clock, and tasks that expect a body and the nth send of their chips. `signInIncident` is `webSendIncident` on a world where the orders app of laptop-lucia also has `/login`, `/admin` (it asks for the `admin` role) and two users, Lucía and Marta (`signInNet`, its firewall lets your laptop in), with one more address (`/admin` by her IP, `admin`) and three more messages: the browser signing in as Lucía (`sign-in-lucia`) or as Marta (`sign-in-marta`) and `curl -i -b sesion=s1` (`curl-cookie`). `addressByPartsIncident` is the incident of the first block of chapter 3 as far as the terminal goes: laptop-lucia alone, the message `curl` alone and eight address chips by parts (every combination of port 3000 or 80, path `/pedidos` or `/pedido` and query `id=7` or `id=8`, after the scheme `http` and the name `localhost`). Its start world (`webNet`) gives laptop-lucia an orders app on port 3000 (orders 7 and 8) and an SSH service on 22 that speaks no HTTP, behind a host firewall with no inbound rules, and gives the server of the internet a web service on port 80 with the shop at `www.lucia.example`, the default site.
- `ladder-of-chapter-3.ts` has `LADDER_OF_CHAPTER_3`, the id, the official name and the sub-chapter of each of the 65 terms of chapter 3 from the table of the spec. `src/levels/ladder.test.ts` checks the registered terms (those of the subchapters built so far, 03-01 to 03-06) against the registry and that no definition names a later one, and `src/game/chapters/chapter-3.test.ts` takes from it what each incident introduces and the names no text of an earlier incident may say. It is test data and lives here, outside `src/levels/`, because a test file cannot be imported without registering its tests again.
- Imports files of `src/testing/` through `@/testing/<file>` (a subfolder may not use `../`), and the barrel of `src/testing/` exports from here.
- Production code never imports `@/testing`.
- May import: `@/engine`, `@/copy`, `@/levels`.
