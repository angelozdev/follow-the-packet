# game/view/panel

What the panel of a piece offers, computed from the session: the tabs of the panel, in the fixed order of the shell, and which one opens first. `panel.ts` has `panelOf(session, pieceId)`, which returns the `Panel` of any piece the map draws.

- The six tabs are `PANEL_TABS`, always in this order: «Estado» (`status`), «Ajustes» (`settings`), «Tablas» (`tables`), «Terminal» (`terminal`), «Navegador» (`browser`) and «Preguntas» (`questions`). A piece has the ones it has and the others are absent, not empty, and the rest keep their order; no chapter adds a seventh. «Estado» is always there: its content is the fields of `inspect`, which this folder does not copy.
- Every card a piece opens (`pieceCard` in `../cards/`) and the rules a firewall lets the player set (`firewallCard` with its chips, as a `RulesPart`) are a part of one tab. `TAB_OF_PART` says which, for each of the twelve kinds: `settings` and `services` are the **sections** of «Ajustes», one under the other (`holds: 'sections'`); `routes`, `translations`, `records`, `cache`, `sites` and `rules` are **fichas** of «Tablas», and `identify` and `classify` are fichas of «Preguntas» (`holds: 'fichas'`, one at a time); `terminal` and `browser` each fill their own tab (`holds: 'one'`). The rules of a firewall are one part whose card carries the two fichas of direction, `entrada` and `salida`, when the ladder has opened the outbound rules (`FirewallCard.outbound`). A tab with a single part has no section bar and no ficha bar; that is how the screen draws it, not a different model.
- The tab that opens first is the one the card of the piece opened first (`pieceCard(...).first`, the rules of a firewall for a firewall) and «Estado» when the piece has no card. A locked piece, a firewall the player cannot set (a diagnose never lets the player set one) and every piece once the session is no longer `playing` have only «Estado». The panel of a server of the internet carries `roles`, the line that says its roles in words with the terms the player knows, once above the tabs.
- The labels of the tabs and of the parts are `PIECE_TEXT.tabs` and `PIECE_TEXT.parts` (`src/copy/`); this table is provisional, as the two `switch` of the screen that draws the parts, until the table of contents of the shell replaces both.
- Pure: no React, no DOM, no clock. Nothing here changes the session.
- Not here: drawing a panel or choosing a tab (`src/ui/shell/`), the cards a part holds (`../cards/`), the fields of «Estado» (`../inspection.ts`), the Spanish text (`src/copy/`).
- May import: `@/copy`, `@/levels`, and other files of `src/game/` (`@/game/<file>`, `@/game/view/<file>`, `@/game/view/cards/<file>`).
