# game/view/cards

What the cards that open beside a piece of the map show, computed from the session: the task card of a firewall, whose kind, title and rules (named by chips) it computes (`firewall-card.ts`), the terminal of an origin (`terminal-card.ts`), the output of a traceroute sent from it (`traceroute-lines.ts`) the card of a device in an identify incident (`identify-card.ts`) and the «Ajustes de red» card of a device (`settings-card.ts`).

- A card is data: the screen draws it and reports the player's picks; nothing here draws or changes the session.
- The output of a traceroute (`tracerouteLines(session, picks)`) is null until a traceroute was sent with those picks. It has one line per probe, as `traceroute` of the engine ran them: the hop number, the IP that answered (a star when nobody did), the time and, on the last line of a Destination Host or Network Unreachable, its mark (`!H` or `!N`, what the command prints), plus the notes that explain the star, the marks and that the times are examples, and the reason when the sender could not send at all. What the player does not know is left out: no time before `rtt`, and a warning whose term is unknown is a star, as the map shows no warning. A router the map does not draw is not named by its IP: the line says «dentro de la oficina» or «fuera de la oficina».
- The card of a device in an identify incident (`identifyCard(session, pieceId)`; `identifyPieces` gives the piece ids that open one) has the device's name as its title, the card's question (or null), and each tray with its name, whether it is `segmented`, the chip `saved` as the answer (the `initialPick` until the player confirms another) and its chips. Each chip carries the `lines` the card shows when the player presses «Listo» with it picked: its own `feedback` and then, for a chip that is not the tray's `initialPick`, the `initialPickRule`; so the rule of the note shows by every path but the note's own chip, whose feedback already is the rule. It also carries `correct`, true when a task expects that chip, so the card writes a right line as a note and a wrong one as an error. The card draws these; it decides none of them.
- The settings card (`settingsCard(session, entryId)`, opened from the entry `PIECE_ID.settings(deviceId)`; `settingsDeviceIds` lists the devices that have one) has a title («Ajustes de red de <device>»), the piece of the device it sits beside (`pieceId`) and, for each setting the incident offers for the device, a tray: its id, its name (the official name of its term, as plain text), its chips and `picked`, the id of the chip whose value is the gateway the device has in the world now (the chip with no value when it has none), or null when no chip stands for it. The card has no draft: a pick is a move of the session, so `picked` always says what the world is. The terminal shows no setting: it does not know them.
- Not here: piece ids, piece labels and the inspection box (`src/game/view/`), the session state and its reducer (`src/game/`), drawing a card (`src/ui/incident/map-cards/`), the Spanish text (`src/copy/`).
- May import: `@/engine`, `@/copy`, `@/levels`, and other files of `src/game/` (`@/game/<file>`, `@/game/view/<file>`).
