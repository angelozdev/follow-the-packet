# game/view/cards/identify

What the card that classifies other devices shows in an identify incident (`classify-card.ts`), computed from the session: the block with its mask, the 32 bits of the IP of the owner of the card, the items with their IP split at the prefix, the two bins and the line of each placed item. The card of a device with trays of chips in an identify incident (`../identify-card.ts`) stays one folder up.

- The card belongs to one device, its owner (`identify.classify.deviceId`), and only its piece opens it: the pieces of the items open no card. The block (`cidr`) gives the prefix (`parseCidr`), the mask (`maskOf`) and the bits the owner's IP is marked with: the first `prefix` of the 32 are fixed (`bitsRow`). Nothing is computed that the engine already knows (`ipBits`, `ipInCidr`, `maskOf`, `prefixNumbers` are the engine's).
- **The first bin holds what fits in the block and the second what does not** (a convention of the card, not a field of the contract: the incident lists its bins in that order, as «Dentro de 192.168.1.0/24» and «Fuera»). Whether an item fits is `ipInCidr`, never the bin it was placed in; the line of a placed item says it (with the first numbers of its IP and the block, or with the first bits when the prefix does not end at a dot) and `right` says whether the bin agrees.
- An item is in a bin when `Session.answers` holds that bin for it under the tray the card names (`trayId`); any other answer leaves it waiting. The IP is split in text (`splitIp`: `192.168.1.` and `21`) only when the prefix ends at a dot (/8, /16, /24); with any other prefix the split is only in the row of bits.
- `insideBlockPieces(session)` is the set of piece ids inside the block: the owner from the start and each item placed in the first bin. `pieceViews` marks those pieces `insideBlock`, which the scene draws as the outline of the block (Task 21).
- Not here: the device card with trays of chips (`../identify-card.ts`), the tabs of a piece (`../piece-card.ts`), the Spanish text (`src/copy/screens/incident-ui.ts`, `CLASSIFY_TEXT`), the drawing (`src/ui/incident/map-cards/identify/`).
