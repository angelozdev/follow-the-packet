# game/view/cards/terminal

What the terminal of an origin shows, computed from the session: the terminal itself (`terminal-card.ts`: its trays of origin, address and message chips, the line the picks make and the pieces that open one), what a traceroute sent from it printed (`traceroute-lines.ts`) and what an `nc` or a `dig` printed (`output-card.ts`).

- A terminal is data: the screen draws it and reports the player's picks; nothing here draws or changes the session.
- The line of an `nc` is `nc -vz -w 3 <address> <port>` and the line of a `dig` is `dig <name>` with no server (it asks the resolver of the network settings of the device, decided 29); `terminalLine` gives null for an `nc` whose chip has no port.
- The output of an `nc` or a `dig` (`toolOutput(session, picks)`) is null until one was sent with those picks. It reads the last send of the combination (`session.sent` keeps the last of each) and says which result its trace stands for: for an `nc`, an `open` connection (`replied`, with the IP it reached and the round trip), a `refused` one (`refused`) or a `timeout`; for a `dig`, the reply of the resolver (`Trace.dns` with its status, records and SOA; the game clock of the send moves the date), a `timeout` or a `refused` one (the host at the resolver address answered with Port Unreachable). The lines come from `@/copy` (`ncOutput`, `digOutput`; the source of each line is in `src/copy/tools/CLAUDE.md`). **Every other result prints nothing** and the card is null: the origin of those lines was not confirmed, so the inspection box is what says what happened. The query that an `nc` by name makes to the resolver is not a send of the player and its lines are not here.
- The lines are grouped into segments for the fold (V11 = B): when the output has key lines (the status line of a `dig` and its answer: the header, the `ANSWER SECTION` and its records) every run of the other lines is one `folded` segment and each key line stays a `line`; an output with no key line (an `nc`, a `dig` that got no answer) is never folded. `folded` is how many lines the folded segments hold in all. Folding hides lines, it does not drop them: the segments hold every line printed. The fold itself is the card's, not the session's (`src/ui/incident/map-cards/terminal/`).
- The numbered output of a traceroute is not a `ToolOutput`: it stays `tracerouteLines`, with the same lines and notes as before, and the card draws both.
- Not here: the other cards (`src/game/view/cards/`), piece ids, piece labels and the inspection box (`src/game/view/`), the session state and its reducer (`src/game/`), drawing the terminal (`src/ui/incident/map-cards/terminal/`), the Spanish text (`src/copy/`).
- May import: `@/engine`, `@/copy`, `@/levels`, and other files of `src/game/` (`@/game/<file>`, `@/game/view/<file>`).
