# game/view/registry

The registry of the world, computed from the session: what happened since the world last changed, as lines. `lines.ts` has `registryLines(session)`, which returns the lines the newest first.

- A line is one of three. A `packet` line is a packet the map draws, a send of the player or a flow of the incident: the id of its piece (so a click on it chooses the same packet as the stamp), its label, its result and, when the player can place where it stopped, the reason; the result and the reason are `resultOf` and `stopOf` of `../inspection.ts`, the words of its box, and the label is the title of that box, so the registry writes no sentence of its own. A packet whose stop the player cannot place (`hiddenStop`: a firewall they do not know, or a router out of sight) has no reason. A `change` line is the notice of the change that emptied sends that were not empty (`changeNotice`, `RESTART_TEXT.notice` with its exact words) and a `rejection` line is the text the table says under itself when the engine refuses an entry (`problemText`).
- The order is the order of time. The sends come from `Session.history`, every send since the world last changed and not only the last of each combination, each as the map draws it (`drawnSends`: a send by name is two lines, its packet over its question, which left first); the lines of `Session.log` sit after the send of the number they carry and before the next one. The flows come after all of them, the last flow first, as `inspect` lists a «+N»: a flow has no send and is older than every packet the player sends. A change that empties the sends starts the list again, so it is the first line, and the next send becomes the newest.
- Pure: no React, no DOM, no clock. Nothing here changes the session.
- Not here: drawing a line or a bar (`src/ui/shell/status/`), the log itself and when a line is added (`src/game/session.ts`), the Spanish text (`src/copy/`).
- May import: `@/copy`, and other files of `src/game/` (`@/game/<file>`, `@/game/view/<file>`, `@/game/view/cards/<file>`).
