# design

The game's design system: every colour, font, size, space, line, radius and shadow of the interface and the map as a token (`tokens.css`), the reader that lets the map use the same tokens (`tokens.ts`), and the interface primitives with their styles and the one focus ring, in `primitives/` (one file per family: `choice.tsx` holds `Choice` and `ChipTray`; `message.tsx` holds `Message`; the ones that hold content, `Card`, `Tabs`, `Table` and `Dialog`, are in `primitives/panels/`).

- `tokens.css` has two layers of colour: the palette (`--palette-*`, raw colours named after what they are) and the roles (`--color-*`, named after what they are for). The green of a right answer is `--palette-green` and its only role is `--color-success`, a muted green that keeps 4.5:1 as text on the surface and under its fill text (`tokens.test.ts`). Styles and the map use roles, never the palette. The other tokens are scales with one layer: fonts and their three weights (`--font-*`, `--weight-*`), sizes of text (`--text-2xs` to `--text-2xl`), spaces on a 4 px grid (`--space-2xs` to `--space-3xl`), lines (`--stroke-*`), radii (`--radius`, `--radius-tile` for buttons and options, `--radius-card`, `--radius-pill`, `--radius-round`), shadows (`--shadow-sm` for a button, `--shadow-card`, `--shadow-tip`) and the focus ring (`--focus-ring`, and `--focus-ring-on-ink` for the dark stripe of a card). Every size, space and line comes from a scale; a one-off measure that belongs to a single rule (a panel width, the size of a stamp) stays in that rule.
- The weights are the ones `index.html` loads: Plex Sans 400 and 600, Newsreader 500, Plex Mono 400. Newsreader 600 is not loaded: a stamp and a term inside a title ask for 600 and get the browser's bold drawn from 500.
- Vermilion has three roles, today with the same value: `--color-danger` (blocked, intruder, alert, a rejected solution, a wrong answer), `--color-emphasis` (the primary button, the stars, the "nuevo" mark, the tooltip shadow) and `--color-focus` (the focus ring). Their value, `#bf370f`, keeps 4.5:1 for text on every paper and for `--color-on-fill` on it, and 3:1 for the focus ring: `tokens.test.ts` asserts those ratios from `tokens.css`, so a new value is checked there before it ships (`DESIGN.md`, "Contrast").
- `Button` (`primitives/button.tsx`) is every action and every link: a `<button type="button">`, or an `<a>` when it has an `href`. Its variant is its look: `primary` (the action a panel or card exists for, one per panel, drawn as a stamp: surface fill, emphasis text and dashed border, a pill, uppercase, tilted `rotate(-2deg)` and moving `translateY(2px)` when pressed, which under reduced motion it does not), `secondary`, `small` and `text` (navigation that reads as a link); `disabled` greys it out and drops its shadow. A primitive takes no `className` and no `style`: a new look is a new variant here, never a class at the call site.
- `Choice` (`primitives/choice.tsx`) is every choice: a `chip` (a short value) or an `option` (a whole sentence, which wears a letter A, B, C that a CSS counter draws: `counter-reset` on the `ol.options` that lists them, `counter-increment` on each option, the letter in `::before` with an empty alternative text, so the letter is in no accessible name and in no page text). With `pressed` it is a toggle (`aria-pressed`); without it, an action drawn as a choice, such as a chip given back to its tray. A pressed chip keeps a name inside it readable: the name tag loses its shade and takes the chip's colour. `ChipTray` is a named tray of chips where at most one is picked; every screen that picks values with chips uses it. Its `segmented` variant, for a yes or no, draws two chips as the halves of one pill; the chips keep their names, `aria-pressed` and keyboard.
- `Card` (`primitives/panels/card.tsx`) is every panel, in style B (rounded corners, thick border, shadow below): a `section` or, with `as="li"`, an item of a list; an optional `header` draws the dark stripe on top from data, `{ icon, title, type, meta }` (the caller owns the `h2` it passes as `title`, or keeps it under the stripe, and labels the card with it; see `DESIGN.md`, "Style B"); the `soon` variant holds what is not open yet, faded to half on the student's choice (2026-10-10): its low contrast is deliberate (`DESIGN.md`, "Contrast"), so do not raise it. Inner layout belongs to the content, not to the card.
- `Tabs` (`primitives/panels/tabs.tsx`) is every list of tabs, built on `@radix-ui/react-tabs`: `name` labels the `tablist`, `tabs` is a list of `{ id, label, panel }`, `selected` is the id of the selected tab and `onSelect` receives the id of the one the player picks (the caller owns the selection, as with `ChipTray`). Radix gives the roles, `aria-selected`, the arrow keys with wrap-around, Home and End, one tab stop and the tab panel right after it; it mounts only the panel of the selected tab, so what a panel's content keeps in its own state is lost when another tab is selected, and the caller keeps that state above the tabs. Its look is the classes `tabs`, `tabs__list`, `tabs__tab` and `tabs__panel`, and its `variant` picks one of two (`tabs--underline` is the second): `pills`, the default, draws every tab as a tile (`--radius-tile`, `--stroke-bold`) and fills the selected one with ink, like a pressed chip; `underline`, for the tabs inside a tab, draws plain text on one row under a thin rule, with the chosen tab bold in ink and underlined with `--stroke-heavy` (student's choice B, 2026-10-28). The tabs are always one row (T4-360 = A, 2026-10-24): they share the width when they fit and, when they do not, the list scrolls sideways by itself and the card around it does not (`.tabs` is a single `minmax(0, 1fr)` column, so the list cannot widen its parent), a fade marks the edge that hides tabs (a `mask-image` whose two lengths, `--tabs-fade-start` and `--tabs-fade-end`, a scroll-driven animation sets from the scroll position: none when the tabs fit, only the right edge at the start, only the left at the end, both in between, where the browser has scroll-driven animations) and a tab picked by the arrow keys or a click, whether the browser focuses it or not, is scrolled into view clear of the fade (`scroll-padding-inline`), without the scroll ever getting in the way of a click (the choice is made on the press, before the tab moves). The list pads itself by `--space-2xs` and takes it back with a negative margin, the same trick as `.chip-card__body`, so the focus ring of a tab is not cut by the scroll: its parent needs that much padding, as every `Card` body has. Design for ten tabs, not for the four that exist.
- `Table` (`primitives/panels/table.tsx`) is every table of the game: a `caption` (visually hidden, the card around it shows the title), `columns` as `{ id, label }`, `rows` as `{ id, cells, inUse?, onRemove? }` and the words `emptyText`, `inUseText` and `removeText` as props, since this folder imports no text. A row in use wears a pill with its word, not only a colour; a row with `onRemove` ends in a small `Button` whose accessible name is the remove word followed by the content of its row (`aria-labelledby`); an empty table shows its text under the header. It takes no `className` and no `style`. Its wrapper scrolls (sideways, and up and down inside a card with a height cap, such as one on the map) and draws its own scrollbar. A new primitive and not a variant because no other primitive draws columns: `Card` holds it, `Button` removes a row and `ChipTray` picks the entry (D-3 A of the chapter 2 plan).
- `Dialog` (`primitives/panels/dialog.tsx`) is every window, built on `@radix-ui/react-dialog` and drawn as a `Card`: `name` is the title that names the `dialog`, `closeText` and `closeLabel` are its X, `open` and `onOpenChange` are the caller's, and `modal` goes to Radix (an overlay, the focus trapped and the rest of the page inert; without it only Escape and the X ask to close). It renders where the caller places it and gives no size or place of its own (`DESIGN.md`, row `Dialog`).
- `Message` (`primitives/message.tsx`) is every warning, error, right answer or note the game says in a sentence: a `p` with a `variant` (`note` by default, `success`, `warning` or `error`) and `words`, the word of each kind (they arrive as a prop because this folder imports no text: `MESSAGE_TEXT` in `src/copy/`). In the «Pestaña» style (V18 of the chapter 2 plan, 2026-10-11) it is text in a frame with a folder tab that sticks out above it and says its kind and a mark («NOTA», «✓ CORRECTO», «! AVISO», «✕ ERROR», upper case by style); each kind has its own frame (thin, bold green, dashed, double in vermilion), so the colour is never the only difference. The tab is `aria-hidden` and a visually hidden word before the text names the kind; the message has no role of its own and passes the HTML attributes of a `p` (`role`, `aria-*`, `id`) as `Button` does, with no `className` and no `style`. The tab asks for room above it: the message has `margin-top: var(--space-xl)`, and a parent that zeroes the margins of its paragraphs excludes it (`.chip-card p:not(.message)`). A new primitive and not a variant of `Card`, because a one-line message inside a card is a card in a card (V18 = A).
- `NameTag` (`primitives/name-tag.tsx`) draws a resource's or an actor's own name. The panels (`TaggedText`) and the map labels (`PieceLabel`) both use it. The map labels' look lives in `primitives/primitives.css` too: a label that is only a name wears the name tag's frame, written once for both.
- A caption is the class `caption` (with `caption--soft` for the label of a field, a tray or a role, or the mark of an optional chapter); every `h3` looks like one, and so does the `h2` of a level of the roadmap.
- `tokenPx(name)` reads `tokens.css` itself (`?raw`), so the map and the interface share one source; it is the only reader the barrel exports. `token(name)` stays in `tokens.ts` because `tokenPx` and the contrast tests of `tokens.test.ts` use it, and no other layer imports it.
- `DESIGN.md` at the project root is the guide for people: every token and primitive, when to use each, and how to add a variant instead of a new element. A change here updates it.
- Not here: screens and panels (`src/ui/`), map pieces (`src/scene/`), visible text (`src/copy/`).
- May import: no layer. `src/ui/` and `src/scene/` import it through `index.ts` (`@/design`); `src/styles.css` imports its stylesheets (`tokens.css` and `primitives/primitives.css`).
