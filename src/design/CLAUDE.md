# design

The game's design system: every colour, font, size, space, line, radius and shadow of the interface and the map as a token (`tokens.css`), the reader that lets the 3D map use the same tokens (`tokens.ts`), the interface primitives, one file per family (`choice.tsx` holds `Choice` and `ChipTray`), and their styles with the one focus ring (`primitives.css`).

- `tokens.css` has two layers of colour: the palette (`--palette-*`, raw colours named after what they are) and the roles (`--color-*`, named after what they are for). Styles and the map use roles, never the palette. The other tokens are scales with one layer: fonts and their three weights (`--font-*`, `--weight-*`), sizes of text (`--text-2xs` to `--text-2xl`), spaces on a 4 px grid (`--space-2xs` to `--space-3xl`), lines (`--stroke-*`), radii, shadows and the focus ring. Every size, space and line comes from a scale; a one-off measure that belongs to a single rule (a panel width, the size of a stamp) stays in that rule. The width and the gap of a card on the map are not tokens either: `src/scene/layout.ts` needs them to place the card, and the anchor hands them to `.map-card` as CSS variables (`--card-width`, `--card-gap`, `--card-x`, `--card-y`).
- The weights are the ones `index.html` loads: Plex Sans 400 and 600, Newsreader 500, Plex Mono 400. Newsreader 600 is not loaded: a stamp and a term inside a title ask for 600 and get the browser's bold drawn from 500.
- Vermilion has three roles, today with the same value: `--color-danger` (blocked, intruder, alert, a rejected solution, a wrong answer), `--color-emphasis` (the primary button, the chapter numbers, the stars, the "nuevo" mark, the tooltip shadow) and `--color-focus` (the focus ring). Their value, `#bf370f`, keeps 4.5:1 for text on every paper and for `--color-on-fill` on it, and 3:1 for the focus ring: `tokens.test.ts` asserts those ratios from `tokens.css`, so a new value is checked there before it ships (`DESIGN.md`, "Contrast").
- `Button` (`button.tsx`) is every action and every link: a `<button type="button">`, or an `<a>` when it has an `href`. Its variant is its look: `primary` (the action a panel or card exists for, one per panel), `secondary`, `small` and `text` (navigation that reads as a link); `disabled` greys it out and drops its shadow. A primitive takes no `className` and no `style`: a new look is a new variant here, never a class at the call site.
- `Choice` (`choice.tsx`) is every choice: a `chip` (a short value) or an `option` (a whole sentence). With `pressed` it is a toggle (`aria-pressed`); without it, an action drawn as a choice, such as a chip given back to its tray. A pressed chip keeps a name inside it readable: the name tag loses its shade and takes the chip's colour. `ChipTray` is a named tray of chips where at most one is picked; every screen that picks values with chips uses it.
- `Card` (`card.tsx`) is every panel: a `section` or, with `as="li"`, an item of a list; the `soon` variant holds what is not open yet. Inner layout belongs to the content, not to the card.
- `NameTag` (`name-tag.tsx`) draws a resource's or an actor's own name. The panels (`TaggedText`) and the map labels (`PieceLabel`) both use it. The map labels' look lives in `primitives.css` too: a label that is only a name wears the name tag's frame, written once for both.
- A caption is the class `caption` (with `caption--soft` for the label of a field, a tray or a role); every `h3` looks like one.
- `token(name)` and `tokenPx(name)` read `tokens.css` itself (`?raw`), so the map and the interface share one source; `src/scene/theme.ts` names what the map uses.
- `DESIGN.md` at the project root is the guide for people: every token and primitive, when to use each, and how to add a variant instead of a new element. A change here updates it.
- Not here: screens and panels (`src/ui/`), map pieces (`src/scene/`), visible text (`src/copy/`).
- May import: no layer. `src/ui/` and `src/scene/` import it through `index.ts` (`@/design`); `src/styles.css` imports its stylesheets.
