# levels/02-ports-and-names/on-call

Block 6 of chapter 2, «La guardia»: one `diagnose` incident, 02-14, with seven cases. Nothing in it is taught: it introduces no term, and the player diagnoses with the ladder of the thirteen incidents before it. Task 33 of the plan built it, the last content unit of the chapter.

- `14-on-call-it-does-not-connect.ts` (title «Guardia: no conecta»): the incident. It starts from the world 02-13 leaves (`changes: []`: it changes nothing of its own, so its report has nothing to tell about the world, and says only that each case is apart, «lo que cambió en uno no sigue en el siguiente») and it has no `shown` list of its own: each case carries one, because each case is a screen and draws only what it uses. The player changes nothing in the world, except in case 5, whose table of routes is its fix: a diagnose ends as it started, so the incident after it (04-01) continues from the start world of this one.
- `cases.ts`: the seven cases, built with `onCallCase` (what its terminal can send, then the rest of the draft) and `cause` (the question, the right reply at its place and the two wrong ones around it), so no two share a shape. A case is a set of `changes` on the world of the incident, never on the case before (`caseWorld`), the `shown` list of its pieces, a report that Lucía says (the card writes `Lucía: «…»`), two hints and one question of cause with three options. The right option sits at the place of its sheet, 2nd, 3rd, 1st, 3rd, 1st, 2nd and 3rd, and between the two wrong ones by length (the longest wrong one is longer and the shortest is shorter), so its length gives nothing away. The world of every case is the world of 02-13 (`NAME_FAILURES` is what 02-13 changes) plus its own changes; the change of each is a constant of `../shared.ts` with a JSDoc.
- The seven cases, with the evidence the engine produces (measured by `chapter-2.test.ts`) and what each wrong option refutes:
  1. **Guided.** `SITE_ON_LOOPBACK`: the service of `servidor-nuevo` on TCP 80 listens only on 127.0.0.1. `www.lucia.example` points to `servidor-nuevo` (`198.51.100.60`) since 02-12, so the sheet's «servidor-internet» (`198.51.100.50`, which still serves `tienda.lucia.example`) is `servidor-nuevo` here (ruling of the orchestrator; no record was changed back). One address chip (the name) and three messages, `dig`, `ping` and `nc` to port 80, so the case card lists the three tests of the sequence in order: `dig` gives `198.51.100.60`, the `ping` comes back from `servidor-nuevo`, and the `nc` ends refused (`refused`, a RST from `servidor-nuevo`, «Connection refused»). In that order the numbers are the sheet's: `dig` 51514 → 40001, the question of the `ping` 51515 → 40002, the question of the `nc` 51516 → 40003 and the `nc` 51517 → 40004; the resolver gets three stamps (the `dig` and the two questions) and the sweep of the map places all of them, also at 360 px (the O-10 open point: no DNS question label lacks a place). The box of `servidor-nuevo` lists «TCP 80 · escucha solo en 127.0.0.1», which hint 2 points at. The wrong options: the name does not resolve (the `dig` came back) and a firewall drops it (a firewall says nothing; a RST came).
  2. `wwww.lucia.example` (`HOSTNAME.typo`, `ADDRESS.mistyped`): no change. The `dig` and the question of the `nc` come back from `servidor-dns` with NXDOMAIN, and the `nc` ends `unresolved` at its doorstep, with no port and no hops (its stamp reads «TCP 80 … Perdido»), so nothing goes to a server of the site, which the case does not even draw. The wrong options: the server does not answer on port 80 (the `nc` never left) and `router-oficina` lost a route (the question went through it and came back).
  3. `STALE_SHOP`: the hosts file of `tu-laptop` has `tienda.lucia.example` → `198.51.100.70` (`IP.staleHost`, an IP nobody has) and `servidor-autoritativo` gets an A record of that name to `198.51.100.50`; the report names both. The `dig` gives `198.51.100.50`; the `ping` reads the hosts file, asks nobody (no question is drawn, no row is made) and ends `unreachable` with Destination Host Unreachable from `router-destino`, which the case does not draw and no text names (the box says «Un router de afuera de la oficina…», as in 02-11). The wrong options: the resolver has the old answer in its cache (the `dig` came back with the good IP) and `servidor-internet` is down (the `ping` went to another IP). No IP chip: a `dig` of an IP is meaningless, so the refutation is the IP the `dig` gave.
  4. `DEFAULT_ROUTE_LOST`: `router-oficina` loses its default route and keeps only the route to `10.0.0.0/16`. The `ping` to `198.51.100.50` ends `unreachable` with Destination Net Unreachable from `router-oficina` (`192.168.1.1`); the `ping` to `10.0.0.10` comes back from the printer of the other office. The report scopes its claim to the chips (the ping to the server), since the sheet's «ningún ping sale de la oficina» is false for the second chip. The wrong options: `servidor-internet` is down (the warning is a router's) and the firewall of `tu-laptop` stops the pings (it lets everything out, and the other ping comes back).
  5. `LOOP` (from chapter 1: `router-proveedor` sends everything back to `router-oficina`): the `ping`, the `traceroute` and the pings with TTL 1, 2 and 3 end in `ttl-exceeded`, the notices coming from `router-oficina`, `router-proveedor` and `router-oficina`, and the `traceroute` alternates `192.168.1.1` and `203.0.113.1`. It has a `fix` (`LOOP_FIX`): the table of routes of `router-proveedor` (`PROVIDER_ROUTES_TABLE`: destinations `0.0.0.0/0` and `198.51.100.0/24`, next hops `203.0.113.10 router-oficina` and `203.0.113.2 router-destino`) and the task that the `ping` to `servidor-internet` comes back. The case closes only with the cause named and the task holding, in any order. The reference solution takes the default route away, writes `198.51.100.0/24` towards `router-destino` and pings again. Writing only the `/24` also closes the case (the longest prefix wins, and the default route still sends everything else back to `router-oficina`), because the task is the `ping` and not the table; the right feedback names the loop and says what to do. The map draws `router-destino` (`LOOP_FIX_PIECES`) because the chips of the fix name it. The wrong options: `servidor-internet` is overloaded (the notice is a router's) and `router-oficina` drops what goes to the server (it hands it to the provider, who sends it back).
  6. No change: the world 02-05 leaves already has the firewall of `laptop-lucia` with TCP 22 as its only inbound rule. `nc` to 22 comes back and `nc` to 3000 ends `timeout`, blocked at the firewall of `laptop-lucia`, which is drawn with it and whose box lists «TCP 22 ← tu-laptop». `MESSAGE.ncSsh` is the chip of the 22. The wrong options: nobody listens on 3000 and the app listens only on 127.0.0.1 (both would give a rejection, not silence).
  7. No change: the world 02-13 leaves already has the printer as the DNS server of `laptop-andres`. The `dig` ends `unreachable` with Port Unreachable from the printer (the terminal prints «connection refused» three times, as 02-13 does), and the printer's box lists no service. The diagnose cases offer no «Ajustes de red» card (their pieces open only a terminal, or the table of the fix), so the hints point at the box of the `dig`. The wrong options: the name does not exist (it would be NXDOMAIN) and an authoritative server is down (it would be SERVFAIL, from a resolver); the text says «un servidor autoritativo» and not its name, because `servidor-autoritativo` is not drawn here.
- Rigor of the texts. A report says only what the player sees (a test that does not come back, a warning): a ping, an `nc` or a `dig` that does not come back, and no verb of the web (`abrir`, `cargar`, `entrar`…). No report and no right option names a switched-off device as the cause; a wrong option may say «caído» (the sheet's), and its feedback refutes it with what the map shows; no text says «apagado». Each wrong option is a real mistake whose feedback cites the evidence and never claims what the world does not show. A feedback never opens with «No.», «Eso es.» or similar: the card already prefixes «No es eso.» or «Correcto.». No text names a piece the case does not draw (`chapter-2.test.ts` reads the names of every text of every case against the pieces it draws). Every change a case makes is named in its report or in the feedback of its right cause (`explained-changes.test.ts`): case 1 names `servidor-nuevo`, case 3 `servidor-autoritativo` and `tu-laptop`, case 4 `router-oficina` and case 5 `router-proveedor`, with the sentence of chapter 1 (`LOOP_CAUSE`).
