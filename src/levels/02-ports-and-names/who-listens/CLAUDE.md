# levels/02-ports-and-names/who-listens

Block 1 of chapter 2, «Quién escucha»: the incidents that teach that a device has one IP and many services, and that a port says which one a packet is for. Task 23 of the plan builds 02-01 and task 24 builds 02-02, which closes the block.

- `01-one-machine-many-services.ts` (title «Un equipo, muchos servicios», the title of the approved design): a `send` incident with three messages sent from your laptop (`ORIGIN.you`) to `laptop-lucia`: a ping (`MESSAGE.ping`) and two `nc` (`MESSAGE.ncApp` and `MESSAGE.ncDatabase`, whose chips carry the port: «nc · puerto 3000» and «nc · puerto 5432»). Its only change is `LUCIA_SERVICES`: Lucía put her app (port 3000) and her test database (port 5432) to work on `laptop-lucia`, each on every IP of the laptop. The report names `laptop-lucia` and says what changed, as `explained-behavior-changes` asks, and nothing else of the office changes. It draws `SERVICES_PIECES` (your laptop and `laptop-lucia`; the firewall of your laptop comes with its device). It introduces `service`, `port`, `listen`, `ip-port`, `destination-port` and `nc`.
- Three tasks, each `replied` by `laptop-lucia`: the ping, the `nc` to 3000 and the `nc` to 5432. There is no failure: the contrast is that the ping reaches the device and uses no port, and the `nc` reaches a service. The ping does not complete the `nc` tasks. `ADDRESS.lucia` is the only address chip.
- The right explanation is the second option (the incident sheet: 01 = 2nd). The question is «El ping a laptop-lucia volvió. ¿Por qué eso no prueba que su app funciona?». The wrong options are a service with an IP of its own (the IP is the device's and the port tells the services apart) and a ping that tests the port of another service (the ping uses no ports, so it tests none: its feedback says only that, and that it shows that `laptop-lucia` answers). The sheet's second wrong option, a ping that did not reach, was replaced on 2026-10-12 because the question itself says the ping came back, so nobody could believe it. The three options of 02-01 use the new terms too (servicio, puerto) and are about the same length (82, 81 and 82 characters of plain text), so the right one, the second, is not the longest; `chapter-2.test.ts` checks the place of the right option and that it is not the longest, and that no wrong option says the ping did not arrive.
- What the box and the card show here is what the player knows at 02-01: `port` and `destination-port` are known, `tcp`, `source-port` and `handshake` are not. So the packet of an `nc` is labelled «paquete al puerto 3000» on the map and in its box, and the box lists «Encabezado de ida» as `192.168.1.10 → 192.168.1.11:3000` (the port of the service, and only the IP of your laptop) and the TTL, with no greeting. «Encabezado de vuelta» is `192.168.1.11:3000 → 192.168.1.10`: the box gates each port by its role in the connection, the service's by `destination-port` and the client's by `source-port`, in the request and in the answer, so it shows the port of your laptop only once `source-port` is known, which is 02-02 (decided on 2026-10-12, so the box does what the label of the packet does; `src/game/view/CLAUDE.md` has the rule and `inspection.test.ts` the two tests). The `IP:puerto` is written in the text of the tasks (`{{ip-port|192.168.1.11:3000}}`), in the header of the box and in the line `Ncat: Connected to 192.168.1.11:3000.` of the output card.
- The output card prints the lines of `ncat` 7.93 (`Ncat: Version 7.93 ( https://nmap.org/ncat )`, `Ncat: Connected to <ip>:<port>.` and the bytes line) and no `[tcp/...]`: `src/copy/tools/CLAUDE.md` has the source of each line and the date it was read.
- The takeaway says the lesson in one sentence and then the two facts about the tool (decided D-5): in Amazon Linux 2023 `nc` is installed with `sudo dnf install nmap-ncat`, and in macOS `nc` is another program and prints other lines. Both facts live in `src/copy/tools/CLAUDE.md`. The first was read in the repository of Amazon Linux 2023 and in its package list on 2026-10-12 (`nmap-ncat` 7.93-4.amzn2023, which provides `nc`) and was not run in an EC2, so the text only says how to install it and claims nothing about the standard image, whose list the user guide gives for an older AMI. The second was run on a Mac on 2026-10-11 (the open case was not tried), so the text says «se ve distinta» and not what it prints.
- No text names a term a later incident of the chapter introduces (`tcp`, `connection`, `closed-port`, `listen-address`, `route`, `dns`…): the report says a service «escucha» in a port and never «dirección de escucha», and it says nothing about how a connection opens. `chapter-2.test.ts` checks it with `a text of an incident names no term that a later incident of chapter 2 introduces`.
- Not here: the chapter's world, chips and pieces (`../shared.ts`), the contract (`src/levels/contract/`), visible UI text and the reasons the box shows (`src/copy/`), the engine.
- May import: `@/engine`, `@/copy`, `@/levels/contract/incident` and `@/levels/02-ports-and-names/shared`.
- `02-opening-a-connection.ts` (title «Abrir una conexión»): a `send` incident with two messages sent from your laptop to `laptop-lucia`, the same `nc` chips as 02-01 (`MESSAGE.ncApp` and `MESSAGE.ncDatabase`) and no ping. It has `changes: []` and so starts from the solved world of 02-01 (the sends of 02-01 change nothing): the report names no device because nothing changed, and `shown` is `SERVICES_PIECES` again. It introduces `client-server`, `tcp`, `connection`, `handshake`, `source-port` and `ephemeral-port`.
- Two tasks (`TASK.openApp` and `TASK.openDatabase`), each `replied` by `laptop-lucia`; both must hold to close the incident, and a second `nc` to the same port opens a new connection and completes nothing else. The player sends the app first in the reference solution, so the connections leave from 51514 and 51515 (decided 6 of the plan: the first connection of a device leaves from 51514 and each new one adds one). Those numbers are the game's, so the takeaway says «Los puertos de origen que viste son números del juego: cada sistema elige los suyos» and no text says that a real system adds one or gives a range of ephemeral ports as a number; the hint says only that the port of the second `nc` changed.
- The box lists «Encabezado de ida» as `192.168.1.10:51514 → 192.168.1.11:3000`, «Encabezado de vuelta» with the ports swapped (`192.168.1.11:3000 → 192.168.1.10:51514`) and «Saludo de tres pasos» (SYN, SYN-ACK and ACK); the packet is labelled «TCP 51514 → 3000» on the map and in its box, because `tcp` and `source-port` are known (the end-to-end test of the label is in `tests/e2e/chapter-2/`).
- The right explanation is the first option (the incident sheet: 02 = 1st). The question is «¿A qué puerto vuelve la respuesta de laptop-lucia?». The wrong options are the ones of the sheet: the port of the service, which is the destination port of the request and where the answer leaves from, and a port that `laptop-lucia` chooses when it answers (the answer goes to the source port that the request carried). The three options are about the same length, so the right one is not the longest, and the wrong ones also use the new terms (a wrong option names the «puerto efímero» as one that `laptop-lucia` chooses), so the right one is not the only option that sounds like the incident.
- Client and server are roles of one exchange and not kinds of device: the report says «aquí, tu-laptop» and «aquí, laptop-lucia» and says they are roles of this connection; in 02-03 `laptop-lucia` will be the client of an `nc`. No text says why a reply comes back through a firewall (decided 16; `chapter-2.test.ts` checks the incident texts, the box of both packets, the output of `nc` and the definitions of its terms) and the incident never says «firewall».
