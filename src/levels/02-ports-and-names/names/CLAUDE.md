# levels/02-ports-and-names/names

Block 5 of chapter 2, «Nombres»: the incidents that teach how a device finds the IP of a name. Task 29 of the plan built 02-10; 02-11 to 02-13 arrive with their tasks (30 to 32) and add their files and their notes here.

- `10-asking-for-a-name.ts` (title «Preguntar por un nombre»): a `send` incident from your laptop with `dig` and `ping` as the messages and the name `www.lucia.example` (`ADDRESS.www`) as the only address. Because the address is the only one, the terminal draws no tray for it: it lists the two messages and «Enviar» (the e2e play and the browser test pick the message and send, with no address to click). It introduces `domain-name`, `dns`, `dns-query`, `resolver`, `udp`, `dig` and `dhcp`. Its changes are `NAME_SERVERS` (`shared.ts`): `servidor-dns` (the resolver, `198.51.100.8`, UDP and TCP 53, which asks `servidor-autoritativo` for the zone `lucia.example`) and `servidor-autoritativo` (`198.51.100.53`, UDP and TCP 53, the zone with one address, `www.lucia.example` A `198.51.100.50`, the server of the internet, with the time to live of the game) appear in the internet; `router-destino` gets one interface for each, `198.51.100.2` and `198.51.100.3` (an update replaces the list, so the action restates `DESTINATION_INTERFACES`, exported by chapter 1, before the two new links); and `tu-laptop` gets the resolver as its DNS server. The servers are pieces an action only adds, so the report does not have to name them; `tu-laptop` already existed, so the report names it and tells the cause: the name and the DNS are explained in plain words first and named after («Ahora tu-laptop puede usar un nombre de dominio…», «Ese servidor es su resolver. Es servidor-dns, en 198.51.100.8.»), then «Apareció en «Ajustes de red» de tu-laptop porque se lo prestó DHCP.» (the sheet's sentence, in the order the text review asked for), and it says, before the player sees it, that a `ping` by name asks first and that each question takes a new source port. `changedNames` of the two worlds is exactly `[tu-laptop]` (`chapter-2.test.ts`). It draws `NAMES_PIECES`: `tu-laptop` with its firewall, `router-oficina`, the globe, `servidor-dns` and `servidor-internet`. The authoritative server is in the world and in the resolver's `upstreams`, and is not drawn and not named by any text until 02-11, where its term arrives (`chapter-2.test.ts` checks both).
  - The card of `tu-laptop` has three tabs, «Terminal» first, «Ajustes de red» and «Tabla de rutas». «Ajustes de red» is the read-only card: the incident offers no setting to pick, and the card shows the DNS server of the device (`Device.dnsServer`) with the line «Lo prestó DHCP.» once `dhcp` is known (`dnsLineOf` in `src/game/view/cards/settings-card.ts`). `send.opensFirst` cannot name a read-only card (see `src/levels/contract/CLAUDE.md`), so the report says which tab to open, as 02-08 does. `servidor-dns` is a locked piece: its records and its cache are tabs of 02-11 and 02-12.
  - What the player sees, measured by `chapter-2.test.ts` (and, for the three lines of the «DNS» field of a `ping` by name, by `src/game/view/inspection.test.ts` › `the dns field of a ping or an nc by name says the device asked its resolver first, or that its hosts file answered`). **The numbers** follow the order of the sends. With the `dig` first, the `dig` is the datagram itself: `UDP 51514 → 53`, translated to `203.0.113.10:40001`, and the `ping` that follows asks its own question from 51515 (row 51515 → 40002). With the `ping` first, its question takes 51514 → 40001 and the `dig` leaves from 51515 → 40002. The `ping` has no port and leaves no row; its box says the IP changed and that ICMP has no ports. The table of translations of `router-oficina` lists `TCP · fija` first (the forward of 02-09 stays in the world) and then one `UDP · consulta DNS` row for each question or `dig`. **The box of a `dig`** is the box of a UDP datagram to port 53 as the view builds it: a «DNS» field («Contestó servidor-dns.» and `www.lucia.example A 198.51.100.50`), the four headers with ports, «Traducción» and «Consulta y respuesta» with two lines, the query and the answer, and no «Saludo de tres pasos». It does not carry the labels the sheet drew («Consulta DNS», «Respuesta», «El resolver buscó la respuesta por ti»): the view was built before the incident, in Tasks 16 and 28 quater, and this task did not change it. **The box of the `ping`** keeps the three lines of its «DNS» field («Antes de mandar el paquete, tu-laptop averigua la IP de www.lucia.example.», the question and its answer, and «La consulta salió del puerto 51514 y se ve en el mapa, antes de este paquete.»). The `dig` that follows a `ping` leaves from 51515 and its own box does not say why; the cause is in the box of the `ping`, in the report («En este juego, cada pregunta sale de un puerto de origen nuevo»), in the takeaway («En este juego, cada consulta, la de un ping y la de un dig, sale de un puerto de origen nuevo: por eso la segunda no repite el de la primera», then why the row exists, because the resolver is outside the office and `router-oficina` translates it, and why the ping leaves none, because ICMP has no ports and in that table each row carries one) and in the rule of 02-02 («Para cada conexión nueva, tu-laptop elige uno»).
  - **The cache.** The `ping` after a `dig` still sends its question, and the resolver answers it from what the `dig` made it keep (`question.trace.dns.fromCache`); no text and no box of 02-10 says «caché», because its term is 02-12's (`chapter-2.test.ts` reads every text and every box after a `dig` and a `ping`).
  - Tasks: `TASK.digWww` (the `dig` comes back and its answer holds `198.51.100.50`, `answerTaskFrom` in `shared.ts`) and `TASK.pingWww` (the `ping` comes back from `servidor-internet`). The question is «¿Por qué dig no hizo el saludo de tres pasos antes de preguntar?» and the right option is the second (the sheet: 10 = 2nd): UDP sends each message loose. The wrong ones are the sheet's, DNS uses no ports (the query goes to port 53) and the resolver already had an open connection (UDP has none); their feedback corrects them. The right option is 46 characters of plain text and the longest wrong one 57, so its length gives nothing away. The feedback of the right one says the query «suele» go by UDP, that the server also listens on TCP and that no query of the game goes by it (RFC 7766 §5, read on 2026-10-11, in `src/engine/dns/CLAUDE.md`).
  - Rigor (the sheet's, guarded by `chapter-2.test.ts`): no text says that the computer asks «una sola vez» or that the query is the only one (a real `ping` or `nc` sends two questions, A and AAAA, from the same socket, and the game draws the one for the A record: `src/engine/dns/CLAUDE.md`); IPv6, AAAA, the authoritative server, records, the cache, the time to live, NXDOMAIN, SERVFAIL, the hosts file and Port Unreachable are named nowhere; the names of `lucia.example` are fictitious and the takeaway says so (`.example` is not delegated in the root zone, D-13, verified on 2026-10-11); DHCP is a line of the card and nothing more.
