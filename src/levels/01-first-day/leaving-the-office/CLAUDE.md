# levels/01-first-day/leaving-the-office

Block 3 of chapter 1, «Salir de la oficina», as far as it is built: the incidents that show what lives inside a local network, what takes a packet to another one, and what a device needs to know to send it there. 01-06 and 01-07 are `send` incidents with one ping message (`MESSAGE.ping`) sent from your laptop; 01-08 to 01-10 join them in the next part of the block.

- `06-your-network-and-the-router.ts`: `TWO_FLOORS` (in `../shared.ts`) adds the second floor (`laptop-andres`, `192.168.2.15`), the office router `router-oficina` (`192.168.1.1` on your floor and `192.168.2.1` on the other) and the default gateway of every device. The player pings the printer (straight, in your local network) and `laptop-andres` (through the router, so the box shows «Pasó por»); a third address, `laptop-lucia`, is there to compare. The box of `router-oficina` shows its IP on each floor. It introduces `lan` and `router`. The rule «aquí la red es 192.168.1.x» is given in the report, with no mask. No text says `salto`, `TTL` or `puerta de enlace predeterminada`: those arrive later.
- `07-who-gets-whats-outside.ts`: a `setGateway` takes the gateway of your laptop away («reinstalaron tu laptop»). The player pings `laptop-andres` and sees it does not leave your laptop, then sets the gateway in the «Ajustes de red» card of your laptop, a tray of chips (`Ninguna`, the IP `router-oficina` has on your floor, `192.168.1.1`, which its box shows, the printer `192.168.1.21` and the unowned `192.168.1.50`) that is not in its terminal. The printer as a gateway receives the packet and does not forward it (it is not a router), and an IP nobody has makes your laptop warn. It introduces `default-gateway`, a saved setting of the device. The wrong option that blames the router does not say it was off: it says it was not working, and the feedback refutes it with what the map shows (the packet never left your laptop).
- Both draw the same pieces (`OFFICE_PIECES`, their `shown` list): `tu-laptop`, `laptop-lucia`, the printer, `laptop-andres` and the router; `laptop-marta` (with its hidden firewall) and `laptop-visita` stay in the world and are not drawn. There is no internet and no router of a provider.
