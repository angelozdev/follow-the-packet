# levels/01-first-day

Chapter 1, «Tu primer día» (level 0, Bases), your first day at the office with Lucía, in fourteen sub-chapters grouped in five blocks, each in its own folder (`where-you-are/`, then `the-ping/`, `leaving-the-office/`, `the-guard/` and `first-watch/` as they are built): one file per incident (`NN-<slug>.ts`, a default export built with `defineIncident`), and `shared.ts` with the chapter's lesson, the base world (floor 1 of the office, `192.168.1.0/24`: your laptop, Lucía's, Marta's, the printer and the visitor's laptop with no cable) and the chips the incidents share.

- 01-01 brings the base world; every later incident brings only its changes over the reference solution of the one before. The chapter is being rebuilt block by block: until the last block, `04-01` introduces the chapter 1 terms that no incident of the chapter introduces yet (`CHAPTER_1_BRIDGE` in `src/levels/04-iaas/shared.ts`: after block 2 it holds `internet`, `router`, `private-ip`, `firewall`, `traffic` and `rule`), and each block takes its own out of that list.
- A device's own name is its hostname (`tu-laptop`, `laptop-lucia`, `impresora`), like the `Name` of an EC2 instance: a proper name with no article, so a generated sentence reads «el firewall de laptop-lucia». The texts written here name a device as its piece shows it, marked with `nameTag` (`laptop-visita`, `laptop-marta`, `tu-laptop`, and «la impresora»), never as «la laptop de la visita».
- Texts tag their terms (`{{packet|paquete}}`) and use only terms introduced by this incident or an earlier one; no text names an AWS term, which arrive in chapter 4.
- Not here: the incident contract and chips (`src/levels/contract/`), the registry (`src/levels/`), visible UI text (`src/copy/`), network rules (`src/engine/`).
- May import: `@/engine`, `@/copy`, `@/levels/contract/incident` and `./shared` (from a block folder, `@/levels/01-first-day/shared`).
