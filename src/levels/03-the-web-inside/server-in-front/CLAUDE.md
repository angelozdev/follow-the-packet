# levels/03-the-web-inside/server-in-front

Block 3 of chapter 3, «El servidor de delante»: the incidents that teach what a reverse proxy does between the client and the app (03-07 to 03-09). Only the first part of 03-07 is built; the burst of `casa-lucia`, the limit and the setting of the client IP come with the engine and the screens they need, and 03-08 and 03-09 come after them.

- `07-a-server-in-front.ts` (title «Un servidor delante»): a `send` incident from `tu-laptop` with one message, `curl -i`, and one whole url, `PROXY_URL` (`http://www.lucia.example/pedidos?id=7`, by name, no port). It introduces `reverse-proxy`, `origin-server`, `x-forwarded-for` and `status-429`. Its change is `SERVER_IN_FRONT` (`../shared.ts`), four actions: `servidor-app` (`198.51.100.61`) appears with the orders app of 03-06 on port 3000 (same pages, same book, same users) and `router-destino` gets its interface (`198.51.100.6`; an update replaces the list, so it restates `NEWS_INTERFACES`, which chapter 2 exports, before the new link); the process of the orders app of `laptop-lucia` ends (a `setProcess`, so her port 3000 refuses); and the web service of `servidor-nuevo` on port 80 is restated with its two sites, the shop, which keeps `/` and now forwards `/pedidos`, `/admin` and `/api` to `servidor-app:3000` (`Site.proxy`), and the blog, as 03-04 left it (`setWebService` replaces the sites, so dropping the blog would be a change nobody told). `changedNames` of the two worlds is exactly `[laptop-lucia, servidor-nuevo]`: `servidor-app` is a piece the changes only add and `router-destino` only gains its wiring. It draws `PROXY_PIECES`: `tu-laptop` with its firewall, `servidor-dns`, `router-oficina`, the globe, `servidor-nuevo` and `servidor-app`.
  - One task (`TASK.ordersThroughTheProxyIs200`, a `statusTask`): the `curl -i` of `tu-laptop` to `PROXY_URL` is `replied` with 200. That `servidor-app` generated it is not a task yet (`expect.generatedBy` is later); `chapter-3.test.ts` proves it from the trace (`Trace.app.generatedBy` and `Trace.app.upstream`, and the box says «La app de servidor-app»). Before the change the same url gets the 404 of the shop, because `servidor-nuevo` serves only `/`.
  - The text is already the final text of 03-07: the report, the hints, the question, the options and the takeaway tell the whole incident, the burst of 20 from `casa-lucia`, the limit of 10 per second per IP at the origin, the 429 and the setting of where the app reads the client IP, which the engine and the cards do not give the player yet. The right option is the second: «Antes del cambio, ¿por qué tu-laptop recibía 429 si mandó una sola petición?» separates who counts the requests (the origin, which saw every request with the IP of the proxy) from who forwards them (the proxy), and the wrong ones say the proxy limits or that `tu-laptop` sent too many.
  - Rigor kept in the text, each pinned by `chapter-3.test.ts`: no text says the proxy limits the requests (the 429 is generated and counted by `servidor-app`, and `servidor-nuevo` passes it on), none says every proxy keeps the `Host` header (the text does not speak of `Host`), the client can write its own `X-Forwarded-For` and the origin of the game takes the last entry, the one its proxy added (a real program may read it another way), a limit per IP also joins the office (every device of it leaves with `203.0.113.10`), many new apps start with the app on `127.0.0.1:3000` behind the web server, on the same machine (here it has its own machine so both connections show), and the map does not draw the name lookup of `casa-lucia`. No text names a 5xx code, `idle timeout`, a load balancer or `/api` (a name of chapter 16: the paths the report lists are `/pedidos` and `/admin`, «como» marks them as examples).
  - The pieces are locked in this incident (no card is offered yet): their buttons read «servidor-nuevo con candado» and «servidor-app con candado», and carry their roles in their accessible description, «proxy inverso y servidor web» and «app, servidor de origen»; the map marks `servidor-nuevo` as a proxy and `servidor-app` as an app.
