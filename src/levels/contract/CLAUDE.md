# levels/contract

What an incident is, and what its chips can do: the zod schema, types and constructor of an incident (`incident.ts`: `incidentSchema`, `defineIncident`, `ruleIdFor`, the flows, tasks, chips and player moves) and the chips (`chips.ts`: the rule two chips name and the chips a rule has, the packet three send chips make (`packetFromChips`, with the TTL of the message) or, for a traceroute, the probe it repeats (`probeFromChips`), every combination of send chips, the check that no combination leaves an office with no routers, and the checks over ids that name something in a world: the ids a task expects, the devices whose card an identify incident offers, and the ids of the list of shown pieces).

- The contract is the only place that says what an incident file may hold; the incident files and the registry read it, they never extend it from outside.
- A send task's `expect.outcome` is one of `OUTCOMES` of the engine, `reached` or `any`; its `by` is an id, and the contract cannot say what it names, because the world may come from the incidents before it: the registry test does (`unknownExpectedIds`).
- An identify incident (`identify.cards`) has one card per device; a card's trays have unique ids and unique chip ids, at least two chips each, and a `segmented` tray has exactly two. A tray's `initialPick` names one of its chips, and an `initialPickRule` is only accepted next to an `initialPick`. Every task and every move of the solution names a device, a tray and a chip some card offers, and an identify incident has no flows. The cards' device ids are checked against the world by the registry (`unknownCardDeviceIds`), as `shown` is.
- `shown` is an optional list of the ids of the world (devices, routers and actors, a server of the internet included) that the incident draws; without it the incident draws the whole world, as chapter 4 does. It has at least one id and none repeated. A firewall is not listed: it is drawn with its device. Whether the ids exist in the world, and whether the list holds what the incident uses, are the registry's checks (`unknownShownIds`, and `missingShownIds`: the sender of every flow or origin chip, the owner of every address it sends to, and whoever a task expects, a firewall through its device), because the world may come from the incidents before it.
- Not here: incident content (`src/levels/NN-<chapter>/`), the registry, the world chain and the term ladder (`src/levels/`), game-session state (`src/game/`).
- May import: `@/engine`, `@/copy`, and other files of this folder (`./x`).
