# tests/e2e/chapter-2

The end-to-end test of chapter 2 (the fourteen incidents 02-01 to 02-14).

- `chapter-2-play.ts` says how each incident is played with the mouse: `CHAPTER_2`, a list of `PlayedIncident` (`id`, `title`, `play`) in game order, with the fourteen incidents of the chapter (02-01 to 02-14). The last one opens 03-01 (04-01 before chapter 3 was registered). Content tasks 23 to 28 added the incident they built to the list. The content tasks 29 to 33 only wrote the exported play function of their incident (`playAskingForAName` for 02-10, `playTheRecords` for 02-11, `playStillTheOldIp` for 02-12, `playItDoesNotResolve` for 02-13, `playOnCallItDoesNotConnect` for 02-14) and left `CHAPTER_2` as it was, because only the tests a task names may change; Task 34 appended the five functions (02-10 to 02-14) to `CHAPTER_2`, with the titles the roadmap shows (02-14 is «Guardia: no conecta»), and renamed the chapter test. `PlayedIncident` is the type of chapter 1 (`@e2e/chapter-1/chapter-1-play`): the shape of a played incident is the same in every chapter.
- `chapter-2.spec.ts` holds the only e2e test of the chapter, `chapter 2 is played from 02-01 to 02-14, each incident opening the next, with 3 stars each`: it starts with a star on 01-14, opens the first incident from the roadmap, plays `CHAPTER_2` in order, each incident opening the next, then, in a step, `03-01 opens after 02-14 has a star`, follows «Siguiente incidente» from the result of 02-14 to the first incident of chapter 3 and sees it open on the roadmap, and ends on the roadmap with three stars on every incident. It took 11 s on 2026-10-21 (`test.slow()` gives it 180 s).
- How the incidents are played: 02-01 and 02-02 send the `nc` messages of the terminal; 02-03 sends the `nc` to the app of `laptop-lucia`, which is refused, opens the panel of `laptop-lucia`, picks «Todas las IPs» in its tab «Ajustes» (which clears the stamps) and sends the four tests again (`sendNc`: address and message in the open terminal); 02-04 answers the question of the block and the mask and puts the printer and `laptop-andres` in their bins (`openTheClassifyCard`, `placeInBin`); 02-05 opens the firewall of `laptop-lucia` and picks `TCP · 22` and `tu-laptop`; 02-06 reads the table of routes of `tu-laptop`; 02-07 writes the two routes of `router-oficina` (`addRoute`: pick a destination, pick a next hop, «Agregar ruta») and sends the three pings again, because writing a route clears the stamps; 02-08 sends the `nc` to port 80 from `tu-laptop` and `laptop-lucia` and reads the table of translations of `router-oficina`; 02-09 sends the `nc` from `casa-lucia`, an actor piece (`openTerminal(page, HOME)`); 02-10 (`playAskingForAName`) sends the `dig` and the `ping` to the name from the terminal of `tu-laptop`, which has no address chip to pick because the name is its only address, and answers the question about UDP. 02-11 (`playTheRecords`) opens the panel of `servidor-autoritativo`, tries the alias of `blog` while it has its A record and reads the reason in the `status` region of the table, takes away the wrong A of `blog` and writes its CNAME, takes away the wrong A of `www` and writes the right one (`addRecord`: pick the name, the type and the value, each in its tray, which is a `group` named «Nombre», «Tipo» or «Valor» because `www.lucia.example` is a chip of two trays; `removeRecord`: the button «Quitar» followed by the row), sends the `nc` to `www.lucia.example` and the `dig` to `blog.lucia.example` from the terminal of `tu-laptop` (which now has a tray of addresses, so `sendNc` picks the address and then the message) and answers the question about the CNAME. 02-12 (`playStillTheOldIp`) sends the `dig` from the terminal of `tu-laptop`, which has one message and one address and so draws no tray for either (the card shows the line `dig www.lucia.example` and «Enviar»; a tray with a single chip is fixed and not shown, so the play presses only «Enviar»), waits five minutes with the chip «Esperar 5 min» of the clock «Reloj del juego» in the top strip, sends the `dig` again, opens the panel of `servidor-dns`, whose cache is its only table, reads the row with the new IP and answers the question about the cache. 02-13 (`playItDoesNotResolve`) opens the terminal of `tu-laptop`, which has a tray of four names and a tray of two messages (`sendNc`: the address and then the message), sends the `dig` to `noexiste.lucia.example`, `www.noticias.example` and `tienda.lucia.example` and the `ping` to `tienda.lucia.example`, opens the terminal of `laptop-andres` (`openTerminal(page, ANDRES)`; its label is a button named exactly `laptop-andres`), sends the `dig` to `www.lucia.example`, which the printer answers with Port Unreachable and answers the question about the hosts file. It never clicks `servidor-autoritativo-noticias`; a play that has to find that piece does it with `getByRole('button', { name: 'servidor-autoritativo-noticias con candado' })`, because at 360 px the text of its label is the cut text followed by the whole name and no `.name-tag` text finds it. 02-14 (`playOnCallItDoesNotConnect`) plays the seven cases in a loop: it opens the terminal of the origin (`tu-laptop`, and `laptop-andres` in case 7), sends the tests of the case and names the cause with `closeCase` (`@e2e/closing`). Case 1 picks the `dig`, the `ping` and the `nc` to port 80 in the tray of messages (the name is its only address, so no tray of addresses); case 2 the `dig` and the `nc`; case 3 the `dig` and the `ping`; case 4 picks the two addresses (`sendTo`, with the `ping` as its only message and so no tray of messages); case 5 sends the `ping` and the `traceroute`, then opens `router-proveedor` (a button named exactly `router-proveedor`; its panel is a region of the same name whose only table is its routes, as a case with a fix draws it), takes away the row `0.0.0.0/0` («Quitar 0.0.0.0/0…»), writes `198.51.100.0/24` towards `203.0.113.2 router-destino` (`addRoute`) and pings again, because writing a route clears the stamps; case 6 sends the `nc` to port 22 and to port 3000; case 7 has one address and one message, so the terminal of `laptop-andres` shows no tray at all and the play presses only «Enviar». Since Task 34 the five are in `CHAPTER_2` and the chapter test plays them; before it, a task that wrote one of them ran it by appending 02-10 and the ones after it up to that one to a local copy of `chapter-2-play.ts`, because each incident opens the next.
- Since Task M20 of the 2D plan the tests of the labels of the packets of 02-01, 02-02 and 02-03, of the still drawing of a translated packet and of the unlock of 02-01 are Testing Library tests (`src/ui/incident/incident-screen.test.tsx` and `src/app.test.tsx`); the label of a translated packet in flight is `the label of a translated packet changes at the router` in `src/scene/traffic/flight/flight.browser.test.tsx` (Task M14). The rest of what Task 34 lists is not here: the states of 02-10 and `in 02-14 case 1 no label or stamp overlaps after the dig, the ping and the nc by name`, `in 02-07 the map and its labels fit the area at full size` and the sentinel of the «+N» are in `map-sweep.test.ts`; `in 02-07 and 02-08 the inspection box of a piece covers no open card, at 1280×800 and at 1440×900`, `the cards of tables, output and services cover no piece of the map` and `in 02-13 at 360 px the incident screen does not scroll sideways` are in `src/ui/incident/geometry/`; `in 02-12 waiting from the clock card changes the answer of dig`, `in 02-03 the services card opens from the label of laptop-lucia and switches the address` and `in 02-14 case 5 can be repaired from the routes card` are in `incident-screen.test.tsx`; `the roadmap lists the fourteen incidents of chapter 2 with their stars` is in `src/ui/roadmap.test.tsx`.
- A send by name (a `ping` or an `nc` to a name, from 02-10) draws its question to the resolver first, and the question counts as one more stamp at the resolver: its stamp opens the box of a UDP datagram to port 53. The map keeps only the last send of each combination (`Session.sent`), so the resolver gets one question stamp for each combination sent by name, besides the stamps of the incident's other sends, and a «+N» can absorb some of them. The chapter test plays sends by name from 02-10 on, and a test that counts the stamps of the resolver must count those of the questions too (`drawnSends`, `src/game/flow-runs.ts`); the check of the question in flight is `a ping by name draws the question to the resolver first, and the ping by hosts draws none` in `src/scene/traffic/flight/flight.browser.test.tsx`, and the same on the real screen of 02-10 is `in 02-10 a ping by name draws the question first and the ping after it, each with its stamp` in `src/ui/incident/geometry/sends-in-flight.browser.test.tsx`.
