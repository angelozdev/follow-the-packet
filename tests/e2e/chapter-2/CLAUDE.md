# tests/e2e/chapter-2

The end-to-end tests of chapter 2 (the fourteen incidents 02-01 to 02-14).

- `chapter-2-play.ts` says how each incident is played with the mouse: `CHAPTER_2`, a list of `PlayedIncident` (`id`, `title`, `play`) in game order, with the incidents built so far (02-01). `LAST_BUILT_OF_CHAPTER_2` is the id of the last one, which opens 04-01 until 02-14 exists, so the specs that open 04-01 read it and a content task that adds an incident moves them with the list. Each content task (23 to 33 of the plan) adds the incident it builds and, with it, a smoke test of its block; the whole-chapter test (task 34) plays the list from 02-01 to 02-14, each incident opening the next with three stars. `PlayedIncident` is the type of chapter 1 (`@e2e/chapter-1/chapter-1-play`): the shape of a played incident is the same in every chapter.
- The shared helpers (`cards`, `closing`, `console-errors`, `map-boxes`, `saved-progress`) stay in `tests/e2e/` and are imported through the alias `@e2e/` (`@e2e/cards`), never with `../`. The progress that opens a chapter with the ones before it done is in `@e2e/saved-progress` (`CHAPTER_1_DONE`, `CHAPTER_2_DONE`).
- `chapter-2.spec.ts` holds the smoke tests: that 02-01 opens with a star on 01-14 and 04-01 stays closed, that 02-01 is played from the roadmap and earns 3 stars, and, in «Sin animaciones» (a still scene, waited with `standStill` and no fixed wait), the label of the packet of an `nc` on the map. That label is drawn on the HTML layer as `.packet-tag`, with `aria-hidden`, so it has no role and the test reads it by its class; the stamp of the packet, which a screen reader hears, carries the same words. At 02-01 the label says «paquete al puerto 3000», not «TCP 51514 → 3000»: the label waits for its terms (`tcp` and `source-port` arrive in 02-02), as `transportLabel` in `src/copy/packet-labels.ts` writes it. Measured on 2026-10-12 at 1280×800 and 1440×900: the still drawing put the tags of two packets that follow the same path (the two `nc` of 02-01 to `laptop-lucia`) at the same place, so «paquete al puerto 3000» and «paquete al puerto 5432» were drawn one over the other (172×19 px at the same x, y). Now `spreadTags` moves them apart as `spreadStamps` does the stamps, and `in 02-01 no piece label, floor name, stamp or packet label of the still map overlaps another` (one `describe` per window, `standStill`, no wait) checks the boxes of `packetBoxes` (`map-boxes.ts`: piece labels, floor names, stamps and the tags, which `mapBoxes` does not hold) with `pairsThatOverlap`; it fails on both windows without the shift. Measured on 2026-10-12 at 1280×800 and 1440×900, before `spreadTags` knew the floor names: the tag of a lone `nc` lay over the floor name «Piso 1» (1280×800: floor name x 343 to 374, y 435 to 474, tag x 313 to 485, y 436 to 455), and with two `nc` the left tag did. Now `spreadTags` keeps a tag off the floor names (`clearOfNames`, `src/scene/layout.ts`), so the same test checks the boxes of `packetBoxes`, which hold the floor names, after the first `nc` (one tag) and after the second (two), and that each tag stays inside the area of the map: with the floor names back in the check it fails on both windows after the first `nc` (`Piso 1 / paquete al puerto 3000`), and passes with the shift (tag x 384 to 556 with one `nc`; x 161 to 333 and 405 to 577 with two, 1280×800).
- Not here: a helper that more than one chapter uses (`tests/e2e/`), the captures (`tests/e2e/snapshots/`), what chapter 1 plays (`tests/e2e/chapter-1/`).
