# tests/e2e/chapter-2

The end-to-end tests of chapter 2 (the fourteen incidents 02-01 to 02-14).

- `chapter-2-play.ts` says how each incident is played with the mouse: `CHAPTER_2`, a list of `PlayedIncident` (`id`, `title`, `play`) in game order, with the incidents built so far (02-01 to 02-03). `LAST_BUILT_OF_CHAPTER_2` is the id of the last one, which opens 04-01 until 02-14 exists, so the specs that open 04-01 read it and a content task that adds an incident moves them with the list. Each content task (23 to 33 of the plan) adds the incident it builds and, with it, a smoke test of its block; the whole-chapter test (task 34) plays the list from 02-01 to 02-14, each incident opening the next with three stars. `PlayedIncident` is the type of chapter 1 (`@e2e/chapter-1/chapter-1-play`): the shape of a played incident is the same in every chapter.
- The shared helpers (`cards`, `closing`, `console-errors`, `map-boxes`, `saved-progress`) stay in `tests/e2e/` and are imported through the alias `@e2e/` (`@e2e/cards`), never with `../`. The progress that opens a chapter with the ones before it done is in `@e2e/saved-progress` (`CHAPTER_1_DONE`, `CHAPTER_2_DONE`).
- `chapter-2.spec.ts` holds the smoke tests: that 02-01 opens with a star on 01-14 and 04-01 stays closed, that 02-01 is played from the roadmap and earns 3 stars, and, in «Sin animaciones» (a still scene, waited with `standStill` and no fixed wait), the label of the packet of an `nc` on the map. That label is drawn on the HTML layer as `.packet-tag`, with `aria-hidden`, so it has no role and the test reads it by its class; the stamp of the packet, which a screen reader hears, carries the same words. At 02-01 the label says «paquete al puerto 3000», not «TCP 51514 → 3000»: the label waits for its terms (`tcp` and `source-port` arrive in 02-02), as `transportLabel` in `src/copy/packet-labels.ts` writes it. Measured on 2026-10-12 at 1280×800 and 1440×900: the still drawing put the tags of two packets that follow the same path (the two `nc` of 02-01 to `laptop-lucia`) at the same place, so «paquete al puerto 3000» and «paquete al puerto 5432» were drawn one over the other (172×19 px at the same x, y). Now `spreadTags` moves them apart as `spreadStamps` does the stamps, and `in 02-01 no piece label, floor name, stamp or packet label of the still map overlaps another` (one `describe` per window, `standStill`, no wait) checks the boxes of `packetBoxes` (`map-boxes.ts`: piece labels, floor names, stamps and the tags, which `mapBoxes` does not hold) with `pairsThatOverlap`; it fails on both windows without the shift. Measured on 2026-10-12 at 1280×800 and 1440×900, before `spreadTags` knew the floor names: the tag of a lone `nc` lay over the floor name «Piso 1» (1280×800: floor name x 343 to 374, y 435 to 474, tag x 313 to 485, y 436 to 455), and with two `nc` the left tag did. Now `spreadTags` keeps a tag off the floor names (`clearOfNames`, `src/scene/layout.ts`), so the same test checks the boxes of `packetBoxes`, which hold the floor names, after the first `nc` (one tag) and after the second (two), and that each tag stays inside the area of the map: with the floor names back in the check it fails on both windows after the first `nc` (`Piso 1 / paquete al puerto 3000`), and passes with the shift (tag x 384 to 556 with one `nc`; x 161 to 333 and 405 to 577 with two, 1280×800).
- **02-02 in `chapter-2.spec.ts`.** `02-02 is played with the mouse after 02-01 and earns 3 stars` starts with a star on 01-14 and on 02-01, opens the link «2. Abrir una conexión» from the roadmap and plays `playOpeningAConnection`. In «Sin animaciones», `in 02-02 the packet of an nc wears TCP and both ports on the map, and the second connection leaves from the next port` reads `.packet-tag` after `standStill` (the stamp first, as in 02-01): at 02-02 `tcp` and `source-port` are known, so the label of the first `nc` is «TCP 51514 → 3000» and, after the second, the two labels are «TCP 51514 → 3000» and «TCP 51515 → 5432» (the first connection of a device leaves from 51514 and each new one adds one, decided 6 of the plan). `in 02-02 no piece label, floor name, stamp or packet label of the still map overlaps another once the labels say TCP and both ports` (one per window, inside the same `describe`s as the 02-01 one) sends both `nc`, waits for the second stamp and the still scene, and checks the two labels and `expectClear`. Measured on 2026-10-12: the nine tests of the file passed 90 of 90 with `--repeat-each=10`.
- **02-03 in `chapter-2.spec.ts`.** `02-03 is played with the mouse after 02-02 and earns 3 stars` starts with a star on 01-14, 02-01 and 02-02, opens the link «3. Rechazo o silencio» and plays `playRefusedOrSilent` (`chapter-2-play.ts`): the `nc` to the app of `laptop-lucia`, which is refused; the card of `laptop-lucia` opened from its label, the tab «Servicios» and the chip «Todas las IPs (0.0.0.0)», after which no stamp is left on the map; and the four tests again (the 3306 of `laptop-lucia`, the 3000 of `laptop-marta`, the 3000 of `laptop-lucia` and, from her own terminal, `127.0.0.1`), with `sendNc` (address and message in the open terminal). In «Sin animaciones», `in 02-03 a refusal leaves the stamp Llegó and a silence leaves Bloqueado, and the four tests wear TCP and both ports on the map` reads the stamps (`Paquete: TCP 51514 → 3306 de tu-laptop a laptop-lucia. Llegó.` for the refusal and `Paquete: TCP 51515 → 3000 de tu-laptop a laptop-marta. Bloqueado.` for the silence: a refused packet reached the laptop and a dropped one did not) and the four labels of the packets after `standStill`.
- **Known gap, measured on 2026-10-12 and not fixed by task 25: at the natural end of 02-03 a stamp covers the label of a piece, with animations on or off.** There is no `in 02-03 no piece label, floor name, stamp or packet label of the still map overlaps another` test, as there is for 02-01 and 02-02: it would fail, a subset of the sends that passes would hide the defect, and `test.fail` would only silence it. Measured at 1280×800 after the four tests of the incident, in the still map (`reducedMotion`) and in the default mode with the packets at rest: the stamps of the three packets that end at `laptop-lucia` are spread 36 px apart by `spreadStamps` and the third (`TCP 51516 → 3000 de tu-laptop a laptop-lucia`, x 476 to 512, y 353 to 389) lies over the label of `laptop-marta` (x 501 to 620, y 372 to 393). `Traffic` draws the stamps in both modes with the positions of `spreadStamps`, which does not know the labels of the pieces, so a player sees it whatever the setting. The failing test with the four sent (1280×800 and 1440×900) listed that pair and a second one that exists only where the labels of the packets are drawn at rest: the label of the `nc` to `laptop-marta` (`TCP 51514 → 3000` when it is the only one sent, x 406 to 535, y 429 to 448) lies over its own stamp (x 499 to 535, y 415 to 451), because the path from `tu-laptop` to the firewall of `laptop-marta` crosses two columns, longer than any path of 02-01 and 02-02, so the middle of the path, where `spreadTags` puts the label, is not far enough from the end, where the stamp stands; `spreadTags` knows the floor names but not the stamps. Both are boxes measured at 1280×800 only; the failing run listed the same two pairs at 1440×900. A fix is a change of `src/scene/layout.ts` that reaches the maps of chapters 1 and 4 (the stamps are drawn in all of them), so it is a task of its own, and review focus 14 of the plan (task 34) asks for it in 02-09 and 02-10.
