# tests/e2e/chapter-1

The end-to-end test of chapter 1 (the fourteen incidents 01-01 to 01-14).

- `chapter-1.spec.ts` plays the whole chapter from 01-01 to 01-14, each incident opening the next with three stars, and ends on the roadmap with 04-01 still locked and the first incident of chapter 2 open. It is the only e2e test of the chapter. What the e2e tests of this chapter checked besides lives in Vitest: the geometry that needs no card (`src/ui/incident/map-sweep.test.ts`, Task M19 of the 2D plan), the behaviour of the screens (the roadmap and its locks, what a screen reader hears from a stamp, the gateway of 07, the tabs of the firewall of 13: `src/app.test.tsx`, `src/ui/roadmap.test.tsx` and `src/ui/incident/incident-screen.test.tsx`, in jsdom, Task M20), what an open card covers (`src/ui/incident/geometry/map-boxes.browser.test.tsx`, Task M21) and the same ping sent three times in 01-03, which is one stamp and three packets in the air (`src/ui/incident/geometry/sends-in-flight.browser.test.tsx`, Task M23). Task M23 deleted `the labels of the map stand where they belong from the first moment they show` and `in 06 and 08 the map is at full size: two laptops of a floor stand as far apart as at any window`: they guarded a camera that jumped and a map that shrank, and the 2D map has neither.
- `chapter-1-play.ts` says how each of the fourteen incidents is played with the mouse (`CHAPTER_1`) and holds `IP`, the addresses of the office, which `phone.spec.ts` and `chapter-2-play.ts` also use.
- The shared helpers (`cards`, `closing`, `console-errors`, `saved-progress`) stay in `tests/e2e/` and are imported through the alias `@e2e/` (`@e2e/cards`), never with `../`. Another spec imports the play file as `@e2e/chapter-1/chapter-1-play`; inside this folder the spec imports it as `./chapter-1-play`.
- Not here: a helper that more than one chapter uses (`tests/e2e/`), what a later chapter plays (its own folder).
