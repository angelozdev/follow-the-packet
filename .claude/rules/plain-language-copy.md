# Plain-language copy

User-facing text is short and plain: one idea per screen, panel or message; short sentences; the plain word first and the technical term after it; and no term the reader has not seen defined.

The reader is a student learning AWS networking without a networking background, reading in Spanish. Dense messages with stacked caveats make them stop reading.

**How to apply:**

- One idea per screen, panel or message. When a second idea appears, split it into its own step.
- Short sentences. Cut side notes the player does not need for the next action.
- Define every technical term the first time it appears, in a few words (for example "NAT: el cambio de la IP de origen al salir a internet").
- Prefer one concrete example or everyday analogy (the doorman with a list) over a list of caveats.
- Visible text lives in `src/copy/` or in the level files, as [[english-only]] says, so editing copy never means editing logic.
